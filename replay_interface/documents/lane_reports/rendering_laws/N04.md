## Timing and separation

Observed write timing is narrow but exact: each consumed CB1 payload is the
last saved write to that resource before the VS/PS slot-1 binds and draw; the
first consumer follows six or seven event records later. The corpus does not
prove construction time, per-frame recomputation, dirty-flag behavior, or the
CPU-side field feeding the write.

`Transp_OffsetX.x` is retained independently from `.y`; all observed `.y`
values in this pair are zero. Shader day/night inputs are separate CB0 data and
were not used to derive alpha. Final-pixel dimming is not an alpha oracle.
