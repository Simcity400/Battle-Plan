## Proven law

An archive must be expanded into a normalized, hash-inventoried directory before replay collision resolution. The replay layer preserves the recorded layer ordinal and every `replace_path`, rewrites only the descriptor content field, and then invokes the ordinary ordered-layer resolver. Storage kind is therefore erased at the replay boundary: later layers win case-folded relative-path collisions, while `replace_path` removes lower-layer files in the exact immediate-parent directory only and does not recurse into children.

Unsafe, ambiguous, duplicate/case-colliding, symlink, empty, invalid, missing, changed, or staged-drifted archives fail closed. Descriptor-backed DLC archives are supported by exact source/staged namespace equality. Archive mods require either an independently proven native-collision attestation or unconditional rejection before materialization.
