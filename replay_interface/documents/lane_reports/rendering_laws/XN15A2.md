## Closed producer laws

### Strategic-air and aircraft scenes

`0x14122cb70` is the airplane scenario catalog/analyzer. It resolves the
scenario entity, builds the exact fighter/bomber/defender/rocket actor catalog,
instantiates the scenario entity, enumerates animation attachment nodes, and
precomputes node transforms and durations. `0x141238530` creates the scenario
root, writes its world transform, creates the exact attacker/defender actor
lists, and stores their maximum duration. `0x14122e6d0` resolves an equipment
entity, creates the live aircraft entity, binds the authored animation state,
constructs its motion paths and appends the live actor object.

`0x14123a340` is the actor lifecycle machine: phase 1 is hidden pre-roll, phase
2 shows the entity, selects the authored scenario animation and advances live
animation/path state, and phase 3 hides it and runs post-roll until expiry.
`0x14123a670` updates the live actor and delayed-effect arrays, removes expired
actors, calls the actor destructor, and issues the direct
`missile_explosion_entity` request on qualifying aircraft/missile removals.
`0x14122e580` destroys every actor entity/path and the scenario entity.

Therefore exact replay requires stable scene/actor/entity ids, resolved entity
identity, actor side and slot, state-machine phase, state-entry sequence, live
animation/motion phase, transform, visibility, RNG state, and direct
create/hide/show/destroy operations. Actor count is the live producer list; it
is never a function of A4R1 sortie, damage, or loss quantities.

### Naval combat

`0x141651870` resolves the exact ship entity through ship/country/equipment
graphics with a diagnostic placeholder only after all real sources fail.
`0x1416542a0` replaces stale ship entities and creates/attaches the selected
entity. `0x14164f560` creates a combat ship visual slot and enumerates every
`hit_effect_locator` and `miss_effect_locator` node index on that live entity.

`0x1416539a0` selects the direct participants for defend/attack sides, creates
up to four live ship visuals, changes them to `damaged_defend` or
`damaged_attack` from direct combat state, and invokes direct death. Its helper
`0x1416541b0` compares previous and current entity animation phase against each
stored authored threshold. Only a threshold crossing calls `0x141652e50`.
That producer consumes its RNG counter/seed to choose an eligible live ship,
hit versus miss, a named locator, and a configured effect entity; it copies the
locator transform, applies the combat scale, creates the effect instance, and
queues an exact expiry time.

`0x141652c60` is the death producer: it enters ship state `death`, creates the
configured death/effect entity, sets its world transform, attaches it to the
ship's authored node, queues it for expiry, clears the live ship pointer and
sets lifecycle phase 3. Exact replay must record those direct transitions and
instances. Neither A4R1 nor SCH1 can stand in for them.

### Trade-route convoys

`0x1412c41e0` allocates a `CGfxTradeRouteConvoy`, resolves the country-specific
`*_traderoute_convoy` token with exact fallback to
`westerngfx_traderoute_convoy`, creates the entity, initializes visibility and
transform, and appends it to the route's live visual vector.
`0x1412c7ef0` computes the required visual count from the live route. The count
is clamped by the engine's visual maximum and becomes zero when the route is
not displayable; it is not the gameplay convoy quantity and is unrelated to
SCH1.

`0x1412c5ab0` rebuilds sampled route positions/segment lengths, uses the
producer counter/seed, creates the exact required object list, and writes per
object segment, progress, position, direction and active state.
`0x1412c80f0` is the wrapped-map placement evaluator.
`0x1412ca7d0` is the live update: it grows/shrinks the vector to the exact
count, advances segment progress, hides completed objects, schedules their
deterministic reactivation delay, recomputes transform and visibility, and
destroys surplus objects. `0x1412c4950` performs full teardown; route changes
flow through `0x1412caee0`.

The shipped convoy entity has `idle`, `move`, and `death` states. Its default
`idle` state has exactly two time-zero, `trigger_once=yes`,
`keep_particle=yes` events for front/back wake nodes. The authored `death`
state exists but contains zero particle events, and the trade-route producer
does not select it: it hides, reuses, or destroys visual objects. SCH1 has no
live convoy-visual id or lifecycle link, so it cannot authorize a sunk/explosion
visual.
