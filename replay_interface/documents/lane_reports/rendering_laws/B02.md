## Retained-VB arithmetic oracle

The read-only replay authority contains a diagnostic oracle in
`border_world_synthesis.rs` that constructs each water row as f32, runs two
radius-6 fixed-end passes, initializes each component accumulator with
`0.0f32`, adds window points one by one in the already-closed chain direction,
and performs one f32 division by the f32 window count. With captured direction
held fixed, the oracle reports `8,578/8,578` water strips exact and
`8,578/8,578` with zero centerline error; fixed forward and fixed reverse
addition orders give only `8,552` and `8,262` respectively.

This closes the retained 21-row arithmetic discriminator at the oracle level.
It does **not** yet prove allocation/insertion/duplicate suppression or bind the
instruction sequence to an exact-a729 producer function; those are outside the
narrow arithmetic claim unless the producer export joins them directly.

## Provisional exact formula

For each pass `p in {0,1}` and interior row `i`, with
`r = min(i, n-1-i, 6)` and source rows frozen at pass start:

```text
sx = +0.0f32
sz = +0.0f32
for j in chain_order(i-r .. i+r):
    sx = f32(sx + src[j].x)
    sz = f32(sz + src[j].z)
count = f32(2*r + 1)
dst[i].x = f32(sx / count)
dst[i].z = f32(sz / count)
```

Endpoints are fixed. Pass 1 reads the f32 output of pass 0. No f64
accumulation, reassociation, reciprocal multiply, fitted seed, or epsilon is
admitted.
