## Raids

Lifecycle arrow colors are preparing `(.7,.7,.7,1)`, ready `(.7,.7,0,.9)`, and active `(1,0,0,.9)`. Icon camera-distance limits are minimum `10`, normal maximum `500`, and raid-mode maximum `1600`.

Evaluate categories in shipped order: air, paratrooper, nuclear, land infiltration, naval commando. Air and paratrooper categories require Gotterdammerung. Nuclear has no category DLC gate but requires a completed nuclear-bomb special project or nukes greater than zero. Land infiltration is `(No Compromise, No Surrender and literally China) or Thunder at Our Gates`. Naval commando requires Thunder at Our Gates and original tag INS or AST. Each raid definition can add item-level gates, so replay must evaluate the scripted `visible` and `available` blocks in file order. A single BBA/MtG boolean is incorrect.
