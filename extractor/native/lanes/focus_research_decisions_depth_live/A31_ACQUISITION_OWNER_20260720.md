# A31 owner-runnable acquisition closeout

The missing A31 reachability seam is now implemented without scheduling d89.
The injected collector owns a concrete `A31FixtureAcquisitionOwner`; it installs
the exact-a729 hook graph, arms one country/hour transaction, seals the latest
complete focus refresh against an authenticated selected-Present QPC/order,
encodes the existing NFP1 grammar in evidence mode, syncs the record, and then
commits its authenticated receipt.

Arming also supplies the country object from the collector's authenticated
country roster. Every `0x141379A50` item callback must carry that exact context
pointer; a different or changing context poisons the whole epoch before the
pointer-free country tag can be admitted.

The hook roots are the exact vector publisher `0x14136F750`, dynamic refresh
`0x14137A780`, per-item country/state producer `0x141379A50`, final item-state
writer `0x1413778F0`, and highlight producer `0x141377430`. Every entry is
sentinel-gated. Partial create/enable rolls back;
uninstall disables and removes all targets and waits for callback quiescence
before clearing trampolines or process-local state.

The collector decoder reuses its SEH-guarded stable reads and the existing D12
definition-id decoder. It double-reads the owner header and ordered item pointer
array, authenticates the owner and both item vtables, joins exact transient
leaves, and invokes the lane's pointer-erasing materializer. The final display
percentage is accepted only from the proven `0x141BB636E` internal callsite;
the public function-entry observation is not treated as equivalent. Measured
joint-view absence is explicit and distinct from a missed helper leaf.

The evidence writer emits an identity-named, content-hashed `.nfp1` record followed by a JSON
receipt. `tools/verify_a31_acquisition_receipt.py` rehashes the receipt and
record, independently decodes the full NFP1 payload, checks every identity and
item invariant, proves the refresh preceded the selected Present, and requires
nonzero invocation counts for every hook family.

d89 remains reserved and unschedulable. The only remaining gate is the live
fixture already listed in H-A31: one complete stable focus-view frame and one
discriminating state/transient transition, both admitted by the root-owned
regrader after central validation.
