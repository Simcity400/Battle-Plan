# collector-governance

Governance capture: politics, political parties and party leaders, ideas, research and technologies, focus, decisions and timed effects, buildings, and the held fingerprint, building, party-leader and party stores that let quiet countries carry their rows, with the party and party-leader writer observers. The host owns dispatch, loans and table assembly.

Read the [shared rules](../../AGENTS.md) and the [collector guide](../collector/README.md).

Scripted map demand can retain the native country sharing roster in ResearchModifiers,
including idle countries. The existing research writer certificate and selected country
census carry its count witness and ordered IDs; no separate periodic census is added.

Run `cargo test -p collector-governance` and `cargo clippy -p collector-governance --all-targets -- -D warnings` for its synthetic checks.

The certified Research owner acquisition includes unused speed, ahead-of-time and cost-reduction grants, uses, claims, restrictions and the next grant ID in ResearchModifiers. Idle countries retain an explicit inventory baseline. It reuses the status and applied-grant authentication from research inputs and carries the inventory under the existing Research writer marks. Without that certificate, the legacy hourly fallback withholds inventory rows; only an explicitly forced full read acquires their baseline.
Politics retains the election interval from its existing copied span. Ordinary next dates derive during playback; only eligible countries with constructor/reset last dates demand the independent cached date under the existing structure certificate. Missing observation adds no cache read. These operands serve the existing politics view.

Research mismatch status preserves old/new counts for Inputs, AppliedBonuses and Modifiers, plus counts and identity/value difference masks per modifier wire kind (25?30 grants, 31 integrated sharing roster). It also keeps take availability, comparison certification and explicit/selection full-read flags. These diagnostics derive only from already selected rows and retained fixed-size fingerprints; no new native reads, hooks, hourly scans or per-owner hourly allocations.

Grant days-in-use is an internal research-switch refund operand, not a remaining or elapsed grant duration shown by the technology overview. The native daily update increments it only while applied; the bonus tooltip consumes remaining uses, speed/ahead values and restrictions, and the grant serializer omits this counter. Inventory therefore does not record or display it as a duration.
The existing decision census forwards its authenticated first-seen countries to the delayed-event baseline store. Certified owners do no additional queue reads on later census visits.
