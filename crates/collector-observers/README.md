# collector-observers

Raid's diagnostic site counts use the timer's owned thread slots. The origin
refresh count remains a separate atomic: route takes compare it around their
bitmap walk to reject a completed overlapping refresh. Running counts, marks,
epochs and that overlap proof retain their ordering.

Certified daily raid capacity stores call the original before timer entry; the
outer pass still brackets native execution and counts every unchanged store.
`untimed_certified_capacities` reports those stores on normal pass returns,
separately from hook timing, not as zero measured cost.

World-input admission counts its complete base hook group before companion hooks
are appended. Companion storage does not withdraw the shared design or fog
forwarders; a missing base group still refuses them.

The country-input observer has an independent design-modifier consumer for the

Existing membership/bonus hooks forward their group receiver, and the existing scientist-base before/after check forwards changed or unreadable bases. A faction consumer takes per-group certificates without consuming another consumer's marks or patching the same native site twice.

Between-tick native writer observers that several capture families share, with the forwarders that let one patch owner per native site notify the others: country inputs (and the weather carry diagnostics they report), rosters, characters, census, advisor pools, opinions, the output and event ledgers, nuclear strikes, wars, naval trees, unit leaders and their traits, retreat bans, raids, air base carry with the air registration counts, rail, territory, the event feed with its history, entry stub and native guard, and war provenance, political status, world inputs, wings, air losses and building bombing. The host installs the hooks, drains the stores at the hourly boundary and composes the capture families that read them.

The war domain forwards status, relation-slot and diplomacy lifetime receivers to the participation owner before and after native mutation. These fixed-size marks keep invalidation local to the affected owner or war while the domain token excludes acquisition.

The receipt gate follows the first refused publication covering the current loss,
including a decline applied after the seal released. An older healthy receipt
cannot clear that loss; a newer loss advances the required publication. Repeated
seals without a new loss do not add another receipt round trip, and the first
healthy recovery endpoint remains Partial.

A loss proven to touch one owner refuses only that owner: a pass retirement missing from an uncertified owner's queue, a decision result or reactivation reaching an uncertified owner's exact member (its occurrence is kept), or that owner's failed baseline. The owner's refusal marker names the reason and counts one rejection; other owners, the registries and the interval keep their certificates. An owner is refused once per registry until a certificate, a native reset of its state or an observation loss re-arms it; later refusals publish nothing and are counted in status. A certified owner missing a member is still a global refusal. Every native status reset ends its members' attribution, and an observed retirement removes an uncertified owner's member, so a reused address gets a fresh label. A recovery epoch, bumped when an owner certificate retires or an owner the next walk would baseline is refused, makes the next country walk visit owners; certified owners read nothing more, and a failed attempt is retried only after an observation loss.

Collector-side store entries (both boundary halves and the census) wait for a live holder instead of declining, and never count a missed observation; a retained owner refuses them. A waiting worker races for each release; once it has lost one it is owed the store, and callbacks decline until it enters, after at most one more hold. Native callbacks allocate nothing and, except for Candidate-to-Candidate admission below, never wait: a declined callback counts its missed observation once, in the decline phase it joined and validated before its entry attempt, without withdrawing the holder's arming. Declines are counted apart from the ownership word, so no release or worker entry waits for callbacks to stop. The seal flips the phase before its unconditional release, then drains the old one: it waits only for attempts that joined before the flip, which run a few atomics each, and their declines refuse the sealed row. The next entrant applies that loss to the store. Every other entry takes only the current phase's declines, and a census or registry sample certifies nothing while one is recorded in its interval. Every sample's writer cut precedes its first native read, so a crossed read of any of them is retried.
The worker lost-race regression gates retry boundaries with test-only handshakes,
forcing release sampling, the replacement holder and completed claim accounting
before checking free-but-owed and worker-held callback declines.

Candidate publication alone waits for another DecisionWriter Candidate, first
polling briefly, then parking with an address wait. That holder only authenticates
selected identities and copies guarded operands into prepared storage; it calls
no original, getter or forwarder and never waits on a game thread. The native
wrapper keeps its Flight and selected operands live until synchronous publication
returns. Candidate has no fallback occurrence path: every invocation keeps its
own fact, and genuinely concurrent invocations linearize in lease order. No
native read, fact or pointer is queued for later drain. A parked attempt leaves
the decline phase; retry moves the writer cut and joins the current phase before
trying again, so it cannot strand the seal or its old-phase drain. A seal that
wins before that retry precedes the still-running publication. A worker, any
other callback site, or an owed worker still causes the same counted decline.
Release wakes registered Candidate waiters only; uncontended callbacks add no
clock reads, diagnostic counters or wake calls. A bounded address-wait timeout
rechecks terminal retention even when an escaped holder never releases; it never
turns a live Candidate collision into a decline or clears retained ownership.

Worker leases allocate only for the boundary's registry baseline, which is small. The seal measures its output in one lease, grows it off the lease and seals in a second; a shortfall refuses the hour. The already selected recovery owner uses the same baseline readers in a private owned transaction off the lease. Its complete emission, token, scope and index demand includes surviving unacknowledged entries, earlier owners and local refusal control space; presize prepares that budget before a short, allocation-free merge rechecks the writer cut. A shortfall publishes no baseline prefix or Ready, refuses only that owner and is retried at the next visit. Owner and registry baselines are cut against a writer epoch that every callback's entry attempt and every bracketed native original moves. A sample a writer crosses, or that starts while one is in flight, certifies nothing whatever error it ends in: a registry retries at the next boundary, an owner is refused and retried at the next visit, and nothing waits for native code. Only a read error under an unbroken cut is a failed attempt. No writer publishes before its native store. Each writer whose store a tap or helper CALL used to precede is a detour of its enclosing unit: the duration adds' and the replaced-event retirement's CALLs, the automatic and scripted mission activations, timed selection, the targeted-ignore and ignore-all commands, the shared removal-request effect and candidate finalization, plus targeted selection and the set-political-power effect under the politics owner. Each unit runs its original once under a writer flight and publishes the committed values it reads after the original returns. Where its native ABI omits the selected instance, branch or operand, the old tap or helper CALL becomes a latch: it stages exactly what it saw, tagged with its frame's token, into the store's demand-sized witness arena, and never publishes. The arena feeds capacity_demand; presize checks both active and offered witness lengths independently of entry and script capacity before preparing the spare and never grows on a callback; exhaustion refuses by name and raises demand. Frame nesting lives on the native call stack, so only a per-thread sequence and depth are kept, with no cap. A latch enters the store the way any callback does, so a declined stage is a missed observation. A publisher takes and removes its frame's witnesses in native order after the original returns; witnesses a frame abandons on an unwind are dropped at the next boundary, which refuses that hour. Ignore and removal suppression follows the staged pre-store flags; set-power keeps its raw requested target and stages through a native-stack frame on the politics side; a fired retirement publishes only its staged match branch, never reading the freed node. A latch with no open frame, or of the wrong unit, refuses by name. A country reset, forwarded by the politics detour, is bracketed by an event-feed writer flight through the original's return. Status reports declined callbacks by declined and holder role and separately for an owed worker, worker waits, lost races and refusals, and crossed samples. Dispatch-instance callbacks and the seal visit only unpublished entries.
