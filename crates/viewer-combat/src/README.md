## Rules

- Bind `Values` by path through native resources, including bounded lazy portraits. Preserve membership order, finalized grid extents and scrollbar synchronization.
- *Match game* rasterises before display stretch. Keep active/reserve name truncation and font-measured three-dot ellipsis.
- Hovers use clipped geometry, reverse attach order and [ToolTip](../../viewer-foundation/src/tooltip_painter.rs); transparent text passes through. Shortcuts append native keycaps/delay. Ship groups anchor beside the window; sunk entries follow grid slots. Naval markers click only backgrounds/visible HQ frames; balance icons hover.
- Land marker counts include reserves; countries keep primary-list order. [Border tails](border_marker.rs) use recorded state/event operands and shared effect descriptions; missing/unsupported fragments refuse by name/count.
- Naval positioning, screening, carrier air and anti-air refresh from runtime operands rather than lagging combat copies. Index joins once per refresh; missing/duplicate keys and incompatible fixed modifier definitions leave values unknown.
- Commander roles use recorded category/skill; terrain hovers use content adjusters/widths. Each land side's hover inputs (LandCombatDetails `side_inputs`) hold the selected commander's timed traits, description token, skill progress, offence and defence and the learning-trait rows its portrait shows, decided at capture with the live display floor; progress ratios are stored at the printed whole percent. The skill hover prints the progress (or the at-cap arm) for the viewing country's commander and the offence and defence lines for every commander. Timed-trait gaps refuse only affected hovers. The offence and defence lines are their own part: without their operands, or their recorded modifier definitions, they alone refuse by name and the skill line stands. The next skill level, the leader block and the live learning floor are optional reads at capture: one that failed refuses only the part it feeds (the viewing country's skill line, the offence and defence lines, the viewing country's portrait). A recording without the side inputs refuses the viewing country's skill line, the offence and defence lines, the portrait's delayed description, the viewing country's commander portrait and the land trait hovers by name. Division commander icons join the hour's UnitLeaders and UnitLeaderTraits rows. Exile flag/frame withhold together on missing inputs. Naval trait context differs from land; only naval shows field-marshal inactivity.
- Hovers whose per-operand breakdowns or operands are not recorded refuse by name, counted; window values stay. Reserve hovers keep the native not-fighting and too-much-width rejections; the chance arm sums the recorded initiative, context keys and the reserve's recorded speed share (composed at capture), prints the country's 0xFB sources after the base line and, when the contextual leader's 0xFB is nonzero, ends with the commander's recorded name and sources, both from the hour's ReserveModifierTrees, their labels and the name shown or recovered like the naval source labels below; a fragment whose tree is missing, was refused at capture or whose label cannot show is omitted by name. A recording without the share refuses the chance arm. The CAS hover derives the anti-air effectiveness from the opposing side's recorded anti-air cache and shows its prevented damage, recorded at the whole units `|R0` prints, from the side inputs, or in formats 101 and earlier from the side details that held both; a recording with neither refuses it. Naval positioning hovers refuse (per-source breakdown), as do ship groups with an escaping member (escape chance), the screen box when it reaches the 0x1B3 country correction, and a linked carrier's sortie prefix below full efficiency.
- Naval positioning, the screen box's 0x1B3 country correction, an escaping member's escape chance and a linked carrier's sortie prefix below full efficiency print their per-source lines from the hour's [source trees](naval_trees.rs) (NavalModifierTrees), joined by recorded identities: a side's country, region weather, terrain and commander, a live ship's recorded sources in order. Their labels are stored text: shown as stored in the recording's language, and in another through the keys whose recording-language text renders exactly to them ([naval_labels.rs](naval_labels.rs), the recording language's localisation from the recorded content stack, indexed when the document loads; a leading inline icon is tried both as part of the authored text and kept before a recovered name); a label no key renders to, or whose readings differ in the display language, refuses by name and is counted. A recording without the trees refuses those hovers by name.
- Retained naval results classify recorded ships and aircraft using native country equivalence, representatives and survivor priorities. Stored result text keeps the recording-language guard; both panels open locally and scroll through retained text bars.
- Scripted tactic text follows the viewer's NATO choice, including nested unit icons; toggling refreshes land bindings without changing a disengaged naval window's supplying hour.
- Unseen icons prefer established view/index frames, else land's authored/naval binding initial frame with a counted history reason. Fallback never establishes history.
