# viewer-counter-products

Unit, task-force and province hover text, fresh counter route and arrival queries, selected movement routes and battle-plan products. The host supplies a fresh immutable content identity containing concrete recorded-stack facts and composes the products with counter grids, raids and world submission.

Shared drawn-control observations, authored GUI fallback, stack anchors, counter input/action/status folds, movement reads and timing come from viewer-foundation. This crate owns hover caches and query demand gates; counter grid lifecycle and mutable selection belong to viewer-counters. It imports neither the host nor another viewer feature. Content facts resolve through installed-content; queries retain occurrence order and counted refusals.

A drawn trade-route hit takes priority over province hover; its transfer tooltip is refused by name by the host. The province hover follows the map mode ([mode_lines.rs](src/map/province_tooltips/mode_lines.rs)): the location block, units and selection only in the default and states modes (states adds the state's live category, States `category`); manpower, infrastructure (`MAPMODE_TOOLTIP_INFRASTRUCTURE` with the state's level, zero for a province outside every state), diplomacy, ideology, resistance and compliance, and scripted modes show their own lines alone; factions, players, air, navy and raids show nothing of their own (the game's raid lines belong to its raid planning, which a replay does not have). The resources mode shows what the state produces through its controller's resource origin ([resources.rs](src/map/province_tooltips/resources.rs)), resource by resource and through the origin its rights choose: delivered against nominal amounts at the origin's efficiency, building grants, the delivery route and convoys, and infrastructure damage, with the same arithmetic as the resource icons ([resource origins](../viewer-foundation/src/resource_origins.rs)); the explanation of `local_resources_factor` lists each of its non-zero sources, even when they cancel, and that of temporary resources each modifier source; recordings hold only the effective totals, so the first is refused by name in every resource's block and the second wherever the state holds temporary resources. The terrain mode's hover is viewer-combat's ([terrain_mode.rs](../viewer-combat/src/terrain_mode.rs)), which the app asks instead. The operatives mode shows the viewing country's network section; a scripted mode's `<mode>_tooltip` and `_tooltip_delayed` expand through the shared scripted text in the game's scopes (ROOT, THIS and PREV the viewing country, FROM the hovered province's state, whose FROM is the viewing country), and a scope function it does not support is refused by name; the state resistance breakdown is refused.

Hover caches retain content and frame identities. Reached route queries build their whole-frame indexes lazily within each hover or diagnostics batch.

Tests live with their modules. `slow-tests` preserves existing gates; `test-support` enables shared synthetic foundation inputs for consumers. Follow the workspace [working rules](../../AGENTS.md).
