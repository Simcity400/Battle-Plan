# collector-production

Production and equipment-variant capture, the authenticated variant registry, owned raw copies and replay projection. The host owns scheduling and loans; this library owns the census, native reads, conversions and synthetic scale fixtures.

Saved designs share one daily census, including designs absent from equipment
pools and production. Twenty private accepted writer/lifetime boundaries share
MIO refresh, clone and consuming move scopes with world-input. Three independent
proofs cover saved membership/writes, applied-source/provider lifetime and current
author-reference liveness. Complete dependency baselines and stable revisions
admit carry; authentication alone does not. Ordered occurrences preserve repeated
accepted edits. Destruction retires prepared hash bindings before native storage
dies and writes an identity tombstone. Scalar, name, icon and niche writers copy only their changed fields; workers
apply compact edits to the preceding state without revisiting dependencies.
Bulk permission writes keep compact flag bytes. Source/provider changes copy one demanded source or owner roster, then
workers replace only affected immutable references. Game-thread callbacks never
shift sorted populations or allocate. World/content invalidation retires live
bindings by epoch and reseeds on the existing worker.

The daily census reuses shared deep design values only under that complete
certificate; its batches request deep native spans only for missing values.
A local refused edit interval retains independent dirty bits on saved designs,
source/provider bindings and demanded authors. Its recovery census still reads
and authenticates all membership, prefixes, modules, stats and upgrades, but only
marked, new or uncertified designs reacquire deep state. A refused occurrence log
withdraws dependent availability but keeps shared writer observation live, so
recovery can still reuse untouched values. Closing shared forwarding revokes all
deep reuse until a fresh complete census. Lost receivers, lifetime or content
epochs, failed recovery and conflicting writers also revoke reuse;
explicit attach/refresh reads remain fully fresh. Recovery rechecks its proof
after acquisition and falls back to the full reader if it changed. Observer status
retains separate running/missed-writer refusal counts, the last missed source
location and the last refusal even after recovery/detach. Competing edits to
receivers unwatched both before and after their originals do not refuse the saved
interval; saved receivers, publication and unknown receivers still do.
Receiver-scoped claims let different threads edit different saved designs
concurrently; overlapping writers of one receiver, including its consuming moves
and its version owner, still refuse. No claim lock is held across an original.
Status counts competing-writer, lock-contention, unknown-receiver and
claim-capacity refusals separately.
Daily projection compares borrowed headers, text and deep values before cloning
changed rows. Unchanged source bindings and held design entries keep their
allocations; country-demand rebuilding recycles its key scratch. Owned definition rows sort only after acquisition, authentication
and coupled module/context visits finish. Reference save admission is
independently checked for nonnull MIO handles.
Applied direct and named source values share an immutable snapshot dictionary;
designs retain source order and duplicates without repeating source payloads.

Country design modifier demand follows the saved designs' creators, archetypes
and modules, plus the first upgrade definition's authored cost currency. The daily
census revises demand even while design events are refused. Immutable currency
metadata reuses authenticated upgrade visits and never becomes a replay pointer.
Independent marks from the existing country accumulator observer
select changed owners; unavailable writer coverage refuses carry. Immutable
registered keys are acquired once from the existing authenticated type/module
visits. Quiet hours publish the same shared rows without scanning owners. Recorded
country scopes retain raw creator references; the demanded numeric refresh
reuses its country resolution to bind aliases for owner notifications.

Run `cargo test -p collector-production` and `cargo clippy -p collector-production --all-targets -- -D warnings` for its synthetic checks.

Frozen design authorship, trait-count markers and bonus entries belong to the existing daily variant-stat acquisition. Native author/bonus/copy/load writers certify quiet payloads; hourly definitions do not acquire these fields.

Consumable design discounts have a separate production-owner certificate. The
existing design demand selects country rosters; append, load, consume/prune and
reset writers mark only their owner. Workers share country authentication with
numeric operands and read changed demanded rosters. Callbacks copy no records;
quiet hours share the previous rows. MIO design refresh and consuming move
notifications arrive through world-input's forwarder, which owns their sole
patches. Both patch families must be enabled before design carry is admitted.

Observer design reveal joins a singleton game fog row. Existing design workers
read that byte only at first demand or after its complete writer token changes;
quiet hours share the same row. The building observer forwards nudge entry to
world-input, which owns the other fog lifecycle and console writers.

Organisation-change factors demand the selected organisation's owning country, independently of the design creator or holding country. The existing native design visit passes its sample-local organisation resolution to owner authentication; no second registry walk occurs. Reference-counted numeric demand shares one country/key value, and foreign organisation owners do not acquire consumable-template rosters. Existing accumulator notifications select changed demanded owners.

Industrial comparisons share that country accumulator certificate. A saved
design demands its creator's conversion-speed factor, or naval refit IC factor,
using the classifier already in hand. Owner/key demand is shared across designs;
switching families retires the unused key. No pair results or extra native
designer evaluation are recorded, including for unused designs.

Dependent unit observers register a process-static
`production::design_changes::Forwarder` with `forwarder(Some(&RECEIVER))`,
and remove it with `forwarder(None)`. `enabled: fn(Availability)` delivers a live epoch capability only after
every dependency baseline and shared dispatch exist and prepublication writer
flights have drained. Publication first opens the new dispatch epoch, then waits
on the existing design/template and world-input design-domain flight census.
Notifications may arrive while the publication fence is still closed.
That census retains rejected originals through callback exit; writers entering
after its zero check see the prepared dispatch and notify. No additional callback
counter is needed. Retain its stamp with the
dependent baseline; validate `Availability::is_current()` (or
`availability_is_current(stamp)`) before/after reads and on every carry/publication,
in addition to the consumer's own writer revision. A notification must never become
an unchecked cached boolean or replace an older baseline's stamp. Reset immediately withdraws
availability without waiting or taking the registration mutex; stale worker
publication cannot restore it. `call: unsafe fn(Writer, usize,
Phase)` surrounds RebuildStats, RefreshDerivedState, MoveAssign, Update, Publish
and Destroy. Move reports both original variant operands independently, before
the consuming original and after it. World-input also supplies both pre-call
identities. SourceInvalidation names the direct source, IdeaInvalidation the
postprocessed idea, ProviderInvalidation its production status, CountryInvalidation
the owning country, and ContentInvalidation has receiver zero. Before/After
surround the unchanged original; Unwound replaces After on unwind. Consumers
must not read a destroyed receiver after its Before phase or a moved-from
receiver after the move. Registration/removal is safe with live hooks and waits
only on the control thread for complete writer flights and terminal notifications. A withdrawn-capability
notification may run on the resetting callback and must not block; active
invocations still receive their terminal phase. Disabled forwarding
performs one null atomic check and no native read, allocation or payload work.

Author liveness is published once per demanded raw handle in
DesignOrganisationLiveness, independently of a frozen design snapshot's lookup
witness. Reconciliation/runtime creation, identity repair and base destruction
supply the narrow certificate; source reset also covers production destruction.
The existing equipment and technology reload owners forward content invalidation.

Air-design creator demands include the single wing XP training operand through the existing country accumulator certificate. It shares owner/key storage and dirty-owner scheduling with designer costs.

Country-context admission is independent of saved-design carry. A failed immutable
cost-key or context-demand seed withdraws both context snapshots and retries only
on the next existing daily visit. That retry rebuilds reached country demand from
the owned census without forcing deep design/source reads. Carried MIO authors
resolve only their reached reference for the context owner. Successful quiet
context visits share the previous snapshots without native reads.

Attach and post-gap keyframes reacquire the saved roster and its dependencies.
A fresh full census admitted by all three certificates starts an empty design
change interval at that boundary, replacing the earlier unprepared drain verdict.
It supplies the definitions, modules, stats, upgrades, source dictionary, fog and
author-liveness snapshots together. Failed admission remains a named refusal.
Liveness verdicts are dated and admitted on acquisition; later decoding uses
that observation rather than the live observer's current availability. Failure
marks retain owned payload storage, and skipped daily reads preserve its date.
