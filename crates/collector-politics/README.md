# collector-politics

Political event capture: the observer of native political writers (elections, ruling parties, ideas, diplomatic dispatch and civil wars, forwarded to the country input, event feed and war provenance observers in collector-observers) and the pack that converts its drained events into the hour's tables. The host installs the hooks, drains at the hourly boundary and assembles the tables.

Read the [shared rules](../../AGENTS.md) and the [collector guide](../collector/README.md).

Run `cargo test -p collector-politics` and `cargo clippy -p collector-politics --all-targets -- -D warnings` for its synthetic checks.

Decision execution and targeted timeout scopes forward their actual selection/completion/deadline edge to the shared event-feed owner independently of power costs. Free decisions therefore retain their execution occurrence.

The politics family owns the targeted selection function and its final duration/default store tap. It forwards actual final status/duration operands to the event-feed reducer and publishes both sites together.

Exact political before/after balances forward with the authenticated selected owner to the event-feed certificate. Observation loss and disabling the forwarder withdraw that scalar certificate. Only final roster boundaries serialize it; no duplicate power stream is recorded.
