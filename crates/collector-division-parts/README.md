# collector-division-parts

Army writer diagnostic call counts reuse the timer's owned thread slots;
writer certificates, dirty marks and occurrence-sensitive reads are unchanged.

Per-division input parts that division presentation (and railway guns and naval counters) read: attack stats with the army stat writer observer that lets a quiet stat block carry its operands, dig-in factors, DivisionLandInputs, supply inputs and unit combat references. The host owns the presentation jobs, loans and table assembly.

Read the [shared rules](../../AGENTS.md) and the [collector guide](../collector/README.md).

Run `cargo test -p collector-division-parts` and `cargo clippy -p collector-division-parts --all-targets -- -D warnings` for its synthetic checks.

Stored division experience has an independent writer certificate within the army observer. Owner decision 2026-10-21 samples recorded XP and officer XP at daily slot 12, or immediately for a new full-handle/pointer occurrence, with fresh full/load/recovery reads. Existing scalar stores reuse census order and storage; daily carry keeps the original certificate and never lends stale XP to the hourly exercise predicate. Certificate and read refusals are named and counted.

Division careers and awarded medals are read only for new or writer-dirty deployed divisions. The common census supplies authenticated identities; history and medal-store certificates include insertion, award application, officer promotion, inheritance changes, lifetime and the existing forwarded medal-modifier rebuild. Quiet snapshots share a persistent row root with no history scan or native read. Appends acquire only new suffixes; award targets and bulk list writers name the entries or list to refresh. Changed paths retain unrelated entry allocations and cached byte counts, publishing changed key ranges. Complete row vectors materialise on the spool thread for recovery and keyframes. Census order is retained between passes. Refused roots retain named summary rows. Stored entry order, repetitions, names, dates, province lists, medal identity/counts, inheritance, multipliers, orders and embedded sunk-ship records are retained.

Division experience certification also requires the existing equipment-destruction combat-damage patch. Its notification covers every original call, including zero-damage XP clamping, independently of the equipment ledger's admission or failure. Template binding marks XP because it can rescale it immediately.

Pending template conversions use an independent certificate on template binding, conversion completion and construction; ordinary XP awards do not reread that reference.

The deployed panel cache is acquired only beside a new or active-stat-dirty block. Each new scalar has an independent numeric replay column; the panel reuses movement_stats.air_defense for anti-air without storing it twice. Active officer XP and the retained history-panel counter use independent persistent caches: officer add/set hooks and the already forwarded damage writer respectively. Officer XP accepts daily sampling; the counter remains writer-current. Quiet values carry without a native read.

DivisionStatus's supply footer shares the daily policy and authenticated occurrence cache. Complete owned footer samples preserve independent scalar/origin refusals and ordered duplicates. Carried samples share their allocation, omit the whole native read/traversal, and settle on FIFO decode without native access. Daily/full preflight revokes the three samples; incomplete captures and abandoned loans cannot establish a carry baseline. Supply ratio, exact ratio and grace stay hourly.

The existing live division-name sizing census prepares the army, officer, career and history watch domains before the first boundary. Name-payload refusals do not discard its completed division count.

Hook construction failures name the owning family and site. Atomic publication failures retain their underlying diagnostic and the full participating site list.

Demanded deployed source roots share country and selected commander identities under naval tree certificates. Pool membership has an independent writer certificate and reusable acquisition scratch; shared variant terrain and state/country operands refresh only after their writer tokens move. The host registers the variant receiver with the design family's forwarder. A missing owner certificate publishes an individual refusal; it never enables polling. Quiet membership and source rows retain storage.

The completed design forwarder covers rebuild, derived refresh, update, publication, destruction and paired move boundaries. Source, idea, provider, country and content invalidations revoke the shared terrain domain. Registered destroy/move bindings invalidate only affected demanded pools on the worker; current membership is authenticated before following terrain pointers. All three design baselines gate availability, and unavailable intervals retain named runtime refusals without polling.

Variant baselines retain the design owner's live availability stamp. Acquisition, carry and snapshot projection validate it; a reset between acquisition and publication withdraws terrain operands by name while preserving independent trees, membership and state terms. Refusal snapshots are prepared lazily by editing only the variant range and reused across repeated publication. A renewed capability requires a new demanded baseline.

Failed or expired terrain reads roll back the affected variant's complete ordinal range, including entries emitted during that attempt. Publication checks the captured row tree for dependent terrain rows independently of acquisition counters. Existing refusal rows keep their allocations when publication withdraws other terrain owners.

Presentation retains each division's context and its last published context refusal beside the held membership. The hourly context selection and native reads still run; equal results skip certified-row lookups and empty removals. Shared source roots take their watch sequence only on their first visit in a pass, as their acquisition already does. Repeated source, membership, state and terrain refusals retain equal summary allocations while invalidated suffix rows are removed, with unchanged refusal accounting. Empty membership storage is reused across dirty refusals and recovery.

Career and stored-experience captures share one experience writer certificate per division; career still requires its independent history certificate.

Presentation preparation ranges acquire context, dirty source trees, pool
membership, state/terrain values and history scalars with reused local memos.
They also select daily supply samples from the immutable occurrence baseline;
missing staging keeps the host's original ordered lookup.
Already admitted receivers take their quiet certificate before resume;
held source, membership, state and history slot hints avoid repeat probing.
A passive gate rejects cold or cleared slots before registration, so parallel
ranges cannot change limited-table admission winners or refusal counts. Cold,
retired, unavailable or incompletely staged source passes retain the original
ordered native path; failed registration cannot create a carry certificate.

Complete warm source passes freeze world, availability, lifetime and dirty-mark
boundaries before the ranges. FIFO decode settles the same shared country,
leader, feature/HQ and province decisions in census order, including first
failures, then updates maps, certified rows and SourceSnapshot without a native
read or later mark drain. The certified tree remains incremental: unchanged
roots and quiet snapshots retain their allocations. Membership observations use
census ordinals rather than another per-division certificate map.

A captured selected-province refusal is division-local. When its effective
context refusal is already retained, it can settle on owned decode too: it
neither inserts a shared province result nor reaches state operands. New refusal
rows keep their allocation barrier before certification. Shared country,
commander and context failures still retain the ordered fallback, as do
cold/full registrations and optional staging shortage.

XP, maxima and stat-registration decisions needed by the final native ranges
remain before resume. Other complete scalar observations settle afterward with
their captured certificates. Native history traversal and entry registration
remain parked; its ordered row/binding edit journal, pruning and snapshot finish
run on decode. Fresh attack reads remain planned after certification and acquired
in final-status ranges; owned attack publication keeps the captured verification
slice, carry, witnesses and held-store settlement. Source and certification
timers read the clock per phase, not per division. Range and journal storage is
reused across hours.

Preparation uses each warm receiver certificate once; querying a registered
receiver again does not strengthen its proof. Scalar carries reuse one baseline
lookup for their verdict and owned value, including reordered censuses. Source
preparation reuses each held pool/root/state lookup, and a held commander identity
is not reinserted for every division. Native demand, first failures, cold/full
admission, sampled verification and FIFO owned settlement are unchanged.

Spool projection borrows one resolved attack row for its scalar and terrain
fields, and checks witness presence without cloning its terrain payload.
Retained panel-source root rows lazily cache nested allocation totals; every
mutable borrow invalidates the cache, including partial reads and growth.

DivisionLandInputs compares each complete owned row and its ordered manpower
contributors with the encoder baseline before copying nested payloads. With
matching membership, equal rows retain replay ownership and only changed rows
are converted. Captured-span checks, duplicate refusals and complete
recovery/keyframes are unchanged.
