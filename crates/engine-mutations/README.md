# engine-mutations

Faction companion descriptors include autonomous-account structure/reset/selection/history writers and bounded scalar sites for initiative, influence/contribution operands, projection, normalization and rank. Projection totals/effects and extracted-pool final stores supply independent scalar mirrors. Shared resource primitives are filtered against registered embedded faction/system pools without a native callback read. Unsupported spans or incomplete writer domains refuse their tables.

Native mutations readers and their operands, layouts and synthetic tests.

`war_participation_sites` describes stored directional contribution writers, whole-ledger replacement and the four gated delivery-history adders. Temporary UI breakdown aggregation is separate from the stored participation ledger.

The public capture API is available through [engine](../engine/README.md). Shared rules live in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); field contracts, arithmetic, native gates and failure order stay beside each reader.

Faction goal sites distinguish sparse status mutations, one-goal progress and
transition calls, actual pending-state stores and the owning cancellation-history
append. They supply writer-driven demand without an hourly goal-list scan.

Sparse faction companion sites identify rule selection/removal/template reset, programme reference lifecycle and manpower transfer/refund/contribution/clear. The two access-unlock effects supply taps at the actual resolved-faction byte stores. Their independent domains omit read-only eligibility checks and programme updates that award contribution without changing references.

Trade-route writer descriptors cover the state-to-state and supply port-to-port
updates, route and country-resources construction, save loading and origin
refresh's inline kind clear. Origin refresh exposes its kind separately so the
observer can skip unchanged refreshes while nested route updates mark repaths.
The origin receiver reads its client country as the census owner; a rights-held
origin's route sender can name the grantor instead of that owner.

The module list is in [lib.rs](src/lib.rs). Production dependencies are [engine-deployment](../engine-deployment/README.md), [engine-focus](../engine-focus/README.md), [engine-native](../engine-native/README.md), [engine-projects](../engine-projects/README.md), [engine-railways](../engine-railways/README.md), [engine-research](../engine-research/README.md), [engine-resources](../engine-resources/README.md), [engine-stability](../engine-stability/README.md). Tests keep their synthetic fixtures and existing ignored scale gates.

Country-input mutation descriptors include recruitment setters, exile resources, acquired crews and sent-expeditionary membership. The embedded manpower receiver names its country through its recorded owner link; crew sites notify their object certificate without marking country tables.
World-input fog descriptors cover the console toggle and game/frontend/nudge
lifecycle. Nudge construction remains in the building descriptor catalogue,
whose observer forwards that writer. All integer and stack arguments are
retained by the collector callbacks.

Army experience writers form an independent observation domain from active-stat and name rebuilds. Unit-leader progress writers cover residual experience additions, daily progression and trait-training insertion, removal, pruning and completion.

Army writer certificates also cover division experience, career/award list edits and officer promotion. The promotion callback marks the history argument rather than the commander receiver. Award application forwards both stack arguments as well as the register arguments.

Officer XP addition and replacement notify the embedded officer receiver. The shared damage hook also certifies the independently retained history-panel counter.

Division equipment-pool descriptors cover membership primitives. Variant rebuilding and assignment remain exclusively owned by the design family, which forwards those writers to the deployed operand receiver.
`doctrine_mutations` describes the doctrine selection, progression, grant,
source-rebuild, reward and load writers and effective-daily-gain store sites.
Their receivers and stored operands support capture on change without a unit
census or periodic doctrine scan.
Country-input mutation descriptors include recruitment setters, exile resources, acquired crews and sent-expeditionary membership. The embedded manpower receiver names its country through its recorded owner link; crew sites notify their object certificate without marking country tables.
Template notifications cover retained data writers, owner-scoped lock/cap effects, deployment selection/reset, and occupation selection and membership. Native callbacks preserve all arguments, including the occupation insertion fifth argument. Missing or overlapping observation withdraws writer certificates.


Headquarters notifications cover target assignment, deployment creation/cancellation and holder lifecycle, requisition, requirement rebuild, equipment distribution/refund/transfer and manpower delivery. The whole orders-group hourly update is not hooked.

The country garrison-priority command names its owner. HQ rebuild callbacks prove source/deployment inputs unchanged before suppressing invalidation, while their running guard still excludes acquisition. No-op requisition attempts leave the pool certificates intact.

Air wing map flag writers share the wing equipment observer through independent inline-field receiver keys; destruction uses its existing patch.

The ship refit owner also forwards the independent carrier Air-capacity certificate; it installs no additional patch. Module-predicate cache engagement preserves the semantic result.

Air-grid raid assignment uses the wing-reference setter and raid deleting destructor in the shared wing observer. These are ordinary entry hooks, each registered once in the common hook audit.

Direct incoming Air-wing append and swap-remove share the wing observer through a disjoint base-inline certificate key. The relocation progress operands retain their separate hourly writers.

The Air-base carry family owns pool-definition rebind alongside membership writers; hourly state-container fuel scalars require no writer descriptor.
