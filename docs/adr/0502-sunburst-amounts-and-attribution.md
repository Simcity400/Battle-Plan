# Keep sunburst amounts, attribution and ordering independent

An independent total can stay known when a part cannot be attributed. Keep exact integer or rational amounts for labels. A missing total clears the slices, and a known total with unresolved attribution can leave blank space. A signed correction stays visible as an exact row without contributing positive area or a share denominator. Reconstruct a rounded-away remainder only from exact, finite numeric operands. Incomplete totals can omit percentages while still showing measured amounts and their qualifiers. Recording-backed measures distinguish missing readings from parts a measurement excludes.

Incomplete attribution propagates from hidden rings to their shown ancestors, including the whole. Uncertainty marked local applies only to the slice for that level. A slice can stay in the legend when some of its parts are excluded and its measured amount is zero. Explicit recorded zeros remain listed when requested. Counts deduplicate IDs across levels because the same project can have repaired and built, with its kind hidden. Add separately aggregated contributions only where their identity sets are disjoint.

Order siblings by whole-period magnitude or a separately supplied stable weight, then by data key. This keeps colour and order fixed while the playhead grows the slices or a correction lowers their final amount. Work which has not yet appeared can participate in ordering and the Fill denominator without adding a current slice. Fill uses the complete denominator when available and falls back to Share where the final total is unknown. The palette assigns drawable slices only. A categorical level groups overflow into Other, while entity levels with recorded colours can show every category. Other preserves its children's identity, folds again only where revealed, and remains last. A removed zoom target falls back to the nearest surviving ancestor.

Confirm an entire roster and its candidate data before showing a world share. The remainder owns everything outside the selected leaders, including unassigned amounts. For a dated entity, use the recorded flag decision, which can establish that art is missing, and keep native-country identity even when its flag is unavailable.
