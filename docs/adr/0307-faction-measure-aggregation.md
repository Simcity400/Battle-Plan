# Sum faction measures over recorded membership

The followed roster panel carries structure, and the UI sums its followed member values when painting. Resolve member slots under the identity rule at the requested hour, and preserve whether each recorded roster is incomplete. An unknown roster or member value makes the affected faction total unknown.

For history, the default membership policy sums each country only during its recorded membership. The end and ever policies use the membership at the final hour or any membership during the window, respectively, and sum those countries across the full window. A custom group contributes each listed country over its clipped spans, or the whole window if the member has no spans. A bin that intersects missing evidence cannot supply a total. Missing membership matters across each policy's selection period.

The recorder's own per-hour faction rows give exact bin extrema for the default membership policy. Other policies read each member's chart once. Their member extrema sum to bounds for a multi-hour bin, since the members' peaks or lows may occur at different times.
