# 0401. Witness building health changes independently of request and attribution

The damage ledger observes the applied writer and uses its incoming return-address witness for known direct-call causes. The observer's own call does not establish that cause. Unknown direct callers stay unknown, and the observed direct calls cannot disprove indirect or bypassing writers. Scorch and nuclear paths can change health outside this ledger. The ordinary hourly writer follows joined air workers, and daily repair follows hourly work. Debug, initialization, exception and other unobserved setter paths need independent evidence.

The `APPLY_DAMAGE_RVA` entry guard covers an instruction that saves a nonvolatile register in the caller's home space. Relocation must preserve that write without changing the stack pointer or the nonvolatile register values.

Snapshot the building and its raw health before the call. Record the signed difference of the healthy-level and partial-health values after the call without substituting request size or returned budget consumption. A no-op does not follow any attribution pointer. A negative modified request can yield health beyond a nominal full level. The location and content mapping must authenticate independently of attacker attribution. Only an air source can name a wing. A positive wing-country override wins over the pool owner, and victim credit follows the post-damage controller independently of repair routing. Zero or unavailable attacker evidence does not create a country link. A location or health failure withholds the event, while an attribution failure retains the absence separately.

The cold building census counts live headers after authenticating the locations, statuses and backlinks. A missing root or failed read leaves sizing unavailable. This counts the population rather than bounding how many writes an hour can produce. Queue overflow remains a separate observation. A process address is valid only inside the synchronous call, never a recorded identity.
