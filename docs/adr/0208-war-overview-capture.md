# War overview reuses writer-certified owner rows

The WarOverview job captures owned war relations, goal snapshots, countries, names, participation, captured provinces and same-side delivery history. The collector host dispatches and assembles it. Native occurrence order stays explicit in the owned keys. Acquired rows leave the worker without live pointers. Reuse the most recent complete war discovery only while the war-domain token and each owner's goal sequence still match the values taken around its walk. The goal consumer identifies the actor, which is an endpoint and owner of the war. Goal construction, reading and destruction also identify their actor. An owner inventory with lost observation requires the explicit full refresh path.

Participation is seeded from the current authenticated census. The native writer truncates signed casualty counts to thousands before applying its factors, so amounts within the same quotient leave scores unchanged. Callbacks use the writer's existing arguments and select fixed-size refresh work. Native root and province membership work belongs to the acquisition worker. Callbacks neither allocate nor wait. A first-credit mark can require identity operands, but ordinary marks for seeded receivers use the existing owner. Constructing status receives an owner before its native backlink exists. A readable owner outside every recorded roster has no work. Unreadable identity that is required for an admitted owner loses observation until a full refresh.

Delivery history reconciliation walks the census once on a topology change. The history of an existing owner survives roster edits, and a newly admitted root gets its own seed. A changed or recycled status address cannot carry the previous owner's queued marks into the new history or erase another owner's row. A seed supersedes previously queued marks for that owner, and shared allocations retire on the acquisition worker. A quiet hour shares its existing table handles. On a province add, only a demanded ledger needs a province identity, even though the native writer processes repeated captures.

The tests use virtual authenticated roots to make first-credit and history read counts observable independently of roster size. They exercise status-address reuse, partial roster refresh, and direct cases for an owner outside every candidate side and a failed required identity read.

The caller of `participation::Hooks::new` owns the hook family and authenticated process image. It retains each patch and callback until restoration and native quiescence. If restoration fails, the owner keeps the callback state with its code.
