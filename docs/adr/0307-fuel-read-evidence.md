# Keep fuel update days and demand reads distinct

Fuel level and capacity, daily historical totals and branch demand each use their own declared series. The detailed hour reply holds the last read at or before that hour, with its preceding 24 hourly updates. Prefer recorded applied updates and use the history ring's sums otherwise. Omit a day that overlaps one already included, or whose ownership evidence cannot establish that the game updated the country throughout. Keep the omission reason. Fuel quantities use a denominator of 32768 and gains and received amounts use fixed5. Total burn includes flat cost and outgoing lend-lease. Incoming fuel can reach the tank without being part of that day's own gains. A table's sample hour dates the read when available, followed by a known applied day's end or a stamped demand pack. A refused or undated day cannot supply an hour.

An award day must be complete, belong to the read and preserve continuity from before its first update. A recipient's identity uses every recorded operand, including kind, reference, owner, host, stored name and name refusal, so a new name or owner starts another recipient. Reconstruct the demand pack from the recorded header and parts. A consumer occurrence requires the same list position and all identity operands. Reorder the engine's army, navy, air rate tuple for the product's army, air, navy order. Distinguish the native family sum from the sum of known part projections, including the clamped sum.

Keep a forward pass per run for detail history. Seed each stretch with the last read in force at its beginning, searching a day back and then to the run boundary when necessary. Writes of the applied-day and demand-pack columns mark subsequent reads. Inspect up to one day beyond the window to bound the last read. Preserve each gap, each refusal, and each read that omits the selected recipient or consumer. A receipt after an unknown span starts a segment. Group the history cost by actual reads and by the country's parts if requested. Resolve panel label templates and stored names with the recording's content and preserve when the oil count is missing.

The military-branch view reads consumer rows keyed by raw country id and native index, with fixed5 amounts and native priority. The series uses the same consumer index as its part.
