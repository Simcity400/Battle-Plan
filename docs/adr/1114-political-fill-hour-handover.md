# Date political fill and borders at the hours that changed them

The side folds province control, subject colours, naval attributes and border classes from recorded rows. Paint keys are canonical country slots plus one and naval keys are region ids plus one. The demilitarised pass draws the split edges. Zone changes reclassify edges adjoining states that entered or left. Unchanged index runs are shared so the display can retain their buffers.

The side hands over each known hour's changes with the values at that hour. A province row is carried from its last complete reading because each hourly delta names only its written cells. Appearance channels and category changes are dated where the source can be followed. If an appearance row changes slots, a link changes under a previously painted province, or old and new subject-colour sources cannot be followed through intervening writes, the affected inner hours remain uncertified. An unavailable core table or a change the border patch cannot represent also stops incremental dating. A changed subject relationship or zone flag at multiple hours cannot be inferred from its final state. Inner-only refused colours leave with the inner picture. Their refusal cannot blank recorded territory at the publication's end.

The display frame owns the installer. It drains its handover without taking the side, coalesces writes by entity through the frame's head, and leaves future sets pending. Holding the head still keeps the pending queue bounded. When the queues exhaust their separate-hour allowance, they fold the oldest sets into the newer hour while retaining the latest entity values. A pause writes a whole picture. A pace change keeps already dated hours, while a seek invalidates the replaced compose generation and supplies a whole fill. Changing map modes stands the political installer down without losing the whole default-mode fill when it returns.

If a write is refused, the installer keeps the last shown hour and border plans and requests a whole recovery. The recovery carries the current borders even if they have not changed since the refusal. A dated whole fill waits for its hour, replaces older writes, and can be followed by later folded writes in the same set. The reported hour advances only when the relevant sets have been written and the side has dated through it. The optional digest compares the publication-end GPU picture with the fold. It does not certify an interior hour by comparing it with the publication's final arrays.

Regression tests compare step-local values with the recording across multiple steps per publication. They cover head-relative install, unavailable spans, demilitarised row deletion, changing links and subject colours, and a shared or full fill refusing an update.
