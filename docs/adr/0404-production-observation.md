# Observe production spending and preserve source-specific outcomes

The collector observes spending at the production pass rather than reconstructing it from queue progress. Credit and delivery are paired by producer caller and retain native signed values. Some production paths credit without delivery. Naval deployment blocked by manpower discards its progress, and a blocked railway gun retains it. Refit credits one when it delivers the ship. Rocket production has a separate entry path because it can deliver without credit. A null variant bypasses rocket spending. Line registration and the ordinary line census do not gate that entry read. Instant progress is separated from factory output.

Credit must be observed at entry because an organisation's automatic upgrade may refresh the line before the call returns. Military conversion depends on the existing line control and the native target rule. A design with a parent or a conversion-family type qualifies unless licensed, and a convert-from list can independently qualify it. Production hover applies the separate module gate for its output bonus. Conversion progress can be present on a tick that completes nothing. It does not prove removal of a source unit. Spending failure stays independent of delivery and organisation history. Scope failure for a rocket preserves independently authenticated fields.

Mixed construction shares one queue order across building and railway lines. The reader skips railway-gun repair without collapsing ordinals. A corrupt shared root can withhold both families, while a map-root failure can leave the railway queue available. Linked building lines have one primary advancement. It spends progress for both and includes the partner's factories. Railway advancement spends the next segment and can carry excess to one more segment on the normal path. Instant railway advancement applies only to the playing or observed country and its aliases. The flag and country select the path, since ordinary speed may equal the instant amount. The construction bank is reserved into speed and withdrawn after spending. Its effect and market contributions are independent of daily factory production.

Completion is a separate observation after one line advances. Getter calls outside the queue-completion path do not establish a removal. A primary building advance can finish and remove its linked partner before the normal completion check. The collector authenticates the partner's roster slot before attributing the removal. Cancellation can finish a railway line independently of route completion. Events are dated at the advanced hour. Off-thread demand counts native queue occurrences without acquiring their payloads.

Production hover captures cached modifiers and native ordered efficiency occurrences without refreshing them. The factory limit bounds the header demand, while active factories and country count do not. Header sizing still succeeds when the factory payload is unreadable, which lets sizing grow from this input independently. The first matching runtime category token supplies its value. Idle countries need no defines or source trees. Consecutive country-slot parts preserve order and the original first refusal when joined. The define context is acquired at the first country with lines and can be shared among parts. Unsupported eligibility implementations refuse the supplement independently of the existing production lines.
