# 0308. Join country histories through witnessed identities

Membership and identity replies use the recorder's group spans and the query layer's tag holders. A tag change can split a displayed membership without changing it in the game. A missing holder or an unobserved hour closes the run without establishing a join or departure. Faction order zero supplies the leader. An unresolved roster member withholds only that faction's roster. A nonempty recorded faction literal takes precedence over the template name and remains stable across languages for settings. War relations establish when a pair became at war and identify the instigating side. Distinguishing a declaration from an ally joining requires native events. Event dates outside the branch are excluded. An event's country is named at its date, with the read-hour tag used when the dated tag is unavailable.

Territory picker entries use each pair of tag and slot at the earliest timeline and hour where its slot owned or controlled a state. The world row supplies no holder. The search uses summary bins and refines only bins with changes, or bins crossing the requested bounds. This keeps the read proportional to changed holdings. The country look is resolved only at the witness hours.

Log pages and counts use the same declared facets. Supported country scopes filter both the count and page before the ordinals are assigned. An unsupported scope or an unobserved stretch reports the refusal. At the recording head, the service can follow appended rows. A product with a custom row shape retains this shared log transport. Reachable products are registered with their keys and frontend bindings. The generic service routes by key, and declared series can answer without a product implementation.

Intel networks display the game's recorded daily coverage and strength summaries from one country toward another. Those cached values may lag state data. The reply keeps the recorded operands, projects the daily values through live cells, and distinguishes an absent network with complete summaries from an unavailable summary.
