# 0904. Carry the observations that project and raid playback uses

Project assignments retain the primary scientist followed by the supports, including empty references and inactive timers. An independently refused assignment stays distinct. Facility operands carry dated daily sources for speed and specialization. The raid target tooltip uses an hourly facility roster and takes the first matching province in its saved order. The daily project program rows cannot establish that hourly answer. Nuke counters retain the signed amount the top bar uses, with its sentinel and machine-width conversion.

Active raid tooltips keep the hourly operands under the full raid reference in native occurrence order. A reserved reference type identifies definition rows and key zero identifies global subunit rows. The engine reader owns each operand family and its keys. A wing name uses a byte length at the reserved maximum key, with zero-padded little-endian chunks at sequential keys. Negative lengths encode refusal reasons and zero names stay empty. Use the shared stored-text language rule when name provenance is absent.

A country's recorded raid status keeps its precomputed eligibility and its one-use history in order as lexical tokens. The raid offer uses the separate player-status cache. It can rebuild on load, reset or a change of the country being played or observed. A zero reference marks a cache that has yet to be rebuilt. The detected-target bitset is indexed by canonical country slot, with out-of-range slots undetected. An invalid target supplies no offer. Cooldowns block an offer of a type against its scope for the recorded signed duration. Include the dispatcher in a raid path search key so a refusal cannot contaminate another search.

Retain the references of assigned units, the countries shown a raid result, the prep hours and the distance flown. The prep tooltip uses elapsed hours in its preparation phase. Each flight hour adds the matching unit speed, and the viewer decides when its final animation ends. The speed inputs stay available for the last hour of a recent uncancelled raid. Essential equipment determines the source of speed. Keep hourly transport speeds because the daily variant stats cannot establish them. Country and ace modifiers join on the same frame. A living ace's contribution depends on its stored speed, assignment, wing size and the named country modifiers. Preserve the ace's optional fuel and night or weather operands, and distinguish a known missing accumulator entry from a failed read.

Arrow readiness reads the raid's equipment pool and nuke count before launch and in flight. Keep variant entries and the stored archetype totals in order, since the engine clamps the totals separately. Save comparisons always check visible entries, archetype uniqueness, clamp-bound range, and known saved variant archetype presence even when variant definitions are unavailable or incomplete; without an unsaturation witness, archetype totals earn no exact comparison credit and both agreements and disagreements within the clamp bound are reported as consistency observations and limitations. Raid offer rows keep per-country effective range modifiers, convoy reservations and applicable stockpile counts. Offer and counterintelligence inputs are read hourly. The air and missile sites retain order and the country's actual capacity at each base. Their daily slice refreshes early for any country a relevant writer marks.

Regional air scores carry every observed pair with relevant weights, counters or spotting state and a final region for each owner. In a complete table this lets an absent pair for a present owner mean observed zero. An absent owner has no strategic-air object. Movement impact and mission counters preserve the timing of the score write. The independently acquired spotting scale follows the same native phase cadence. Historical formats can omit movement operands. Counterintelligence preserves roster occurrences and deduplicates operative objects before stacking their contributions.
