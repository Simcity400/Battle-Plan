# Prepare dashboard pages without joining foreground playback

Load the restored page's widget code first, even when the map is shown, then warm other renderers while idle. The foreground mounts visible bodies before its layout is first drawn and moves newly visible widgets ahead of queued work. Background mounting is prioritized by viewport distance and then reading order, with at least one mount per frame and enough time left for layout and paint at 120 Hz. Layout-only edits preserve widget body inputs, and clock frames go to visible bodies only. Header portals have their targets on the first body render without requiring a separate frame render.

When foreground work settles while paused, the preparation lane lays out hidden copies at the measured viewport size, starting with the shown page's below-fold bodies and then later pages. Copies use the lane's client, skip native maps and do not register for foreground playback. Completion waits for all mounts and requests and two settled frames, allowing the last reply to commit and schedule effects. Hold the resulting products for that page until re-prepared or removed. Playing or seeking discards the copy, letting foreground requests take the pending work. Discarding it on the starting commit keeps hidden widgets out of the playback pass.

The acceptance probe compares the signature of the widget settings it should draw with the signature its body last committed and painted. The signature includes relevant faction presentation. Matching signatures and a settled body witness the applied setting. Widget errors stay inside their boundary and Retry remounts the failing widget. The catalogue's groups share the sidebar page colours. It stays open while adding widgets and confirms each add. It offers the real widget at its native size and the recording's end, where late-emerging data can be present. The preview can scroll and show hover details, and blocks clicks from opening controls over the catalogue. Its option hooks assume the registry order is fixed after registration.
