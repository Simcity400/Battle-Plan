# 0401. Authenticate character inputs once and keep the two outcomes independent

The character input tables separate role trait arrays from operative links and their resolved operands. The roster visits historical then dynamic occurrences. Country-leader roles use their ideology key, advisor roles use map position, and a scientist has one role. Country-leader trait ordinals distinguish definitions sharing a token. Native trait predicates consult the appropriate arrays and the unit leader chosen from the direct reference or the operative registry. A raw nonzero operative link may exist without its object. A missing skill object carries no level.

A shared roster, page or identity failure affects both tables. Map, trait and operative faults affect their owning table and preserve its first named failure. The role maps authenticate their headers and sentinels even when empty. Each role read checks its vtable and parent character. Row and value demand reflect every valid occurrence. When a row cannot fit, the writer stops storing that table's suffix while continuing to count its demand. An earlier null element has precedence over a later unreadable one.

The captured roster census can supply the authenticated identity and gathered role headers without duplicating their reads. Current country-leader and advisor arrays may come from a carried read only with a certificate. The scientist and operative paths retain their fresh checks. An operative carry places definitions and name strings in the fresh-read order of display name, codename group and custom name. It preserves individual name failures, the whole codename refusal, optional skipped spans, and inline or heap-held storage demand. Only a stored row commits its byte spans. The equality tests check fresh and carried rows across available and insufficient capacities.

Advisor idea tokens are kept in a hash table whose bucket order depends on insertion history. The compiler's empty image array contains no key. The stored bucket distance includes a sentinel and stops a probe when the matching bucket cannot be farther ahead. The reader authenticates the manager and header even when it can carry certified rows without looking at buckets and keys. The collector publishes the keys sorted. Portrait observations track insertion, replacement, whole-holder copies and individual entry copies. Construction and destruction invalidate retained lifetimes, including reuse of an address. Renderer caches are outside the recording.

The scientist walk follows the scientist-roster link of each authenticated country before authenticating character and role objects. The roster wrapper has no established independent class witness. A null country link contributes no rows. A failed roster read or malformed vector withholds both scientist tables. Signed counts determine live membership and specialization order rather than capacity or a fixed limit. A stored level of zero is valid, and experience is its own signed field. Serialization's positive-only rule does not change what the live reader records. An unavailable appended field leaves membership and specialization rows available. Injury and retained-visibility operands are demanded at the supplying read. Only a nonempty visibility conjunction demands a filename, from that specific retained role template. An empty conjunction establishes native true without filename context.
