# End and join battles only when their observations support it

A land battle is a combat handle read at consecutive hours at one province, with a duration counter that does not fall. A reused handle at another province or a reset duration starts a new battle. A gap in the census leaves the prior battle's end unknown. Read and keep distinct side unit counts only when the roster is witnessed. Withhold a side's whole country list when the source row is unavailable, has no country, or contains an unresolved reference.

A finalizer can close a land battle only from a whole land event drain and with matching combat and province. Its date must fit both the drain and the observed battle's bounds. An event between two samples can carry the earlier sample's stamp. Match a just-closed battle before its replacement. A result with no matching battle is published as a result-only row, with unobserved roster and counter columns unavailable. When several results refer to one combat at the same hour, keep only their agreeing result cells and equipment operands. No complete result is required for the observed battle to exist. End rows live for the hour they are published; on resume prime the live battles and end keys from the commit.

A naval battle is the same combat handle and province in consecutive samples. Counts track peak ships and floors on losses. Participants can be detached, so adding sunk counts across hours would duplicate losses. A zero ship handle marks a convoy. Keep a named unknown when the member inputs needed by a count were unavailable.

Join a naval result only by a persistent `NavalResultSources` witness. Use the combat sample at or immediately before the source's game hour when it was observed. An out-of-order source, duplicate result or linked combat, persistent merge, battle that began after the source, or missing combat sample yields a named refusal. Transient strikes cannot witness a battle. For a duplicate result in one hour the later refused source supplies the single observation row, the same rule as a later hour. Resume reconstructs live battles and participants from their tables and restores the last source, previous links, and not-yet-listed results from the blob.
