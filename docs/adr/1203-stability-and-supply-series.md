# 1203. Draw stability and supply from recorded value series and known hours

The stability graph overlays stability and war support in country colors. The default scale is 0–100%. Zoom follows recorded values and can extend beyond that scale where a mod permits it. Raw fractions use 100,000 per whole, and text can retain decimals copied from the answer. Formula terms come from the recorded engine operands. Replace the modifier term with its recorded tree lines and retain a residual and clamp separately. A tooltip preserves source order for the largest lines, with other rows counted to fit the window. The drill-down keeps every recorded line. Unknown terms expose their keys, and missing names use the recorded game's wording when available. Mark a value held from an earlier input read with its read date.

Use recorded availability for stability breakdowns. Within a known interval a sparse part is zero outside its value runs. Outside known intervals and chosen membership it is unknown. Sum in wrapping signed 64-bit arithmetic to reproduce the game's total. Rank parts by magnitude times known duration, allocate each palette color in order and combine the remaining parts in grey. Preserve positive and negative contributions as separate bands even within a grouped layer. Compute and draw only samples overlapping the view and adjacent boundary samples, retaining a crossing band even if there is no sample inside the view. A chart bin uses its first-hour value to avoid exposing future samples. At the head override its active bin only to the shown hour with current followed values. Distinct handles of one source can identify the same part. Ask for names at the latest hour the source is held in the view while its dictionary still contains it.

The main and drill charts share playback when opened from a following widget. The actor and measure must have been drawn. An overlapping click stays unresolved until the user chooses it. Pinned, ranged and campaign views use their own moment and a saved pin stores the raw branch hour independent of chart alignment. The hover and keyboard read a precise breakdown at the selected hour. While focused, wait for a settled hour before asking; retry a hovered failure with bounded backoff. Use one ref per widget lifetime because resetting the observed root forces an extra commit. Reuse dated labels across live reads, preserve separate failure notes per marker source, and expose line styles and values in text. Drop crowded end labels by priority.

Supply health has three share and count series per country. Any missing share in a bin leaves a gap. At the head use current followed values only after the last missing hour in that bin, and clip at the actual head. Read all three counts for a point and distinguish a recorded zero from a missing count. Follow all three counts before painting the head breakdown. A pushed answer can repaint a head without creating a new tile. Hold one point read at a time as the hover moves. Plots keep the newest tiles outside React and wake the painter as answers arrive. A standalone multi-country drill requires a selected actor. It uses the country's tag at the drill origin and follows its recorded identity spans afterward.
