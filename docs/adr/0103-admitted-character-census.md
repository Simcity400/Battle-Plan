# 0103. Loan one complete role census per stopped-world callback

After the roster pass, gather the role fields once from that callback's complete portrait census. Include identity, exact country bytes, leader and advisor map headers, unit-leader pointer, scientist pointer and operative reference. The role jobs and the character-input consumer share these owned observations while the game is stopped. Grow occurrence storage away from acquisition because both vectors need room without resizing or holding a worker. A guarded gather admits the census only if it copied every field of every occurrence. A copied occurrence can supply the role fields a reader's requested view needs.

A nonzero generation names exact held content. Equality requires the same scope, historical length, ordered occurrence pointers and every gathered byte. Comparing with the latest held observation permits later role jobs to match whole inputs without reading or inspecting them again. If the preceding history is loaned, locking is contended, or advisory storage cannot grow, take a fresh generation and do not keep an incomplete observation. Zero means no admitted content. Keep the live roster demand for later visit-list sizing.

When the census writer token taken before the last complete gather repeats, and the scope and ordered occurrence identities still match, skip gathering role fields again. A matching portrait stamp skips the occurrence comparison. The same exact-byte history remains available to distinguish fresh observations that differ even when their identities match.

Copy sentinels in bounded guarded batches, offering all hints before reading the completed prefix. A failed or unaddressable item ends that prefix. Check the map header before gathering its sentinel. If the sentinel lies within the owned view, the reader consumes the owned bytes and gathering it separately would change its read behavior.

The parity fixture renders each requested byte window on demand at country scale. It compares decoded rows, arena bytes, failures and capacity demand across the loaned path, independent roster walks and the typed walk. It measures guarded calls at the copy, scatter, gather and prepared-plan boundaries used by live acquisition. A held loan or missing role jobs prevents a new gather for that hour without waiting.
