# 0204i. Hold naval counter inputs at the scope that certifies them

One authenticated fleet walk reads fleet and task-force rosters and the independent counter inputs. The task force's fixed fields come from one guarded gather shared by the readers. The capture's first-combat memo is separate from the division and railway captures and is cleared between captures. Port mission inputs use a separate text arena so their growth and refusals cannot consume the cached-name pool. Port rows preserve independent unavailable fields and the acquisition order.

Mission-region IDs and the fresh ETA location terms are held only for an observed native world occurrence. Fleet leaders and receiver censuses need their own certificates. The reader takes a receiver certificate before reading or serving a census. A capture may keep that census after completion only if the certificate still matches. Each capture takes the prior held census set before it starts. A refused or interrupted part cannot return those censuses or claim unavailable data as an empty set. Census entries are ordered by identity for lookup. Fleet-region retention checks the certificate on both sides of a fresh read, and compacts retired entries only when they substantially outnumber the live ones.

Native reads, certification and the dependent refusal state are settled before sending the owned tail to FIFO decode. A later sibling failure preserves a completed walk's refusals. The acquired owner set is eligible as a certificate only with a complete movement outcome. The capture clears temporary owners on every exit, including a staged prefix that is never published. Projection refuses repeated task-force handles because one task force belongs to one fleet roster. Its supply display preserves the sign of a nonzero amount smaller than half a display step.

A fleet header read that races its writer cannot use the newer token, even if the old entry is cached. The next quiet observation retries the header and does not treat that refusal as a permanent empty value.
