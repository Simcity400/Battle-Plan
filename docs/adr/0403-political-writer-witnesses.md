# 0403. Track political structure, popularity and party leaders independently

The hourly Politics pass observes two domains. Structure covers capitals, election date, ruling party and party and idea membership. Popularity changes daily, so it has its own token. Daily politics updates popularity through the clamped setter and writes the last election only on election days. It reaches ruling-party and idea writers through their separate observable paths. When the politics family forwards daily politics, move the popularity token every time and move the structure token when the election date changes.

The capital setter covers restoration after state control, replacement, transfer, effects, civil war, peace and capital change application. Appearance reset also covers dynamic reuse and runtime reset. The political status pointer and back-link are set by construction. Election date changes through politics setting, the election update, and status clearing on reset or load. Forced ruling-party selection comes from puppet and subject paths and peace conferences. Ideology selection is used by politics setting, civil war and console. Party build and clear are reset and load operations. Idea replacement reaches the single runtime writer through two entry paths from effects, expiries, focuses, daily changes, commands and console.

Daily politics and forced ruling-party selection are observed through their entries because the clamped popularity setter starts with a branch. The party and popularity effects are observed at their call to it. The additive and redistributing writers change multiple parties inline. Relation boosts first store an ideology on the relation and daily politics applies it. All these writers run on the game thread. The politics capture family forwards reset and daily politics. Save readers, validation and load completion run in the load epoch.

PoliticalParties carries the party's id, ideology group, tag and name fields. Construction is the only writer of id, ideology and tag. Name assignment writes the keys and clears the default flag. It is reached from name and politics effects and from the civil-war, dynamic reuse and peace copy paths, with loader and destructor as lifetime writes. The hourly walk still compares the entire party pointer list and reads ruling party and popularity. ActiveIdeas compares every slot pointer and uses the session's fixed idea definition tokens. Reading those lists each hour removes reliance on additional hooks for the array headers and memberships.

PartyLeaders needs a certificate for the party vector and a certificate for each character whose id and leader-map key are used. The leader's id and character link are established by creation or parsing, then published through a vector insert. Promotion can insert or move the new leader to the front. Role addition, leader creation, registration and exile can append. Removal covers role removal, retirement, death and post-load validation. Civil war can clear a vector or transfer between two distinct parties, invalidating both. Party destruction occurs on status clear, appearance reset or destruction. Load-only array construction fills fresh objects. The collector checks the country party array hourly, and the politics family already owns the interior build site. An election can change popularity or ruling party without a leader vector write.
