# Infer peace outcomes from complete reads

The recorder samples conference rosters daily, so a single roster cannot establish the effects of a conference. `PeaceOutcomes` compares complete snapshots of identities, owners and wars. An open conference uses the most recent complete read before it, advanced while the conference is unfinalized. After the conference is finalized or disappears, the first complete later read can settle it. Overlapping sampled conferences prevent individual attribution. Reappearance after a conference went away opens a new span even if its ID is reused.

A territorial transfer requires two different known owners at war in the earlier snapshot and evidence that their war ended by the later one. A changed tag on the same underlying country, an unresolved or conflicting relation, or an incomplete read cannot certify a transfer. A complete later snapshot without the war pair can prove it ended. Consecutive complete hours also yield unclaimed outcomes for transfers no single sampled conference accounts for. Transfers are grouped by connected pairs, ordered by the least holder. New subjects of a winner, releases from a loser's territory and government changes of a loser are published alongside witnessed transfers. Government changes compare the ruling party, its token and a recorded leader ideology.

Outcome rows live at the later hour. Conference spans live at the hour that moved them. If both roster observation and settlement change a span during one hour, only the final row is set. Owners and existence can stay keyed by raw country IDs, while comparisons use tags. Ambiguous tag or slot mappings withhold attribution. Subject expiration can invalidate the current snapshot without a newly written subject row.

Incremental reads reuse the world in the current tables. A gap forces those tables to be read whole again. The resume blob keeps the prior complete read, tag numbering, first existence hours and open or waiting conferences with their earlier snapshots. Map keys are serialized in order to make identical state produce identical bytes. The writer primes itself from the commit hour so it can resume without emitting its already-written rows again.
