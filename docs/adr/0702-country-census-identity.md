# Keep country links distinct from physical census slots

An authenticated country has a raw native link and a canonical census slot. Preserve both and publish one row per canonical country. A null positive slot alone proves nothing about its link. A readable zero link is unresolved. Invalid or unreadable links fail the table even if the output is full. Authenticated relation operands resolve a positive id through country links. When a referenced object already gives a pointer, its resolved id must lead back to that object.

Ordinary country readers admit positive references only. Queries that act on the actual reserved country take physical slot zero, including readable nonzero links resolving to that slot where the native query accepts them. Share that root's selection, count, and authentication attempt within one module, state, and callback. Keep a failed selected pointer private so an equal positive physical alias receives the same failure without rereading. No other positive root can be excluded on that evidence. A complete positive roster can supply its authenticated exact links, and any other valid link takes the ordinary native read.

Prepare and clear the memo once for each pass. The owned roster snapshot can replay that pass's authenticated results without allocation during acquisition. When capacity is short it keeps the demand but publishes no incomplete positive census. The independently observed reserved root or first failure stays available even if the positive capture is incomplete. The snapshot clears both lanes before the next pass and never carries worker-native references across loans.

Memo tables keep raw aliases and canonical slots separate. Retain successes, absences, and first read failures in their own lanes. A reserved query without a shared source cannot reread the reserved root or turn into a positive lookup. Grow from the authenticated header count or the number of distinct admitted questions. If growth fails, later unseen references are refused without a query, and counted demand remains a lower bound. Preparing one arena must not leave a mismatched pair when the other arena's allocation fails.

The country row gathers its independent scalar fields in their original order. If a gather stops, the field it faults on retains its own error without retry. Later fields are consulted only if the ordinary row would reach them. Political identity and ruling-party evidence can fail independently of the Countries row. Retain signed fixed-point stability, command power and war support without narrowing, and take convoy quantity only when the cache is valid.

The economy and intelligence objects have their own authentication boundaries. Decision state, the dynamic modifier container and the operation-token manager require their country backlink. The resource and convoy objects require their own vtable. The parallel country-character and scientist views reach the same owner. Candidate ship and volunteer-tally counts recorded in test support do not establish runtime writer coverage.
