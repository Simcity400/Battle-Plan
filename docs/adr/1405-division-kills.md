# 1405. Division damage and Enemy killed

## Context

The game keeps no count of the enemy a division has killed. A division's only casualty counter is its own losses ("Casualties taken"). In land combat each attack lowers the target's strength and adds the same damage to a pool of strength damage pending on the target; the pool does not note the attacker. When the target leaves the combat, its loss fraction is the pool over its maximum strength, capped at one and scaled by a define, and every manpower pool of the division loses that fraction, rounded to whole men. Close air support and other damage add to the same pool without an attacking division.

At the moment an attack is resolved the game has the attacking division, the target and the strength the target lost, and hands them to the per-army combat-log writer. Border-war combats are resolved by the same routine.

## Decision

- **One hook**, a call-site hook on the attack resolver's call to the per-army activity writer, installed with the land-event family after the combat-edge hooks and withheld by name if it fails to locate or construct, or if the edge hooks are withheld. It copies the combat, the attacking division, the target, the attacker's army (none when it is in no army) and the strength lost, and nothing else.
- **Folded at the boundary, published at the exit.** The serialized land-event boundary sums each hour's hits per combat, target, attacker and army, and publishes the sums as `LandDivisionDamage` rows when the target's exit is drained. A combat's end or the target's destruction drops what is left open; any incomplete hour, or a hit or exit that could not be read, drops everything open and withholds that hour's table. Hourly rows would repeat every attacker and target pair for each hour of a battle, and no reader needs them: a share is only known once the target's losses are realised at its exit.
- **The pool is read at the exit.** `LandCombatEdges` exits carry the strength damage pending on the division before the flush clears it (`pending_strength_raw`), so a share is taken against the whole pool, and losses caused by air support or other damage go to no division.
- **Enemy killed is derived by the recorder.** The `DivisionKills` writer, at each hour both tables are complete, gives each attacker `losses × its damage ÷ pending pool` of each exited target's realised losses (the target's own casualty counter across the exit), and keeps the running total per division, fixed-point with five decimals. A share that cannot be worked out (losses or pool not read, or division damage larger than the pool) marks the division's count incomplete from that hour. The table is complete from the first hour both inputs are recorded in a branch until either is not; after that it is unavailable for the rest of the branch, because every later count would be short.
- **Meaning.** The figure counts the battles the recording holds. Men lost when a division is destroyed are not in any casualty counter, so they are credited to no one, as in Casualties taken. The shares are exact up to the game's own rounding of each manpower pool to whole men; when the pool exceeds the target's maximum strength the capped losses are still shared in proportion.
- **Older formats.** Recordings before format 152 open with `LandDivisionDamage` and `DivisionKills` missing and the edges' pool column missing; every reader shows Enemy killed as not recorded.
