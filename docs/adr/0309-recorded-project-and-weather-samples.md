# Date project and weather details to the observation that supplies them

Special projects show recorded facts and never infer attempts across reads. A project is a country, raw owner and local id. One custody check decides every country-ledger reading: a read of an owner's Projects, history or programs supplies an hour when its table was complete at the read, it is within a day, the country held the tag and the owner stayed linked to it from the read through that hour. The list, details, effects, iteration, scientist and the completed-project trigger all use it. Details, effects and iteration read each project row through one lookup, at its own stamp, never from the playhead frame. The list is published at an hour only when the owner roster is read and every owner on it has a usable Projects read. History is the authority for what happened, kept whatever Projects shows; a project seen only in history is published with an unknown status. Each choice and completion marker is kept once by its identity, with its own date and the read that first listed it, and each ordinal remembers the entry it last held while custody lasts. A marker counts as a completion when it is dated within the campaign, no later than its read, and its owner stayed with the country from its date to its read. The status is the latest usable Projects read, or not recorded, and each owner's read at each hour is in the reply. A status carries no completion date. Dated completions are history entries. Each is named by the completed Projects row usable at the read that first listed it, when nothing follows it in that read and that row was read at or after its date; otherwise its name is not recorded. The feed keys completion evidence by raw owner so recorded completions are emitted once at their game date even when the owner changes tag before the observing read or lacks a recorded tag; each completion is attributed to the tag its continuously linked canonical slot held at its completion date, or carries a named owner-not-recorded state otherwise. A first usable read already complete is "already complete when first recorded". The recording identifies no cancellation marker, so a cancellation is shown only from a Projects read and is never dated. A new attempt is shown only where one history read lists a completion marker followed by a later entry, or a read holds a different entry at an ordinal than it last held. Missing entries prove nothing; they make the count not recorded. Iterations are the choices after the last marker in the usable history read. The scientist comes from the program read whose owner and active project are the project's, with that read's hour on the status. Observed unassignment, an unread program and the last observed scientist stay apart. Every view is cut at the playhead, and the follow equals the batch reply at every prefix. An iteration opens only when a Projects read is stamped at the choice's date with the listing's definition, its history is a prefix of the listing read's, no completion marker lies between that history and the choice, and no read between them proves a replacement; otherwise it is withheld whole. Choices are labelled by the definition in the Projects read usable at their history read. Effect and iteration scripts read the accepted Projects rows of every linked country: a matching completed row proves that country completed a project, usable reads of all its owners prove it did not, and anything else, a project with no recorded spelling included, is unknown. Details and effects spell a project from the read that supplies it. Unstarted pool rows stay hidden. Feed completions keep the game date and the observation hour distinct.

Daily project details use the last available read in their day while the same holder owned the tag. A sliced table's date applies to the owner's own rows. A scientist's daily reading needs a matching roster entry. The scientist reference and role template are resolved from the daily read, as are recorded facility speed and its sources. Empty templates establish visibility directly. Evaluate nonempty templates against the recorded world of that read, cached by branch, read hour and country. Find the recorded project definition by the game's case-insensitive match, then use recorded context for scoped names and the authored order of sprite choices. Effects preview reads the tables scripted effects can name only when requested.

Weather samples each day at the last hour its region table was known, with the latest read stamp at or before that point. Fold whole days into the requested column count and count observed days separately from clear days. Modifiers use a witness to distinguish a recorded zero from a missing value. Any member province without proven temperature withholds the equal-weight region mean. Temperature and province membership depend on the recorded province graph, while conditions and modifiers can remain available without it. A request beyond the timeline can list names from the nearest recorded hour. It cannot supply measurements for the missing window.

Raid history follows the indexed lifetime and changed cells of each raid row. Preparation is kept for preparing phases and compressed into linear runs. The first progress observation or finite nonzero distance establishes launch, including a raid first observed already ended. The course remembers the last recorded raider, phase and outcome. Prefer the end event for the final target and outcome. Where it is absent from the requested span, use the target history from the raid's last hour. Resolve the target's explicit province before a nested scope's location, using the game's location order. A declared target-specific sprite can specialise a raid type's default art.
