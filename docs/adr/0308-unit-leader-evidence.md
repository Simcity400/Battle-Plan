# 0308. Date unit leader careers from recorded lifetimes and gains

Unit leader products identify a leader by the full handle. They select roster entries with a recorded owner at the requested hour. A captured role can require conditions in the owner's scope. The planner reads the leader and character inputs first and loads the full trigger world only for an unresolved conditional role. A withheld world prevents a conditional role's visibility verdict. Unconditional roles use the local inputs. A complete verdict is shared at the same run and hour while its content catalogue remains valid.

Career totals read the last applicable sample of each run and subtract the previous totals where the branch enters that run partway. A battle participation joins to the first result of the same combat read at or after it. A subsequent held-before or first observation leaves an earlier trait-gain date intact. A new gain moves that date. A partial battle log leaves lower-bound counts. The career and picker retain recorded command lifetimes and last service hours across recorded stretches. An absent row or missing career operand ends its run. The head view keeps the chosen leader's experience and training as live cells keyed by commander reference or training key. Formation names without content keys stay in their recorded language.

Names and portraits come from the leader's recorded character and the content for the selected role. Conditional skill requirements without enough recorded inputs yield an unavailable ceiling.
