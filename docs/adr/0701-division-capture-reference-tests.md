# 0701. Compare division capture changes against serial observations

Keep a direct scalar or per-object reference independent of gathered field layouts. It witnesses the original field widths, order, admission and refusal boundaries. The test adapters count actual guarded operations without counting the internal spans twice. Declining a speculative gather selects the original serial path. A partial failed gather does not make its resulting bytes usable. A transient failure reached early by a batch can recover, and persistent faults must keep the original failure path. Capacity probes and stale scratch must not authorize source rows beyond the admitted prefix. Preserve the same result across replay boundaries, stat-block aliases and held observations.

Synthetic division identity exercises corrupt vtables and complete handles, a foreign resolved owner, contradictory country aliases, malformed roster counts, and unreadable or mismatched provinces and templates. The division's group and leader consumers keep their own results under partial reference faults. The movement comparison explicitly exercises shared province identity through source and target references. Status and movement checks also distinguish unrequested optional suffixes from refused ones. Cold benchmarks spread objects beyond typical last-level cache capacity and measure the original and shared-read paths over the same logical objects.

The synthetic native timing adapters translate only addresses inside their owned immutable source. Keep the source and every pointee alive until all runs finish. The guarded movement fixture keeps its source arrays immutable and passes failure cases solely through the guarded copy protocol. These conditions support the test-only `LiveMemory` constructions without changing normal capture ownership.
