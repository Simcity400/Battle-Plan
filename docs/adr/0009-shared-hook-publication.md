# 0009. Publish hook families under one thread suspension

The runtime collects each taking-part family's requested publication, performs the batch under one stable census and suspension, then replays the families' normal bookkeeping with their recorded outcomes. `Round` excludes nesting, and nested calls publish on their own. A call reached only during reporting also publishes independently. Hooks belong to one family, and the round's collected slices and distinct pointers borrow those owners until report. The publisher has exclusive access in that interval. The temporary vector used to collect references does not own the hooks.

Attach publishes only the eligible prefix in runtime order. Empty predecessors still perform their atomic preparation. The publisher runs each reached family's preparation before its checks and stops at the first busy or refused publication. Each successful family's writes are flushed and verified and its original protections restored before the next writes. Shared pages are reopened using the next family's immutable saved protections. Atomic preparation inside the suspension may use atomic operations only. Reference-vector construction and other allocating or native work stay outside the suspension. A missing reference vector records a failure only if the chain reaches that family. Report consumes an existing outcome before rebuilding references so it cannot introduce a new allocation failure after a later family has already written.

Detach checks each eligible family independently, even when one is busy or refused. It restores all affected pages before resuming threads. A shared page reaches its old protection if any of its restores succeeds, while a family gets success only when all its own restores succeed. A thread at any family site or tap postpones the family's write. The watch tables and executable owners stay live until hooks were never enabled or were removed and drained after the last capture.

An instruction-cache flush error triggers rollback for every written group. Reopening a page for rollback can fail, in which case that page remains untouched and the executable owner stays reachable for a future detach. A second flush failure also retains the owners. A thread resume failure is shared by all participants and takes precedence over per-family preparation errors. A failed batch allocation falls back to serial reporting without allowing a later family to write before the missing one. Refusal diagnostics retain numeric and symbolic facts during the suspension and format after resume.
