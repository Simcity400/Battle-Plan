# Refuse a frame only when its political map cannot be established

The political map requires complete `Provinces`, `CountryLinks`, and `Countries`. Unavailable or truncated core tables cannot establish province ownership and canonical country identity, so the viewer refuses the frame by naming the table and preserving the recording's reason. Rendering a subset would imply ownership information for the rest. With a complete core, other gaps degrade individual features. Early-hour collector buffer growth and staggered collection can leave a replay with truncated or omitted tables.

The window and dump share one ordered registry of limitations. Its statements describe the display effect, so feature passes only have to decide what they can draw. A complete table can still hold an unusable field, such as raw province-name bytes that are not text. Field omissions have their own messages. The viewer reports limitations of a plan actually drawn, so an hour without a plan does not report missing operands of one. The canonical perspective and fog control accompany the render, including the reason a requested country could not be shown.

With fog disabled, admission returns full visibility before reading sight inputs. With fog enabled, missing unit evidence can leave stack appearance at its fog-off presentation, with the limitation reported. Missing province fog inputs have their own report. A fractional frame uses the earlier recorded fog snapshot. Co-belligerency requires all contributing relation tables before admitting other countries' counters. In an older recording that lacks `DivisionStatus`, figure and exercise glyph inputs come from `DivisionModelInputs`.
