# Carry rail output only under a complete batch witness

RailCooldowns, RailEdges and the selected building inputs share one acquisition. It only needs the game state, so its worker can start in the first burst. After the frame reset, installing its result swaps storage with the frame and carries its outcomes and cost past Late. A failed rail job refuses all three of its tables.

The worker records batches of rail slots in native order, including cooldowns, edges, selected levels and the original building pointer state. Before any slot reads, it consumes the hour's writer marks and reads the manager's cooldown list. A held batch can skip all slot, province, info and building reads when its observation epoch and manager and table headers still match, no mark falls in that batch, and its last read held every info it found. The marks consumer and cached output store belong to the same worker loan. A frame can invalidate the store without allocating. On the next walk the worker drops the held outputs. Accounting exposes bytes measured at the last finish so the game thread does not walk the store.

A daily countdown can modify every occurrence on both the held and current list. Mark those entries even when a mod supplies duplicate or non-positive cooldown entries. Outside the countdown the lists must agree in their counts of unmarked provinces. Slot planning also compares each freshly gathered identity, cooldown and pair header against its held location. When a batch must be walked, an unmarked info with a matching location can still carry its held payload. An unmarked changed, new or vanished info reveals an unseen write, so the rest of the walk reads fresh. An invalid province cannot name a bounded slot. The raw fixture countdown visits the list backwards and removes expired entries with a swap, preserving its repeated decrement semantics.

Room for planning is reserved for one batch. If a particular info cannot be retained or does not follow the held outputs in slot order, that batch lacks a complete witness and is read again next hour. An incomplete walk, refused take or failed reservation for the store invalidates the held result. A failed job whose take consumed the marks must forget the store. The first complete walk also establishes the slot count for sizing the mark bitmap, even when no certificate could be retained. The parity fixtures compare every carried row, level and outcome against a fresh walk and count guarded reads to distinguish a skipped batch from a reused field after a slot read.
