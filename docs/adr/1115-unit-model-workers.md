# 1115. Place unit figures on the frame and resolve their models on workers

Land stacks use the same province, canonical owner and next two route hops as the direct viewer. The oldest member supplies placement and model-selection operands. A stack without a visible member has no figure. The layer uses the ordered vision product and will name a refusal if it is missing. A sight change updates just the affected divisions unless the perspective or the whole sight inputs changed. Vision changes remain available until their dependent layers settle.

The camera can defer re-keying divisions while neither the old nor the new stack location is visible. That work is reached when the province is admitted. Above the land cutoff, the layer drops its land stacks but retains their table declarations, so returning across the cutoff re-keys from the mirrors without a reset. Naval, raid and convoy models have the separate ship cutoff. When a figure is marked for replacement, the current field can draw and animate it at its last placement until its new placement is ready.

The mesh worker reads decoded views for the step, declaring equipment by keys of the oldest stack members and learning further tables read by content. The clock is always an input since a mod's graphics limits can compare dates. The worker preserves a chosen mesh while waiting on new input lanes and reuses answers until the announced dependency changes reach them. It drops results superseded by a reset and removes withdrawn asks from the wait set. A mesh can stand at a later hour without being rebuilt when no dependency changes.

A separate worker builds the remaining models from the newest decoded frame and finishes each started build. It reuses held output if the input tables and presentation request are unchanged, and learns further table reads without replacing the held models prematurely. On reset the old field can remain painted until the new generation is ready. A result for a different canonical perspective is withheld. The ships and convoys layers supply their own products when appropriate. Worker lag contributes to the model publication hour, while frame placement work is carried separately. A saved frame requires both workers and every outstanding model choice to be whole at its applied hour.
