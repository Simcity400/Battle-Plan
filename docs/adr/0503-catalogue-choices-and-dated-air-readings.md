# Keep catalogue choices stable across recordings and show recorded air readings

Aircraft catalogues page through the complete listing. A listing's lease, content generation, scope and query own its pages. Changing any of them cancels outstanding work, and reinstating an effect requests cancelled pages again. An answered page must match its requested range. The first answered page establishes the counts. Debounced search requests only pages covering the inclusive row range, leaving a failed page's retry focusable. Page completion or failure advances a revision because the pages are updated in place. Individual selection follows the recording on this device, and failure to store it keeps the session choice.

Widget settings and in-chart chips share one selection. When no saved item exists in a recording, show its automatic default. The largest recorded item wins if the widget ranks a default, preserving order on ties, and the first item can stand in when none have a recorded size. The first toggle starts from those displayed choices and preserves saved tokens for other campaigns. Tokens absent here count against the stored limit, and one token can consume several lines. Removing a token is allowed while another displayed choice remains. Campaign-local handles store their campaign key with the token so a reused handle cannot silently select a different subject. Encode arbitrary content keys before storing them.

Air widgets preserve recording-specific faction, coalition and theatre identities. They can share a setting schema and keep choices in independent widget specs. The view compares known friendly and enemy scores using its recorded denominator, signed multiplication and strict thresholds. An unknown score makes the comparison unknown, and a known zero share is left out. Activity or a changing share can be painted from the latest push without replacing region and side structure. The compact tooltip still reports unknown scores and assigned wings. A selected theatre absent at that date keeps its selectable ID. A loss drill binds its selected tag to the native holder at the picked hour, then reads that holder's full history. A range keeps its recorded date bound, whereas a pinned moment refers to a branch offset.

Artwork validation preserves the useful part of a conforming entry when its image cannot be drawn, replacing only the icon with the placeholder and keeping the reply identity. A malformed entry still refuses the reply. Localized text can be genuinely empty, while unresolved keys stay unavailable. Construction names and coordinates hold that distinction and describe a railway by its endpoints when available, then fall back to the recorded province or a named refusal.

Equipment widgets use names resolved against the recorded content stack and share the refit setting. An unresolved equipment token receives a storable marker shared with the data product so the widget can still select it and identify it in diagnostics. Its title and arm remain unavailable. A variant's known name applies only from its recorded read, until a gap or an unavailable read ends that interval.
