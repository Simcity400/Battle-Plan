The weather writer preserves fixed-point values and projects flag bytes to truth. Region writers inspect full scalar words before applying zero omission. Temperature is always emitted and absence is incomplete evidence. The manager emits every array slot in native order, so reject gaps and repeated slots without collapsing them. Temperature may be reconstructed from a recorded anchor and pass operands. Refused derivation withholds the temperature comparison. Weather clock words depend on the recorded update and lack an independent save counterpart.

Use the recorded mod stack’s weather and static modifier definitions. Compare saved province lists in exact order with days, and treat a refused or historically absent list as its own state. Rebuild a province accumulator from the weather mud, thermal, static list and ground snow values, adding each defined and categorized key at the game’s multiplier. Child modifier entries are separate, while a surviving zero-value entry still creates a key. A static modifier with only disabled entries and no surviving tooltip or child is empty. Match native scaling using a wrapping multiplication and signed division. A missing content definition or failed acquisition leaves that accumulator comparison incomplete.

The save’s current temperature and snow determine the load rebuild, but a live accumulator may hold its earlier mud, thermal, and snow contributions. Reconstruct candidate states from content intervals. Merge identical contributions and require an identifiable fit before certifying the saved list’s part. An impossible or distinct ambiguous fit remains visible. A stale runtime modifier whose days have ended can still remain in the captured accumulator until a rebuild, while the save no longer names it. Compare missing, additional, and different entries against the relevant rebuild by definition name where known.
