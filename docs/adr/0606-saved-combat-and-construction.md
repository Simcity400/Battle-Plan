# Preserve saved combat and construction occurrences before comparison

Game save comparisons use the saved country and group hierarchy with full typed references and original order. Combat logs preserve the sequence of managers and group records, then each loss reason's monthly entries and their first-write hours. The save's equipment pools retain their allow-zero policy. The application may compare a saved land battle with ordinary or border-war combat, whose transfer-state fields and unconditional signed combat width are only present in the border-war form. Keep the six damage values in air, ground and prevented order with strength before organisation for each. Each optional positive-only value carries omission separately. Naval combat retains the original-health and damage values by side, the six-by-six damage history, optional damage-by-type, and the stored disengage flag's absence. Source zero is the stored null reference. An air wing without an external wave retains the invalid initial date from the writer.

Fleet counters keep each fleet, task force and division in native roster order. A present but empty list can differ from omitted input. Keep spotters and accessible regions in their original order with repeats and null references where emitted. A strike-force target is emitted when either reference half is nonzero, independent of whether it resolves. Naval transfers instead include only nonzero unit handles resolved by their writer. Keep their source province scalar and combat reference order. The writer's conditional fields retain their presence for the comparator, including fleet pride, regional missions, detached activities and the attached headquarters count.

Construction compares physical queue positions across building and railway entries. A skipped item still occupies its queue position. Positive-only repair counts retain their nonpositive class when omitted. Partial health reports progress towards one repaired level. The literal `remaining_hours` on railway construction measures the remaining work operand. A country's offsite building uses a country key and zero location. Occupation retains the literal law key, nullable group pointer and ordered settings and modifier vectors. Garrison manpower records positive rows only. The save writer always emits both equipment objects, so missing objects remain distinct for the oracle. A suppression target omitted by the writer certifies exact fixed-five zero.

The resource-origin reader keeps both occurrences of `efficiency` in their emitted order, since the convoy client's value and the origin's stored product are separate fields. The convoy request precedes the naval route cost reduction. Land routes retain state IDs and naval routes retain strategic-region IDs. The resource-rights writer sorts by resource name, which cannot establish the live list order. Preserve resource owners, sparse origin states and the ordered building-override entries. The separately read state-history resource templates are initial content, while the saved resource pools and modifiers are runtime state.
