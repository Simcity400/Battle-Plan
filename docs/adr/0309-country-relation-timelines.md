# Resolve country and war products against their recorded timeline

Country styles use changes in recorded looks to recover each holder's name and colour, plus artwork selection in its recorded mod stack. Resolve the names in the reader's language using the recorded operands. A dynamic or mod-added country follows the same identity rule. A period before a tag was held is explicitly without a country. Where world tension contributions outlive the country, use the slot's last holder. Preserve a recorded threat source's reason text or unrenderable bytes and reason. A threat life starts from the sources listed at the beginning of a stretch because the change log is silent there. Its identity includes both country references, date, reason, and ordinal among equal sources. Use ordered recorded reads for keyboard steps and changed tension where an older recording lacks read dates. Tension is stored in thousandths of a percentage point and its contributions in hundred-thousandths. Country factory groups use recorded content flags, including mod-defined buildings. Conflicting flags leave the type outside the named groups and generate a warning. Nuclear totals truncate each type's amount before signed 32-bit addition, and unnameable types remain reported. Scoreboard columns are declared measures, each with its own ranking and missing reasons.

Subject intervals follow the map's observed relation rules. Cancellation ends an observation, and an end after its start bounds it. Identical parties with different start dates are separate relations. Level comes from the subject's current recorded autonomy state, preserving a switch between levels with equal numeric bounds. Missing identity or coverage remains unknown. Resolve the level's order and localisation from the recorded content. Territory uses the world row as denominator, and computes land area from the recording's own map facts. Its region panel names structure separately from moving live amounts. Trade flows cut at either holder's tag change and count unreadable or unattributable deals instead of treating them as zero. Volunteer links are simultaneous observations rather than accumulated dispatches. Land links and tallies have the union of both observed keys. No row implies none only while the sender's list is known and the table was available and read.

The training queue uses a daily read under the same holder. Show the divisions actually recorded under an order and their stored training progress. A design whose type is unknown may satisfy an equipment requirement, so it can withhold attribution. An order's location follows the owning state and its stored priority text follows the content's interface and current recorded scope. The stability series carries the last reading between sampled hours and exposes its read date separately. Stability breakdowns preserve identities and recorded text even without content. A missing modifier registration keeps its sentinel, and command power supplies the two command power totals from its own record. Character naming prefers the saved name and leaves scoped text unknown where no country context can evaluate it.

Build a viewpoint's war timeline from native groups of recorded relations at the hours their structural inputs change. Per-run history replays those changes in memory, so the work depends on changes rather than campaign length. Groups have timeline continuity through recorded relations. Each relation is keyed by its canonical and raw participants and start hour. A name uses the representative relation, goals, state and country names as of the first suitable observation. Read the first naming goal on each side, its type literal and the state names at that hour. Read historical looks for naming operands and follow overlord links, using the reserved country row only where a naming chain reaches it. Cache the walk by timeline, viewpoint and catalogue. Keep casualty counters outside the structural walk. Read them by the relation lifetimes active in the asked hour. Sum known losses across the relations, taking unknown-attacker losses once per country at their largest because the game repeats them in its relations. A missing lifetime withholds its total. Contributions use native groups at the asked hour. War goal progress is a moving live cell, and relationship status depends on recorded war or faction evidence.

Naval region views use the recorded map's sea provinces, naval terrain and controlled-ratio setting to establish coverage and minimum supremacy, cached per resolved content. Technology artwork requests are independent of research history and labels, and reject the reserved null template index. Country flag lookup also checks whether the recording identifies a usable country object for the atlas.
