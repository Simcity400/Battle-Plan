# Anchor fuel demand and score to their supplied observations

Fuel demand records each occurrence under the tooltip's traversal and independently retains its roster, expeditionary and logical owners. A signed-positive country override can select a different owner from the pool owner. A wing's context index belongs to that exact read. Use the same-hour context rather than a later regional lookup. Rates are fixed5 and describe present required use independently of the completed daily fuel awards. Carry the recorded game-language name bytes through the shared language guard. Native cached army and navy sums and the recomputed air sum preserve wrapping; a reporting consumer needing a widened sum must work from the contributors. Failure in one family leaves the others and observed contributors available.

Treat the supplying CountryFuel row's read hour as the default demand-pack time. It stores a constant tag in current recordings. A current explicit stamp has its own tag, while legacy recordings store the hour as an untagged word. Store the pack's six lists as independent rows when the supplying table is complete and positions and repeated identities can be represented. The key contains country, list, identity and occurrence, and a separate position restores native order and context indices. Verify contiguous positions during assembly. An unrepresentable or truncated read retains its whole packs and withholds the parts table. This keeps one contributor's change independent of other list elements and preserves the historical whole-pack layout.

Game score keeps the game's stored witnesses rather than inferring counts from current unit inventories. Preserve each fleet occurrence and its signed task-force count with native wrapping. Preserve the air subsystem's first-site list including repeated bases and its order. Distinguish an unreadable subsystem, an observed null subsystem and a present subsystem with an empty list. Retain stale or reset headers when that is the game's source. Wing witnesses use every wing in the shared census, join by full handle, and preserve the signed mission selector for the score operand. Unreadable leaves remain independently unknown. Record each effective fixed5 country or part define independently, and apply the part multipliers before the total multiplier.
