# 1110. Derive map visibility from a recorded country viewpoint

The country choice and fog switch are independent. The viewer can follow the recording client's viewpoint, a named tag, or a canonical country slot. A slot follows its country through tag changes and keeps the supplied label for messages. If the requested country is absent, the viewer resolves the recording's default. If the recording lacks its client viewpoint, the lowest canonical slot supplies the viewpoint. An hour with no country cannot resolve a viewpoint. Disabling fog provides full map and unit intel while keeping that country's battle plans, allegiance, silhouettes and shared-vision relationships.

Shared vision requires a proven relationship or the viewer's own country. The relationship calculation applies a war veto to faction, subject and overlord sharing. An incomplete relation or side census can still prove a positive from rows already held. A negative requires the whole relevant census. The engaged flag comes from its own recording index because it can lag diplomacy and also accounts for roster ownership. Naval progress reads attacking primary countries in order. A sharing country turns the result, a hostile one stops it unturned, and an undecided entry withholds orientation before later entries are considered.

The land and naval detection ladders differ. Naval detection levels map to 0, 100, 150 and 250. Land uses 0, 150 and 250 and includes the province byte. Shared vision supplies level three, and the viewing country's fixed-point modifier and saturation still apply. A land unit invading from sea takes the landing province's intel without moving the unit there. Full counters start at 250, partial counters start at 150, and a lower byte excludes the unit from counter buckets. Province-anchored objects use the province threshold of 50 unless explicitly always shown.

The known-intel readers leave unread values missing. The display falls back to fog-off rendering for stacks it cannot judge and reports the limiting input. Missing province bytes affect land visibility and fog drawing, while the naval ladder can still use its own inputs. A truncated unit census can prove a recorded detection entry and leaves an unlisted foreign unit unknown. Own units remain visible. The viewer takes the first matching recorded unit detection entry and interprets foreign air and navy intel from its own slice. Air-base admission accepts proven access, province visibility or intel. An unresolved operand leaves an otherwise negative result undecided. Carrier estimates seed the count from the clock, viewer and base independently of the controller whose intel is tested.

Vision is memoized by branch, hour, choice and identities of the required input tables. A missing current input withholds the corresponding result. A decoder certificate epoch can change while its immutable allocation is retained. The incremental rebuild shares unchanged province bytes and replays contributions only for dirty provinces, preserving saturation order. The materialized flat value map is available to diagnostics alongside the root and changed-key readers used by display consumers.

Naval count text takes the visibility and jitter seed from the first grouped task force. Complete ship and capital sums include partly visible members. Below the count-intel threshold, the text is `?` without reading the sums. Otherwise, an unread required sum stays missing. Wrapping signed arithmetic and a deterministic seed hash produce the original bounds, including negative or reversed results. The definition loader clamps count ranges. Naval base detail uses the highest reached of the five authored thresholds. Mine visibility reads the recorded feature bit for the relevant DLC. A complete naval dominance ledger permits omitted regions to have zero sources, and independent region failures retain their labels.

Port strike tooltips load score, estimate width and likelihood independently. Exact intel needs no estimates, and early status arms need none of those operands. The score coefficients preserve their authored order and values. The screening ratio clamps separately, and likelihood thresholds clamp individually while retaining their original order.
