# 0401. Witness air-loss events around the original writer

Midnight differences can recover aggregate losses if both advances are witnessed. They cannot distinguish event context because several families' cause fields overlap. The observer captures the hour, region, family, cause and opponent around the original synchronous writer. Tapping its direct calls keeps the writer's prologue and system unwind metadata intact. The observer reads state and compares the before and after values. It leaves the original writer in charge of updating them.

Read family selection in its native predicate order. The recorded event retains the original raw cause and whether the losses are own or credited. Family zero is the simulation's fall-through for a readable unknown family. The two selected words are family and family-plus-cause. A zero cause targets the same word twice. A credited accident or a null region produces no field write. The opponent is resolved from the opposite tag argument, using the signed-positive gate. A missing history is a known zero only immediately before this writer, because the constructor initializes the record before the first add. Afterward an allocated history must authenticate in full.

When a history already exists, reread its window's current slot and record pointer as the witness that the same record is still open. The original writer can touch regional combat data but does not reassign the existing history or its record. A changed cursor or record refuses the event. Read the two selected words in order, preserving the first failed reason, then compare their actual signed increments with the expected writes. A saturated bookkeeping counter does not establish full inventory removal. Removing aircraft may exhaust the variant bank before the requested count, and airbase bombing can remove aircraft without invoking this writer when no attacking wing exists.

The healthy path gathers the serial operands in guarded batches and uses each value in the original check order. A failed read or authentication falls back once to that original serial path, including the case where the writer would allocate a new history. A one-shot read failure therefore does not make acquisition stricter than the serial path. The synthetic tests check the selected spans, order and agreement with the serial path. Those tests alone establish neither agreement with live saves nor aggregate gameplay cost.

The daily roster comes from the retained signed slots of the actual air pass. The reader does not reconstruct eligibility from diplomacy after the fact. The midnight advance finalizes the previous daily record, and load, attach and partial-day membership can leave earlier records without a supported date. Queue sizing visits every country's wing-vector headers, even when absent from the daily pass. The count describes storage demand and does not establish event coverage.

The `air_loss_live_memory_five_thousand_country_cost` fixture owns all pages its synthetic pointers target. It keeps the arena allocated and immutable throughout the guarded reads.
