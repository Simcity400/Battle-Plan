# Keep supply footer precision separate from movement inputs

Supply footers retain exact demand counts and preserve every distributed origin in order, including duplicates and nonpositive amounts. An unreadable origins list leaves the scalar footer usable. An observed empty list supplies zero origins. The disruption operand comes from the last native aggregation member. The game prints amounts at two decimals with half-away rounding. The codec keeps one signed step for a smaller nonzero amount when the original sign decides whether a line is present. The owner accepted differences at display boundaries when aggregating rounded members, including an ordering tie or a cutoff in the printed hub list. Supply ratios and hub tables cannot regenerate this footer. Legacy format 101 stores unscaled words.

Division footers encode received amounts as wrapping shortfalls in native display steps. Fully supplied amounts then keep zero while demand drifts. The origins option tag distinguishes actual received steps from shortfalls with the same field width. Tags zero and one hold the old received layout, and two and three hold the current shortfall layout. Early captures with the same outer format can still decode. Older readers refuse an unrecognized tag. Formats before 99 and empty provisional format-102 payloads both signify historical absence. Counted scalar and origins failures retain independent availability. The slot-12 sample can carry off-slot, with a fresh read on first occurrence or a full refresh. Ratio and grace remain hourly.

The counter hover prints the effective supply as the larger of the received ratio and the stored-grace share. For a positive whole number of grace hours, capture quantizes the displayed `supply_grace_remaining` to a multiple that preserves its whole-percent share, preserving the native sentinel and using the base wire step for unknown or nonpositive grace, negative remaining time or wrapping cases. Current captures always populate `supply_ratio_exact` and `supply_grace_exact` (leaving rounded `supply_ratio` unset and `supply_grace_remaining` zeroed to avoid duplication, and deduplicating `StatusInputs` on the exact operands), so counter thresholds, derived `SupplyHealth`, attrition, and movement ETA all decide off-grid mod thresholds exactly even at zero out-of-supply days. Exact entrenchment, attrition threshold, minimum grace gate and raw derived-product operands remain separate from presentation steps. A maximum strength or organization shown to one decimal keeps a nonzero minimum step, while movement's low-organization decision keeps both exact operands when reached. Preserve a refusal of that decision independently of a proved unreached branch.

Historical recordings that omitted `supply_ratio_exact` and `supply_grace_exact` at zero out-of-supply days remain playable through exact display-step preimage intervals: decisions proved across the whole interval stand, while intervals straddling an off-grid mod threshold withhold the undecided counter glyph, derived count, or ETA rather than guessing. Published `SupplyHealth` records its derived writer version in the container so version-1 files (computed from rounded display ratios) are identified and withheld on playback rather than reporting rounded counts as definitive.
