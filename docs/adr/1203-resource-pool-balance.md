# 1203. Keep resource pools, counterparties and the game net distinct

Supply consists of domestic output, receipts from subjects and imports. Demand covers special projects, production lines, exports and amounts sent to the overlord. The headline net is the game's own surplus or deficit, which deducts production lines from what remains after special projects. Keep the recorded signed contributions for the balance and display demand as positive needs in its own group. Supply and demand bar length sums absolute widths, so a negative component still draws. In a net breakdown, keep the game rounding remainder as its own contribution. Use recorded fixed-point amounts and name unavailable live cells with their reason.

The bars use the product's ranking order and paint amounts and missing notices from live cells. Time series come from declared tiles, while chip values read head live cells. One resource selection can name several declarations of the same token. Count Automatic as one choice, and check how many declarations actually draw separately. Resolve repeated token names with a numbered suffix. Before a choice has been made, default to the resource with the longest campaign bar. The drill uses its own product and clock or pin, including for deals and displayed pools.

The recorded counterparties remain reachable past a fixed viewport height. Name only mounted deal rows, and calculate totals and scale from all deals. Notice, delivery count and amount may have different recorded coverage. Unknown delivery prevents a confirmed total. Tooltips show the largest deals that fit and group the rest in a remainder. A refusal to read deals means neither named partners nor an inferred remainder. Keep every shown resource's net in the fallback for a cramped tooltip.
