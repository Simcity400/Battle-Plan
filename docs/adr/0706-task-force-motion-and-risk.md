# 0706. Keep task-force motion, mission risk and stored presentation inputs independent

The fleet traversal authenticates a task force by its actual primary and secondary identities and full registered reference. The reference's type word is distinct from its class. Authenticated fleet membership supplies the task's location, ship roster and inherited unit fields. The task's raw roster owner is an operand, so the walk does not invent an additional owner gate. Repair links, the saved detached mission and the activity marker control detached presentation. Received-supply ratio and attached headquarters remain separate operands for low-supply and combat presentation.

The task-force counter walk reuses the movement and route reader shared with railway guns. The legacy movement prefix retains its ordered failure, route capacity and signed combat-count attempt. A successfully reached header can supply the independent risk query even if motion or path capture failed. A prefix that stopped before that header may leave an independent demand to read the word once. A failed original attempt is not retried as a different read. An independently demanded receiver originates in its authenticated native ledger and can contribute risk operands without a combat gate. Its producer deduplicates acquisition by full receiver handle, while preserving each raw ledger occurrence. Outside-census receivers do not change the old motion census or route accounting. Every native pointer loan lasts only through the current capture.

Every reached task force independently authenticates its embedded mission and backlink before publishing mission operands, including when its row would be omitted for capacity. A nonnull bombardment or accessible region must be the actual region at its registered ID. Null inputs stay zero. The mission view preserves the ordered, first-occurrence region choices from the original accessibility list. A mission with no current or accessible region needs no row. Bad data refuses the dependent table, while short row or path storage truncates by complete rows and records native demand. Cached name, port mission and the supply footer acquire their own optional data after the established prefix and retain independent refusal.

Fresh ETA acquires stored speed, range, fuel and common modifier operands from the task force's actual location in the same authenticated hour. The playback search supplies the departure terrain and content scale. The reader neither refreshes abilities nor asks presentation state. Its key guard applies once to the modifier-derived values. Native stat and simulation leaves remain independent of that refusal. Speed and range use the fuel-deficit terms only on the reached branch, sharing the current fuel read. An absent terrain uses the loaded ocean fallback. Exile and strategic redeployment remain distinct stored fields, and the latter is not reconstructed from a guessed save rule.

One shared modifier graph serves the sorted key requests of ETA, own mission risk and independent ledger contributions. It shares failure and root observations while retaining every ship and headquarters occurrence in native order. The original ordered ship census can loan the class and model attempts it already made, including refusals. A completed pointer page supplies exact fields in their original order. The incomplete suffix falls back at its original admission points. An authenticated empty ships vector is a known empty contributor list. A failed or unobserved header leaves that list unknown. An ordinary task-force statistic is independent of its graph. The spotting relation selects the original roster country and actual-location controller, including the native slot-zero fallback when the readable country reference is zero or unlinked.

Location and strategic-region values are held only for a witnessed world. Fleet leader data can carry while both the fleet binding and leader lifetime witnesses hold. An accumulator census is held by receiver, accumulator and exact sorted keys, and can be reused across captures only under the same certificate taken before serving or reading it. Every used certified census is logged under that certificate. Selectors and the shared success or failure observations of roots remain capture-local. Modifier registration faults and current-hour refusal do not become numerical zero.

The cached name reader copies the exact retained bytes after revalidating the secondary and primary identities and full handle. An empty copied name is success. Sizing authenticates its boundaries without copying heap payload. Port tooltips independently read stored searcher occurrences, target references and ordered ship-type strings, with their own byte and record arena cursors. Searcher removal can reorder the tail. Unreadable slots and known null, stale or live targets remain distinct. The liveness operand uses the native secondary-reference predicate, without adding a task-force class requirement. It can skip later groups only when the corresponding earlier condition is known. Sizing still admits the native demand of those groups so a subsequent capture can copy it. Port reference memo entries retain their first outcomes through a grow and clear every transient pointer on retirement.

The tests compare gathered data and copy failures with the original scalar reads and check that failed motion can coexist with complete risk or supply. They exercise cache resets, class and owner changes, repeated native occurrences, independent range and speed outcomes, boundary faults, and input reused under unchanged and renewed certificates. The mission tests authenticate its identity even when storage cannot admit a row.
