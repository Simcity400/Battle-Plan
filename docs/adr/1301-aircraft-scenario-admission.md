# 1301. Reconstruct regional air scenarios from recorded mission inputs

Visit regions in ascending order, then the viewing country's wings and the two recorded participant lists. Honor native registration order and repeated country membership. Incomplete relation vectors cannot prove the first-occurrence order, so only the viewer's own wings are eligible. Only its initial grouping call gets a scout array. Mission, counter and regional gates precede target scans. Training can fly without an active regional mission, and a transfer whose override resolves does not use that base for a scenario.

Group bombers, fighters, base equipment, transports and scouts into the game's fixed arrays. A direct launch mission bypasses its local cap after the side-wide gate, while an earlier overlapping bomber definition still uses the cap. The scheduler checks an exact kind and outcome before the whole wing batch, allowing the batch to cross the limit. Content controls division factors and formation thresholds. Positive counts ordinarily yield at least one unit. A family with a nonpositive step refuses by name because it cannot progress. A nonpositive scenario limit still admits one batch. Independent bombers use their own counts. CAS eligibility uses the raw division and diversion erases the chosen mission without reordering its neighbors. An available fighter can escort more than one batch.

Paired outcomes use the first witnessed history window. A captured empty window is zero, while a missing acquisition witness cannot decide outcomes or the post-dogfight arrays used by transport. Native subtraction, signed wrap and rounding affect threshold comparisons. Select wing pairs by replacement before reducing counts so a failed gate leaves the order used by transport. Patrol and bomber passes use different arm thresholds and may stop at different scope levels. Group admission and sortie dependencies should be resolved before a model lookup.

Only the four initial bomber and fighter arrays gate training. The pass admits the first eligible pool members in pool order, includes potentially admitted but unresolved bases in its diagnosis, and uses the pool owner despite country overrides. It selects from the eligible set with a reproducible presentation stream. It uses the transport count factor and the authored fighter patrol steps, and exits a region when the selected wing produces fewer units than one step or its batch cannot be admitted.

Unpaired naval patrols use the first task force fighting in the mission region and the viewed country's first-window losses and sinks. Sinks plus losses choose the combat province with success. Losses without sinks choose another reached sea province when possible. Other cases return to the region's ordinary draw. Ordinary location selection deduplicates provinces in first-listing order, while region counts preserve repeated known entries and omit absent IDs with a warning. Draws and random seeds are presentation state. The recording retains the inputs for admission and outcomes, so unlike the native wall-clock seed the viewer's replacements are reproducible across seek and play.
