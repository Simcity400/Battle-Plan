# Invalidate each country input only for the writers it depends on

The existing country-input observer assigns writer marks by affected field. Modifier recalculation reaches stability, research, counters and visibility, while the technology row remains independent. Only interception statistics enter the recorded stability inputs. Cosmetic tag writers mark appearance, and division writers mark counters. Every counter writer also marks unit speed. MIO policy reload has no specific owner and moves the counter and unit-speed epochs. Scientist identity and trait inputs reach research. Party leader changes reach appearance, and diplomacy reset reaches stability, appearance and counters through their existing forwarders. An unresolved positive owner revokes the affected epoch. An operation that leaves an unowned state unowned supplies no owner to mark.

Technology-level changes mark the relevant faction members for shared research inputs and only the source country for its technology rows. The mark covers the original operation and refreshes faction membership on return. A country outside a faction has only its own mark. Slot synchronization, deferred unlisted leader removal, and faction sharing-base refresh compare the reached fields and mark changed owners. An unreadable comparison operand conservatively keeps the writer active. The effect progress path marks its country's technology progress, and ordinary daily and assignment updates follow their existing acquisition paths.

Subject rebuilding identifies the overlord again on return. A new positive but unresolved overlord revokes appearance carry, and a country left without an overlord still receives its own mark. A state owner change affects both owners' drifting research, focus and project inputs because an owner may have gained and lost a state between daily checks. If either positive owner cannot resolve, all affected drifting families read afresh. An individual set requires its own patch and the forwarders of its writers. Unrelated sets can remain observed. Weather writers outside the hourly window count entry and return and force a full weather refresh after overlap; their marks carry no country field.

The console growth path and forwarded country reset both affect intel-network carry. A growth retains national payloads, and a reset uses its already-counted invalidation instead of adding a separate lifetime counter. Counting the reset requires the politics family's patch.
