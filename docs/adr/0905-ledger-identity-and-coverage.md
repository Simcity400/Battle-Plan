# Give event ledgers branch-local identity and explicit coverage

Air losses and building damage use branch-local increasing event ordinals. The saved game hour dates the application even if the recorder drains it later. A complete empty interval proves zero events. The first interval and intervals with lost events cannot support totals. The daily update roster covers every canonical country so observed false and no witness remain distinguishable.

Air-loss rows carry the signed requested fixed5 amount and identify whether it is an own loss or credit for an enemy loss. The game's daily counters can book more than the actual removals when a wing pool is exhausted. One write increments the family base and the cause-specific counter, including both contributions when the cause is zero. The recorded physical daily cursor identifies the affected slot across midnight. Resolve country references at the event frame and regions through that recording's inventory. Refuse saturation to avoid presenting a known wrong total.

Building damage retains the source class and the native cause separately. An absent wing admits an event with no attacker. With a wing, use its effective country. Use the location controller as the victim and retain a null country when the controller is unknown. Preserve the distinction between province structures and state structures, including a known absent state for a province building. The signed health difference and credited budget may differ after modifiers and clamps. A health gain is a negative removal. Playback resolves type tokens from the recording.

Equipment loss causes and division deletion dispositions are separate stable values. Scripted division and national reductions keep separate labels. Temporary volunteer or exile transfers have no loss cause and remain active until reappearance or deletion. Gameplay deletion records can have no lost equipment after returns, merges or HQ transfers. Rejected spawns, false intelligence and load or teardown cleanup provide no gameplay witness. A complete deletion interval lets playback compare its census against authoritative disappearance witnesses. The division identity is a full handle, and the expeditionary owner is captured before deletion clears it.

Consumers begin counting at the first complete equipment interval. The initial partial interval intentionally contributes no count, while a later unobserved interval withholds totals. Historical recordings distinguish that startup case using the exact stored reason until the format and consumers support a typed witness.
