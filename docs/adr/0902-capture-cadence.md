# Match capture cadence to game writers and replay consumers

A table stays hourly when a game writer can change its rows before the next daily tick or when an hourly capture drains its hook log. Moving a table to a slower cadence requires either evidence that every writer follows it or a maintainer decision accepting stale panel values. The capture cadence match and its tests enforce the classification. The writer evidence used by the tests already lives in [the cadence audit](../../crates/replay-data/cadence-audit.md).

Daily captures can take a complete table at a fixed slot or spread the work over native country ID modulo 24. Slicing keeps the highest-cost walks from clustering in one hour. It records each owner's actual read hour in SliceReads and merges the fresh rows into the held table. A full hour reads every owner. For families with complete writer observations, changed and new owners are added to that hour's work. Where observation lacks a continuity certificate the collector reads afresh. Some families run through the sliced merge with no periodic owner slice and refresh from the owner census and certified marks only.

Static data is re-read at branch start, load, continuity gap or forced refresh. Static covers content with no runtime writer, including lexical tokens and air-sortie modifier metadata, strategic-region weights calculated at map load, and registered modifier definitions. The collector rereads a modifier database if its pointer or count changes, and rereads the lexer when its maximum token changes. Off-slot hours record the skip reason and carry the held rows forward. Keyframes encode the rows already held. Retired tables are excluded from the current capture. To interpret a previous format, apply the earliest cadence change after that format rather than the present schedule.

Maintainers accepted up to a day of staleness for advisor pools used by panels and for decorative supply nodes and train inventory. AdvisorTokens and appointed advisors remain hourly because triggers read them. Supply nodes and inventory share the same owner slice so the node rows and their count agree. The supply map and railways still use hourly ProvinceSupply. The October 21 decision also allows once-daily samples for commander trait experience, residual commander XP, the two numeric CombatLogObservers columns, and the DivisionStatus supply footer, stored XP and officer XP. Their other columns and membership remain hourly, including division supply ratio, its exact ratio and grace, and the exercise decision. New rows are read fresh and full or due boundaries revoke previous carry. The three whole-roster reads land at different nonzero slots. State building input panels take the shared slot and energy operands daily. Division equipment used to select the 3D model catches up at its daily read, and a newly deployed division uses hourly NewDivisionEquipment until then. In contrast, local sub-unit clones and division templates keep their hourly contracts because unlocks, edits and trigger consumers use them between ticks.

Research inputs, technologies, focus and projects combine an owner schedule with same-hour writer marks. Research observes assignment, roster and bonus changes so script conditions reflect them within the hour. Daily drift concerns owners with states, with a fresh read on a transition into or out of ownership. Technology definitions follow the union of the rosters; reloading definitions marks every owner. The daily focus and project ticks occur inside the update entered at hour 23, so state-owning countries are sampled at the next hour. Focus choice, progress and completed membership, and project mutations and rewards, also refresh in the hour they change. Panel-only project programmes and breakthrough points keep their separate daily slot.

Air relations, air patrol histories, stability breakdowns and naval mission modifiers use writer observations to refresh affected owners while keeping the ordinary copies sliced. Relation queries must see changes to opinions and the serializable relation vector, including replacements forwarded from other observers. Combat-log equipment needs its current and previous month during ordinary reads, with all months on a full or sufficiently stale read. A log about to be deleted by the daily prune gets a last read. Registration order for naval missions and task forces stays hourly because a remove-and-append can change surviving order. Native result events and awarded experience are drained at the next boundary and the awards belong to the interval just sealed.

Raid launch inputs, essential archetype stock, convoy reservations, ace range and retreat bans are hour-sensitive. Essential stock is sampled directly because equipment quantity updates are too frequent to hook. The reader keeps only archetypes named by raid requirements and caps each at the largest required whole count. Raid air sites and resource inputs refresh new, unpublished or marked owners, with a full read when marks cannot certify the state. The ordinary supply pass rewrites resource-origin route flags, so the collector adds known origins to the `StateResourceInputs` read every hour. The resource, dynamic modifier, core and country building override tables keep their existing schedules and writer marks. Trade-route owners refresh on membership or marked route changes. Province names, region access, tension and dynamic modifiers also keep hourly contracts, using held data where their writers certify it.

Whole table families share slots when one table names another's rows. Production and building output totals share their census so ended and new lines are interpreted correctly. Theatre details are daily, while the theatre roster stays hourly with the orders groups that use its order. Theatre areas stay hourly because occupation can change fog coverage. Logistics and naval dominance are sampled just after their daily update, and the viewer refuses a naval dominance sample from a prior cache epoch. Intelligence network topology comes from its daily rebuild with an extra refresh on dynamic-country reuse or console writes. Daily CountryFuel sample windows tile the preceding 24 hourly updates, and the fuel consumers are read with their status.

Perspectives, flags, ideas, persistent variables, event targets, power balance, agency presentation and other scripted operands remain hourly because user commands, effects and scope queries act between daily ticks. Variant changes can publish hourly without scanning the whole census because accepted edits are already carried by the mutation ledger, which complements the daily roster. Radar coverage is hourly because control changes can edit its groups in any tick, and measured read cost supported keeping it hourly.
