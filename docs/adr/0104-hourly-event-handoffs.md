# Seal events at the hourly boundary and commit them with the frame

Observer drains and the operation-completion and political lane seals share one worker dispatch after the country snapshot. The snapshot and the intervening preparation do not append lane events. All seals complete before the game resumes its update. Event feed preparation happens with these drains, with its seal after the governance census joins. The shared dispatch reduces worker wakes while keeping each seal's costs with its own table. The registry boundaries are charged to the land drain. When one of the preliminary drains fails, the unreached lanes retain the stopped-pool reason their former separate dispatch would have received. Lanes reached after the drains retain the worker's actual failure reason.

The game thread seals map-event coverage in constant time and hands off operation lane indices. The spool worker copies and decodes the sealed interval, including exact operation description bytes. An admitted interval must advance the admission cursors before assembly starts again. Its lane space is released once the frame is written. A map interval that can be copied but fails decoding still advances at commit and reports lost events in the next interval. A lost or short operation interval is unavailable as a batch, with native overflow demand retained for sizing. Admission state can reset after the writer loses admitted frames and their queue is empty.

Land drains carry raw words off the callback. The lightweight framing counts are spool metadata. Only decoding can certify the pairings, verdict and timestamps. The interval admits the previous sample's hour because a command ahead of the hourly update can still use it. Visibility refresh can change full reads and encoder recovery while the land interval remains continuous. A frame that never reaches the spool cannot contribute its events to a later interval. Combat terminal counters can be present without an hourly combat row. Unavailable storage loses the whole interval with a named reason, preserving the distinction from complete zero casualties. Destruction-killer observations pair by invocation so nesting and deferred deletion do not attach a result to the wrong event. The country and owner copied at entry survive changes during that invocation.

The admitted acquisition worker drains equipment removals and native naval combat/result links. The spool projects from the frame-owned rows, preserving naval merge events. Equipment removals and gains can cancel within the same owner, cause and design. Deletions preserve native order within a full division handle because a transfer can precede a second transfer or disposal in the same hour.

The failure-reason test exercises the selection in isolation because actually failing a pack mutates process-wide drain and lane state shared by other tests.
