# 0904. Separate equipment loss and transfer evidence by its native source

The land Combat Log keeps the native variant, group and reason for equipment by calendar month. Its fixed5 amount accumulates from the hour of the first write. Reasons distinguish combat loss, the three attrition reasons and combat recovery. A country's recent complete read lets the next slice inspect the two latest entries of each array. Otherwise it reads the retained list. A removed row represents expiry or removal of its log rather than a reversal of losses. Publish final rows observed at the daily prune and repeat if publication was uncertain. A retention setting below one refuses coverage because it can remove a month that still changes. The viewing-country operand and runtime feature bit explain native admission. Admission alone leaves orders-group membership and prune observation to establish coverage. Ship and convoy events have separate witnesses, and the land table supplies no air losses.

The equipment destruction drain records signed net removals over consecutive hourly samples. Negative amounts are gains or corrections. Historical amounts remain usable with their original identity even when coverage and reason were absent. Dedicated zero-key markers with a nonempty reason denote the refused supplemental writer group (`SupplementalUnavailable`) or an observed-but-uncounted startup or branch transition interval (`PartialInterval`). Look for supplemental markers even in an hour with no amount rows, and refuse a partial-interval marker combined with loss rows. A complete empty drain establishes zero only for the hooked writers. Other losses, including convoy-pool and lend-lease losses, scrapping and inter-pool transfers, have different coverage. Unknown writer causes remain counted without guessing a known cause or dropping the amount. Resolve the equipment domain for scripted stockpile fractions through recorded variant, content and lexical identity. Planes take precedence, then ships and convoys, then land. Unresolved identity leaves the domain unknown.

Lend-lease exchange rows preserve the daily delivered and sunk cumulative totals by variant, with interval coverage ending at the last read of an exchange that disappears. The current agreement can saturate or reset its totals on amendment. Keep the actual occurrence quantities separately in `MarketEvents`, using a distinct baseline and ordered amendment and schedule records. Preserve status and ordered manifest independently from agreed price or price levels. A holder's unchanged market domain carries from its previous complete writer coverage, including empty holders. Retired auto-acceptance columns preserve earlier framing. Pending requests and accepted contracts have different kinds.

The market occurrence stream keeps changes in deliveries, loss, fuel, schedules, requests, contracts, payments and removals in their own kinds. Preserve the reached cumulative amount separately from the hour or revision delta. Difference rare final snapshots before amendment or removal. A missing amount uses the reserved refusal kind with the affected amount's mode and ordinal. Other event rows remain complete at a keyframe, and the refusal payload supplies no amount. Agreement schedules are read from agreements independently of draft-action resets.
