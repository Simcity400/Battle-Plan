# 0904. Derive regional views from the recorded memberships and scores

Naval region control is recomputed when any relationship or supremacy input changes. Sum friendly currents over the viewing country's family and enemy currents over countries at war with it. Retain its own current and the pre-floor share inputs so the display can explain its minimum and control threshold from recorded content. A region can provide a common fallback row for viewers without a personal row. All-zero values apply only to a complete table with no applicable row. Refused control and share operands carry their own reasons. An unresolved owner contributing a nonzero current can withhold the viewer's own value.

Air region mission counts preserve registration order. A positive signed mask adds the wing's whole planes to each selected bit, including unnamed bits and zero planes. A negative accumulated count restarts at zero and later additions wrap in native width. A mission with no selected wing keeps unknown, while a complete region with no owner row has no registered wing. Separate projections record actual wing lists by registered region, mission region and air-theatre membership, with distinct coverage.

Daily air score summaries accumulate the last reading of each earlier game day. Keep only changes in current composition and let intervening quiet days take the same value. Owner rows preserve each raw holder and its scores by region. A negative score or an entry that has gone contributes zero. The all-region summary combines owners, while the sides comparison applies its recorded group inputs before taking the friendly score. Persistent rows let a later span recover the sum over its days.
