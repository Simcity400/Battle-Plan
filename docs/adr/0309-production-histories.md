# Build production histories from changes in recorded rows

Index line and output lifetimes once per run, and read their changed columns over the requested window. A raw producing country belongs to a tag through the holder's native country links. An unread stretch within a line's life breaks its steps while preserving identity. Count line appearances across the branch so line details use the same keys as the list. Final output comes from recorded ended rows at their last hour. Names prefer the latest read where the variant or organisation is defined and fall back to its last applicable hour. A live panel transports changed structure and names separately from its moving amounts.

Amounts and production cost are fixed5, while efficiency is Q15. The rate truncates after division by a positive cost. In breakdowns, per-factory gain is clamped before averaging. The active factory count scales output even when the cache lookup for the efficiency average is capped. Keep aliases distinct and occurrence numbering stable when a zero native modifier is hidden. A nonzero native modifier that scales to zero remains visible. Use the tooltip's own selection depth, including the negative depth for growth and depth one for output, refit and conversion, while the recording retains the deeper operands. Today's efficiency cache cannot establish the history of changes in assignments, capacity or production switches. Show their reconciled residual where attribution is unavailable. Licence attribution depends on importer and exporter accumulators, archetype, year and faction. It is withheld when that combined evidence is absent. Name organisations at their recorded reads and share country modifier trees across their lines until those trees change.

Names and pictures depend on the catalogue of the recorded mod stack and the time of the lookup. List a holder's lines by the run index when available, then read output, definitions and organisations and the keys needed for token spelling and state-dependent art. A cached name needs no further reads. Prefer an organisation's country-prefixed localisation key, using its script key where no name key was saved. The first declared sprite wins in this order. Try the stored sprite, the country-specific sprite, the generic one, then the content's unknown-organisation sprite. Cache drawings per source and catalogue. Their published keys include the asset and a digest of its pixels. The recorded empty policy token indicates an unselected policy.

Production spending publishes branch-wide runs so the widget can read any hour without another request. It keys a producer by the holder's raw identity and breaks its totals at each new stretch. An absent design row implies zero only while its country row and coverage establish the total. A missing cell, a missing country row, or hours past the coverage boundary are unknown. Dockyard rates use 24 hours a day. A part has zero where a known split lacks that row, and is unknown where the split is unknown. A Rocket source is named only when the recorded content identifies a unique, unambiguous production building. Unresolved equipment types keep their own keys.
