# 1106. Preserve viewer-local selection ordering and hit geometry

Selection belongs to the view and clears on a branch change. An observer-country change keeps handles as in the game. Ownership checks admit a new click and keep already-selected handles through owner changes. A complete class census can retire an absent handle even with unresolved owner links. An incomplete census cannot. Selection is ordered as well as set-valued because native tooltip dispatch and first-member queries read the insertion order. A toggle can change order while preserving membership. A click that changes neither preserves the retained identities. Grouping checks selection before owner and coarse compatibility, and the selected split places each selected unit on its own plate.

A normal click replaces the selection. Shift-click toggles plate members, while a shifted box only appends. A model click may add its stack mates if they belong to the viewing country and match the first selected unit’s kind and theatre. A refused theatre prevents mate admission and reports why. An already-selected foreign unit surviving a viewer switch does not admit its mates or fall through to another click path. A combat hit is measured in interface coordinates so it selects exactly the drawn quad after scaling. Unsupported native hit shapes block lower windows without dispatch. The state target is the state of the clicked province.

Naval fleet selection stays independent of selected task forces and uses the fleet roster even for empty fleets. The viewer’s selected fleets can mark their home bases. A port click admits the viewer’s forces at the port or with a ship on its repair queue and follows the recorded fleet roster and each fleet’s ordered task-force roster (`FleetCounterInputs`), reporting the order as unavailable when that roster is absent or incomplete. Mission selection uses the viewing navy’s ordered registrations. Current province, combat and mission execution are operands of the region grid and do not filter this click.

Selection effects are queued in the game’s callback order for newly added units. The first added division can request the viewer’s original country idle voice, with its own cooldown. Distinct effects play once per frame and a recently started effect is skipped. New units must belong to the viewer before requesting audio.
