# Invalidate map objects from their recorded operands

Aircraft scenarios share one random sequence over ordered wings, launch records across regions, and group caps and mission scans across tables. Rebuild the plan as a unit on its worker when the relevant rows or script pools change. Keep a model selection when the same variant, country and inputs still apply, and keep scenario placements when their inputs are equal. Seed the reader with the known tables and learn extra script dependencies. Only the viewing-country resolution is needed from the perspective. Scripts can compare dates, so the layer also has an hourly cadence, with a playback rate limit of ten makings a second when only the hour changes. Recapture diagnostics that include an hour. Release inputs above the object camera cutoff.

Buildings and floating harbours share authored slots. Slot creation, growth and destruction latches remain in the scene's building history. Declare the layer if either building or city rendering is enabled and the camera or its zoom target enters the corresponding height range. That prepares the objects while the camera eases. Missing CountryLinks withholds the product. Other missing operands preserve the full-hour builder's per-table limitations. For a location and building type, choose the row whose token is greatest. Report the first unnamed token in state-table then key order. Floating-harbour decisions read the controller and deployment bit, so changes to unrelated flag bits do not invalidate them.

Index fog readers by province and slot gates by the facts they used. A vision delta marks only provinces whose fog decision changed. An epoch or viewer change can require a complete read. The worker mirrors the fog bytes used by the slots, keeps them through successive jobs, and shares the unmodified building-slot chunks. It hands the scene only products made against the current bank and thresholds. A reset starts scene history cold, including when the first reset product was superseded before installation. A named upload error is required when a drawn hour cannot obtain its bank binding. A hidden layer never owes an upload for a product it did not build.

Building health has separate slot and city-sprawl effects. A health write in the same slot class can still change the city's minimum average health across positive-level rows. Keep city locations dirty while their inputs are refused. The scene's city index needs a patch only when the drawn class changes, and a complete rebuild on a reset, when crossing into or out of refusal, or when its thresholds change. Without the classification thresholds, judge each move as due. Leave the previous allocation intact when no visible class changed.

Convoy preparation maps the current perspective to the viewed country, withholding lines as soon as that identity changes or is refused. A late answer for another country cannot be published, and a restored vision starts from whole inputs. On the worker, retain route stops by route, direction and province ports and states, and ribbons by their geometric and color inputs. Defer changed rows that cannot affect the viewed country's product, and take them before the next job that needs them. Unresolved links remain in scope because they produce named report limitations. A playing or still publication polls the worker under the side lock. Blocking there can prevent the frame window from arriving. A whole settle can wait for its job outside that publication. Count the answer's hour as worker progress, and refresh diagnostics whose words depend on the hour.
