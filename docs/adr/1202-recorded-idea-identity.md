# 1202. Read idea identities at their recorded appointment and focus dates

Idea timelines get the categories and slots from recorded content. Eligibility follows the political screen, and a saved category that a mod removed displays that limitation. An advisor uses the seat recorded on its span before consulting content. Seat continued appointments before newcomers at the same hour, so a change of detail cannot move the same holder to another seat. A simultaneous holder beyond the authored seat count gets an additional lane rather than overlapping another. Dated names and portraits never borrow from another appointment. Tuple keys distinguish mod-supplied text even when it contains a separator. A defined empty name leaves the editable widget title intact.

National and continuous focuses use separate content databases. An active focus is named at the beginning of a continuous run of that order, and a dated completion uses its recorded date. An undated completion uses the start of the stretch that first shows it. Use only explicitly static text before the dated title arrives. The title, description and icons are independent and a bad artwork URL leaves that item without art without refusing the rest of the reply. Requests hold identity when the displayed item is unchanged. At playing speed, prepare artwork for the upcoming stretch and prioritize the active order and newest completions. Repeated completions can share dated text while keeping distinct disclosure state. Closing a row releases its description request but retains its last shown text for the closing transition. A new client or recording identity clears that retained text.

The current focus progress is painted between structural updates. Compare readings only when the same order was recorded on both sides of its daily update. A gap or a first read cannot supply a rate. Compute the remaining duration from exact recorded days and their most recent known daily gain. The fill and knob glide without a CSS row transition, and a row's disclosure prevents shared entrance motion from overlapping the next row. Follow keeps new completions in view.

National spirits are classified by the content's national-spirit type. A held idea that could not be classified prevents a complete empty result. An unrecorded stretch supplies neither a gain nor an ending. Starting a new country instance creates a new segment even when the tag is reused. A spirit fading out owns its exit timer, and showing a tooltip again after names arrive requires that its owner and recorded context still match. A spirit's effect targets use the hour that acquisition began.
