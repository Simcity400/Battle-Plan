# 1202. Separate fuel quantities, daily accounting and projected demand

The tank uses native quantity units, while gain and demand amounts use fixed-five units. Daily accounting retains the product of both scales so it can show every recorded digit. Widget precision changes labels only, never amounts, ordering, chart shares or totals. A receipt or projected rate for one consumer remains exact. The by-branch view distinguishes requested fuel, received fuel and a share of the request. A read with no request leaves a gap in the share series.

The day-accounting detail shows the recorded sources of gains and consumers of burn. Incoming lend-lease has its own chart. A selected recipient uses a bounded detail window and stays attached to the country instance and branch it was picked in. Current projected demand comes from the demand packs, which describe divisions, task forces and air wings and the army, fleet or air-base links selecting them. A group's aggregate establishes a total only when the readings cover every consumer in it. The demand history splits where a consumer cannot be read. While following a playing clock, the overview uses the pushed family totals and prepares the individual list for the reached read when playback stops. This avoids issuing a destructive refresh of the list on each playing hour.

The playback charts draw details from held day series and read detailed hover values at explicit days. A detail range only changes its reading windows when it reaches another window. Draw binned daily bars over their bucket width rather than placing a falsely exact maximum dot at the bucket's first hour. Where the day's strip is unavailable, use its reason without a drawn baseline crossing the text.
