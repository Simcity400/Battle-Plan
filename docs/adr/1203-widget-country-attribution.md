# 1203. Attribute widget rows to the country at the recorded hour

## Eligibility and dated labels

The election table lists a country only where the recorded game predicate confirms it holds elections. A refused eligibility read counts as unknown even if interval or schedule fields were returned. A complete read containing no schedule differs from a refusal. Missing group membership takes precedence in the empty state, followed by refused eligibility and then a proven absence of elections. Eligibility follows the underlying holder across tag changes.

Election intervals are signed months. A usable term meter requires the interval, next election and term start, with a next election after the start. The next date can still be shown when the term meter is unavailable. Track structural change hours from all run starts, the hour after each run ends, tag changes and group membership changes. Between them the rendered rows can stay in place. The countdown, term ordering and tooltip use the displayed hour. Round any positive partial day up for the countdown.

The occupied-state table resolves membership at the hour its panel is current through. Followed pushes reuse the country-name and flag requests from the last relevant identity change. A name sort needs all corresponding labels, while artwork can be requested only for mounted rows. A missing country or unavailable group membership prevents a confident match. Report that omission instead of counting the result as a complete zero.

Garrison strength is a fixed5 fraction, unlike resistance and compliance, which are already percentage units. Only cell text rounds half away from zero and narrows to a clamped integer percentage. Tooltips and sort retain their exact values. The strength bar applies the game's signed narrowing after multiplying by 100. Keep the amount, strength and threshold-driven color as live values. An unmet requirement uses an empty red bar even when strength would color it differently. No recorded threshold means no threshold-based color. The reply structure distinguishes a completely missing garrison, a known zero requirement and an unmet requirement. Assigned manpower for a known unassigned state is zero even though the reply has no corresponding live cell.

## Holder history and missing values

Occupation reserve is positive manpower held from other countries. Senders that share a tag contribute to one bar slice but retain separate tooltip entries. Keep negative entries independently from positive bar totals. A missing amount makes the total unknown. Separate an admitted holder with no entry, which is a proven zero, from a missing read. Top-N excludes known zero holders and ranks unknown amounts last. An all-country choice can still reach a zero holder. Holders and sources follow raw identity across renames. The source drill uses the tag at the displayed hour (extended through the reply's proven interval) and shows a missing-tag label when no tag is known at that hour. Gaps remain gaps and absent sources contribute zero only to admitted segments. The reserve race repaints amounts and rank from live cells, using changes to holders and source entries as render boundaries.

The occupation trend uses exact fixed5 means with a truncated division by the count of occupied states. A zero state count gives no mean. Its mix keeps a chosen holder through alias changes. The chart queries detailed totals and collaboration on a paused hover so a moving playback head cannot turn into one request per frame. Group choices keep the reference hour while the head moves.

The operative roster combines the agency census with confirmed departed operatives when the widget asks for a whole campaign. Dated membership applies to each row at the hour it is shown. A departed operative's countries are named at that operative's last recorded hour. The tenure request can stay held across its covered window. An unheld request key may be a canonical slot, which must be named at the displayed hour. Count changes such as experience or assignment progress paint without a render. A pushed row with the same set of countries keeps the name requests held, avoiding another render for data it already has.

Production licences keep parties' tags and canonical slots distinct. A drill opens by the canonical slot when known so it continues to follow the same country through a tag change. A licence with an unnamed party cannot always be filtered out safely, so the result includes an unresolved count. No group members, missing membership and no licences are different outcomes. A pinned drill requests its own day. A followed drill reads the live panel, with coarse look and membership steps and labels refreshed at recorded identity changes.

National spirit timelines render new lanes at observed changes and move the cursor with playback. A spirit already held at recording start or after a gap has an unknown acquisition date. At the end of coverage show its last observed day when the actual end is unknown. Unclassified ideas may be spirits, so an incomplete catalogue prevents a result that claims to know the full set. Use the recorded effect from the hovered or last visible acquisition and expose the same holdings to keyboard and screen-reader users.
