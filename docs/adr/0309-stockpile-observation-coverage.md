# Preserve observation coverage in stockpile queries

Read changed stock, production and logistics rows once per holder and timeline. Source page entries carry read dates without decoding every row. A daily read supplies no more than its stagger period under that holder, truncated by the next read or the end of the stretch. Older pages without a stockpile date use the production read date because both are sampled in the same daily slot. A complete logistics observation without the holder's rows is an AI-controlled observation. A missing observation leaves its values unknown. A row carrying zero retains that value. A design without a row holds no stock only in an observed, complete stock interval.

Daily military output truncates each line's fixed5 rate before summing. An unknown or nonpositive cost leaves the design's output unknown. Naval, refit and railway-gun lines cannot establish additions to a land equipment stockpile. Logistics need is the same write's available operand minus its balance, with signed wrapping and saturation limits respected. Convoys, trucks and trains can count availability outside the stockpile, so their need cannot be derived from stock alone. For ordinary types, use the corresponding stock when the recorded operand is absent. Distinguish exact zero from an unknown operand in group arithmetic. An unresolved design holding any stock withholds affected grouping totals. A logistics type with no confirmed zero-only history remains visible, including rows with unknown operands.

Keep the stockpile model by timeline, holders, included types and content. When rendering history, preserve breaks after gaps and unknown values. Draw exact pieces over an hourly view and keep first, last, minimum and maximum in each absolute grid bucket at broader scales. Name a design at the last relevant hour where its definition remains available. A design fingerprint requires a complete read of both modules and upgrades. An archetype's best design is withheld if an unknown candidate could outrank those with recorded operands. Artwork is drawn through the source's recorded catalogue and cached by the content binding.
