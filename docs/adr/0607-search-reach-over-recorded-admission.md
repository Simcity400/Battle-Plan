# Search reach over recorded movement admission

## Decision

Resolve the content graph once from province definitions, ordered neighbours and crossing operands. Keep the definition land and lake bits, raw zero-distance gates, the first matching crossing operand independent of distance, and neighbour-table absence separately from a known empty table. Land masses visit definitions in order and connect land edges other than kind 4. Terrain cost, controller, continent and the crossing trigger do not create a land-mass boundary. Debug output presents a deterministic view of the hashed lookup. The recording supplies live facts through `MoveWorld`. Canonical slot zero is the absent country and its actual sentinel status can still matter. Some gates inspect signed raw country references before resolving aliases.

Raid reach uses mode 2 for class-0 armies with hostile-node and region checks. The request precheck skips the land-mass test because it uses hostile-node checking. Both avoid and block deny region entry. Require movement admission and an existing source first. Keep retreat, exile, combat duration, commander bans and occupying-army checks separate. A defending army can demand opposing attacker locations where an attacking army does not. Use the army's ordered combat and occupant facts and fixed5 strength and organization. Country access distinguishes a directed war, stored path-friendliness, subject and faction relations, military access, naval docking rights, and occupation permission plus a common enemy. The engine's canonical-slot comparison can treat two absent country references as equal. Border-war admission uses actual orders-group membership with a combat side.

The raid advance branch uses fixed5 strength 0.1 and organization 1.0. Its move-access shortcut treats matching controller slots as sufficient after the earlier gates, with the optional gates disabled. The selected-unit implementation has separate, recorded threshold and controller-area operands. Native equivalence for those operands has not been established for the raid branch, including when a controller has multiple areas.

For this request, keep same-medium edges. Embarkation needs the origin's corresponding port and an active base unless the army can exfiltrate. Landing needs the destination's active port on the corresponding sea. Land-edge war admission tests the raw controller before comparing the countries' wars. Strait checks may need the first qualifying enemy task force in the through-sea province. Only a fleet with ships and without a route, mission or all-submarine flags qualifies. Whether it is visible matters separately. Content crossing rules keep the actual decisive trigger or country pair. Evaluate with the country scoped as `THIS` and `FROM` unset. An unrecognized condition has no rule child. Country and overlord chains and required provinces are walked in order, and a missing fact only makes a verdict unknown when a known operand cannot settle it.

Share reach for the same army key and world. The engine search has no node limit, closes per province and uses admission independent of accumulated cost. Its only cost admission gate is a raw zero checked before scaling. This permits a flood fill for reach-only queries. Search proven edges first, then paths a missing operand could permit. A known admitted path proves reach, and absence even after uncertain edges proves refusal. Otherwise report the missing fact. A missing neighbour table leaves unreached destinations open. The search can pause on an edge and resume without changing its final answers. Use undirected components of edges without a proved refusal to eliminate searches only when the component cannot reach the destination or encounter a missing neighbour table.
