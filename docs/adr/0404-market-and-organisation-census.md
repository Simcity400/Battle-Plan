# Keep market membership and organisation details independently authenticated

Purchase contract capture authenticates membership of both the owning vector and the directional vectors at the canonical country slots. The stored parties remain raw references. One read of a country's full directional vector corroborates all its owned contracts and preserves failures on unreadable slots. Even an empty owner census needs a valid global root. Delivery and cumulative efficiency remain exact signed native values. Membership scratch and native pointers end with the pass. Retirement changes holder identity before deferred cleanup, so an address cannot identify a later contract.

Market inventory, automation, grants and pricing have independent writer certificates. The writer's domain determines which holder fields are read. A failed selected read cannot publish or certify a partial destination. Initial acquisition and whole-pool replacement read the whole native pool. An ordinary item write publishes the retained destination obtained by ordered comparison. Equivalent source identities can map to different retained identities, so the reader uses the chosen destination's own identity, absolute quantity and position. Agreed terms have their own per-model prices and require authentication of the existing owning and directional membership. Native order, signed amounts, and empty text or lists remain distinct. Normal stored prices are present even when omitted by serialization.

An organisation census retains country-local array order and the organisation's full native reference. The owning roster and the stored owner link are distinct. An empty name override selects the script key and an owner-prefixed localisation key can take precedence. Nonempty sprite overrides take precedence over owner-prefixed and plain script-key candidates. History is keyed by the full variant reference and preserves occupied slot order. A variant can have completed units without a design date. Signed slot distance distinguishes empty slots and the table terminator. History validates both native geometry and the exact occupied count. A refused history does not erase an independently valid organisation row. A capacity-limited organisation census cannot attest to the histories of unread organisations.

Additional organisation operands are acquired only while that census has authenticated its objects. A visitor has an independent result and cannot start a second census. Rare rosters and stores may carry while certified. Direct effect setters can bypass adjustment helpers for policy cost, cooldown and base scalars, so the daily visit rereads those scalars. The effective policy cost applies feature eligibility and the definition's clamp. Known absence of an accumulator entry is zero, while a faulty accumulator is unknown. Content definitions for display are resolved offline.
