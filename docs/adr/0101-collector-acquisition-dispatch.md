# 0101. Advance collector dependency edges from completed owned producers

The callback owns the country lease, submits jobs to fixed preallocated slots, and settles observations in the established order. Its first burst overlaps independent work. Later ready publications may release a complete dependency fan-in before the callback collects any ticket from its prior phase. Worker-only acquisition reserves enough slots for overlapping tickets, derived from roles and part counts. Consecutive observer and event-seal jobs can share a reservation. The root and child orders and division phases reserve their own slots. Only admitted leaves can run as join helpers. Coordinators with a child wait or a retained loan stay on a worker except the `ChildFronts` path without an allocating operand.

Priority orders waiting jobs FIFO within each class. The presentation chain, variants and raid state can open their critical work ahead of the crowded census tier. Variants' speculative lanes yield to the initial presentation ranges. Start the producers that unlock other roles early, including observer boundaries, country naming, orders, naval and wing copies, division and character input censuses, ship details, and the raw gates. World and Economy run in parallel ahead of their early joins. Long independent bodies overlap the rest of the capture. A separate slack burst follows the required Naval copy and includes task-force, province, state, weather, capitulation, war and relation work. The priority ladder only governs queued work. Starting a slack job too early can still occupy a worker a dependency needs.

The shared authenticated country cache replaces repeated census and row identity reads. Walks outside those spans still use their native sources. Raid air and mission joins resolve countries directly from snapshot bytes because a raw identifier can name the reserved country that the positive-country roster omits. The wings supply missions and regional patrol history admission. Independent air statistics and base verdicts merge at the mission join, while air supply comes from the first burst. A refused mission job is withheld and opens no history part. Late merges completed inputs, and its own failure leaves already acquired plan prefixes and execution scalars. FinalInputs is led by its complete source packs and every late and history admission result.

The Orders root census opens owned ranges and plans. The stitch combines ranges into the serial census order, with its first refusal, before later passes. A root refusal or missing part carries an explicit failure. Bound fronts run after Orders joins, beside the child-front pass that takes the accepted Naval copy. That loan returns to ordinary decode on every exit. Missing evidence refuses the child group inputs without a native retry. Army icons read alternate templates from seeded census operands, and the Map model witness uses the seeded model number and acclimatization inputs. The presentation speed, movement and status passes retain the serial failure precedence. The body returns its independently owned passes and merges their timing at settlement.

Worker publication transfers owned data before waking a consumer. Batch adoption uses the parent's pre-reserved worker space. Rejected requests can park and retain their payload until the ordered join. An eligible caller-run request uses the normal providers, timing and panic boundary. Worker-only growth cannot run on the game thread. On unwind the batch releases a held burst, collects or cancels accepted work and returns parked requests, settles active publishers after closing their ledgers, then drains unadopted successors. Each request and its completion drop in separate frames. A terminal allocation failure stops additional jobs without skipping a join. Panic while dropping one result cannot interrupt the remaining drain. An Orders stitch panic is contained until its ordered join, or counted exactly once during drain when the join was skipped.

Production and construction acquire their scheduled parts, while Economy owns hourly province supply and the resource census plus its own daily operands. Production modifier slices and research jobs can run beside their parent. The projects and governance producers can release independent raw source work beside their main walks. Fingerprint or held-row stores are borrowed once by their owners and restored or invalidated on failure. Daily production walks still supply distinct table scopes. Shared unspecialized reads belong to the family's hourly anchor, including ProvinceSupply in Economy. Nested scopes account for each table's own inputs. Refused or panicked Map model, relations and presentation work preserves its named missing state.
