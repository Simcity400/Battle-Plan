# Balance of power keeps authored histories and recorded contributions

One selected country sees its history. A group or multiple countries see each active balance record once if any member matches. Membership is evaluated at the records' read hour, with unknown membership surfaced when it prevents a decision. A table row opens the first matching member's history, falling back to the first member. Sorting by members needs names for all rows, and flags are read for rows in view. Value sorts by magnitude of the lean and is labelled with its exact sign.

History follows the native holder across its aliases and excludes a different holder later using the same tag. Where a tag is not held at the chosen hour, the country view uses its last holder before that hour or its first future holder. A slot-key request identifies one holder directly. The saved balance takes precedence over the game's choice. If the game has no choice then, use its latest earlier choice, its first choice by the display limit, or the first balance the holder belonged to. The hourly detail reads the holder's tag as of the detail's hour and prefers the record the game showed when it matches the chosen balance.

The recording supplies the names, the bounds and the range and trending runs. Missing bounds stay null and a malformed history without the required runs is refused. The value chart uses the authored bounds as gridlines. An unrecorded interval separates adjacent recorded lifetimes and never supplies an end date. A known empty history is distinguished from a history with unknown hours. Following playback limits plotted spans at the clock, while the layer structure and its labels change at structural hours. A held panel paints values from their reported read hour.

The detail query is uncached and uses the recording, revision, branch, content binding and hour as its identity. An open drill pins its detail hour. The membership detail for the live view waits for a settled hour. The drill reports member contributions and derived drift sources as signed fixed-five amounts with separate totals. An unrecorded operand leaves its total unknown. The two totals remain independently sourced. The header rounds the displayed balance to two decimals.
