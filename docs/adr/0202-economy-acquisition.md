# Preserve economy outcomes across owned acquisition and decode

Economy acquisition stages raw bytes before it walks the direct resource readers. The FIFO decode worker materializes the hourly and daily rows and clears the per-hour gate. The gate survives reset during handoff. Resource definitions, pools and exchanges reach decode already owned in dependency order. They consume the enclosing frame's exact logical grants even when reusable physical storage is larger. The resource census is walked once every active hour. A capacity shortage before starting the source permits the original path. A shared source refusal belongs to that attempt and never triggers another acquisition. The worker grows buffers from the original headers and installs outcomes without live access.

The daily country and market walks share one canonical census. A failure of that census also withholds active resource consumers. Independently read province supply survives a later daily census failure. An hour when only province supply is scheduled touches no country or market source. Only active supply tables participate in a shared supply fault. The fuel status serves both fuel outputs, and its ring serves scalar fuel only. A bulk copy can serve sub-reads, while strings and similar split reads retain the exact spans the original walk asks for. A market epoch establishes its contract baseline even away from the daily fuel and purchase slot, and the carried contract route needs no live reads.

Resource definitions and hourly supply, pools and exchanges can reuse their validated baselines. Separate recovery caches receive complete daily rows. Row equality follows replay keys and values even after native enumeration changes, including signed identifiers with unsigned replay ordering and embedded zero or non-UTF8 bytes in source strings. A bounded fallback after a failed batched value read can complete on a transient failure. A persistent failure withholds that value's table as in a direct walk and preserves independently known siblings. Replaying copied bytes does not repeat a native read. The differential fixtures compare grants, actual source faults, overflow, exact fit and available empty tables against the direct walk.

The Fuel demand pack is owned at acquisition and converted once per cohort. Its country index avoids rescanning the whole military world for each owner, while full-reference ancestor chains preserve reached foreign or untagged commands. Context IDs are pass-local and attached only to reached occurrences. After scalar conversion, complete fuel reads split representable pack lists into sorted part rows, with each parent retaining its header. An unrepresentable key leaves its pack whole. A truncated read keeps the available packs whole and withholds its parts because the remaining countries are unobserved. An unavailable read propagates its own reason. Demand and award windows attach before final row comparison, and a named demand refusal leaves its known fuel prefix intact.
