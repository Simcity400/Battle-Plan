# Keep raid arrows tied to recorded movement and authored geometry

The viewing country’s raid arrows follow instance order and the replay’s raid category filter. Authored active colours apply while launched and ended. Other colours come from readiness in the preparing phase. Readiness needs the unit at the source, level-four resources from the raid’s pool including nukes, and the execution trigger gates. Missing required evidence refuses the arrow or unit by name. The offer’s own eligibility cannot stand in for this execution gate.

Arrow geometry shares the battle-plan manager’s texture, blend and pass state. Follow authored movement neighbours and permitted crossing midpoints, skip a skipped hop’s control, and wrap each control relative to the preceding one. End handles follow the adjacent segment. Type-specific width, smoothing and subdivision settings select the curve. Air and ballistic arrows control their own vertical lift while others keep terrain. A prepared model uses the first matching primary-roster variant for an equipment category. The remaining resolution order covers country-tag spelling, graphical culture, the base entity and default entity before the wing resolver. A chosen roster variant’s resolved model may itself be unavailable.

The recording supplies hourly movement from the recorded speed operands. A paused frame uses the hour’s own progress or the requested dump fraction. While playing, the display fraction interpolates movement between hours, with the game’s end and cancelled outcomes governing visibility. A carried ended unit can remain for one frame before its update reaches full progress. Units keep entity state by raid ID. A seek or new view can enter the current stage directly, while a stage reached during display uses its introduction. Animation advances on the display’s wall clock while replay is paused. The entity’s mesh LOD governs visibility rather than the province-object camera cutoff. Speed fallback, transport and ace bonuses keep their recorded operand order, and missing speed inputs remain absent.
