# Use recorded inputs for manpower and election facts

Deployed manpower comes from the recorder's sums for army, navy, required naval crews, air and exile air. The rest of the recruitment view uses the hour's recorded controlled-state roster in its original order. A missing roster or missing state remains unknown. State resource operands are local and local non-core manpower, recruitment add and recruitment factor, with a separate core operand. Country effective modifiers are mobilization speed, conscription, the conscription factor and non-core manpower. Multiply effective conscription by its factor for the target. The applied ratio uses a hundred times that fixed5 fraction and maps the engine's three non-finite words to an invalid result. Read recruitment defines from recorded content and report a refusal when they are unavailable.

Election month arithmetic preserves the offset within the month, so an interval reaching a shorter month can spill into the following month. Keep signed wrapping for dates before the epoch. An exceptional initializer can provide its own next-election cache. Otherwise compute the date from the last election and the interval. The game skips daily politics for countries that own no states. Resolve election facts and exile input rows by the country's raw aliases, and use the canonical slot for the recorded deployed sums.

A positive recorded host identifies an exile. A nonpositive host means it is not an exile and a missing host remains unknown. Count missing hosts separately from missing legitimacy, manpower, cap and leader resources. Exile air crews come from the recorder's sums of wings flying on behalf of the exile. The product reads the whole small identity tables first, then the relevant countries' rows by raw references and slot, and finally the states and modifier definitions their recorded operands name.
