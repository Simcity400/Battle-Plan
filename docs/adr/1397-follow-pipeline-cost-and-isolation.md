# 1397. Follow pipeline isolation, cost balance, and work proportional to change

The follow pipeline keeps the playback clock, the time gate, the follow stream, and whole-picture installs ([ADR 0505](0505-frame-and-drawing.md)). To support 30 visible widgets at 60 fps and 1,440 game hours per second on four slowed cores, four invariants govern correctness and cost across native, the pull worker, the drawing lanes, and the page.

## Cell-group ownership across seeks and per-subscription fault isolation

A dated panel that names live cell groups owns those cell-group subscriptions across seeks. `Facts.seek` drops old facts for every subscription without releasing the panel's ownership of its cell groups, and `Facts.prune` keeps a newly owned cell group while it has no facts yet; when a panel names a cell group again, the lane clears any prior `gone` marker for that id. Native's follow stream can therefore send changed rows for a cell group after a seek without encountering a deleted base.

A decode, merge, or missing-base fault on one subscription fails only the subscription that faulted, never the whole drawing lane. `parseFollowedFrame` validates packet framing before per-subscription decoding so a corrupt answer body isolates to its own subscription; `Lane.answer` and `Lane.panel` record failures per subscription id (clearing a panel fault only on a complete data/refused replacement, seek, or unfollow — never on `STANDS` or `WAITING` — and gating panels by current seek so stale-seek packets cannot mutate current state). `Lane.select` checks cell faults only against the active panel's cells (`Facts.activeCells`) and the selected picture's cells, and `Lane.serve` emits a failure entry even while a normal picture is still in flight so paused faults are never lost. Sibling widgets on the same lane keep serving normally; only an unrecoverable lane-level or transport-level error fails the lane.

## Panel hand-off cadence and bounded speculation

Non-exact panel rows fed through `Apart` (`crates/viewer-layer-core/src/playhead_host/views/apart.rs`) pace from `playhead.frame_period()` clamped to `[4 ms, 50 ms]` with a lead of `3 × period` clamped to `[32 ms, 64 ms]`, run at normal thread priority, sleep on a condvar whenever paused or without open readers (waking immediately on playhead activity or store reader-demand mutations, with `Store::publish` waking `Playhead::wait_for` via `wake_waiters()` without altering `stood`), and key redundant-post suppression on `(applied, epoch, demand)` without clobbering `jobs.from` when no steps arrive. The `follow panels` loop in `crates/dashboard-follow/src/host.rs` polls at `(playhead.frame_period() / 2).clamp(4 ms, 8 ms)` only while panels are shown and playback is running, and stays at one frame interval otherwise.

On the page, speculative targeting clamps the playing frame step to at most `1.5×` the expected hours per display frame so a single main-thread stall cannot throw the lane's target hundreds of hours ahead and latch the monotonic target high. The lane reply-delay EWMA is bounded to `[1, 4]` frames, and early entries wait in a bounded per-widget sorted queue (up to 4 entries) where a newer entry only displaces an older entry whose start it covers (`newer.lo <= older.lo && newer.hi >= older.lo`) so open-ended windows like `[120, ∞]` and `[144, ∞]` coexist until their hours arrive. Unclosed early `open: 'followed'` entries keep tracking shared confirmations on install, while entries closed while parked freeze at the close-time confirmation.

## Work proportional to change

Every stage of the pipeline scales with what changed rather than with total mounted widgets or total table rows:

- **Changed rows (`ui/src/dashboard/follow/tables.ts`, `fields.ts`)**: `patchAnswer` merges sorted changed rows by galloping/binary key lookup onto persistent 8-row chunks (`RowPatches`), copying at most `8 × columns` cells per changed row without mutating the base or retaining stale texts from overwritten rows. Worker-drawn consumers read directly from the chunked view; when a main-thread `answer:` field materializes a flat wire `Answer` via `serializeAnswer`, `fieldsOf` collapses `head.value.answer` onto that compacted buffer so the worker immediately drops the older base and patch tree.
- **Lane split (`ui/src/dashboard/follow/split.ts`)**: Records directed to a single lane index directly into that lane's buffer in O(1) without scanning other lanes or allocating empty buffers for idle lanes.
- **Page & Pipeline (`page.ts`, `pipeline.ts`)**: Quiet clock ticks update a shared route confirmation box for open followed widgets without scanning them, carrying `(route, channel, hour, seek, reset)` snapshotted before `crop()` so stale-generation or mid-crop confirmations never leak across resets or changed records. Bounded widgets are skipped while the target remains inside `[safeLo, safeHi]`, only widgets with queued early entries are touched, and `flush()` notifies only subscribed `dateWatchers`.
- **Drawing lane (`lane.ts`)**: `serve()` indexes widgets by subscription, route, dirty state, active motion, in-flight status, inspection, and a conservative safe window `[windowSafeLo, windowSafeHi]` over unpinned finite-window widgets (with `Facts.nextStart` including future cell-group starts), visiting zero widgets on quiet frames and redating confirmation-only advances (`redateEntry`) without repainting. `prune()` schedules future prunes in `prunableAt` from the earliest next-prunable hour returned by `Facts.prune()` so subscriptions holding a fallback fact perform zero work on quiet frames.

## Cost-based lane placement, non-starving budget packing, and no live migration

Each lane reports measured smoothed paint costs back to the pipeline on `served` messages for painted widgets. `DashboardGrid` supplies `FollowedWidgetKind` (and `headProduct` passes `kind` explicitly) so `Pipeline` tracks per-lane cost load and per-kind fallback cost by real widget kind rather than instance ID, placing new and remounted widgets on the lowest-cost lane (breaking ties by widget count and keeping copies of one kind together only while within cost share). Inside `Lane.serve()`, if the next due widget's estimated cost exceeds the remaining frame budget while budget remains, the lane continues past it so cheaper due widgets pack into the remaining budget instead of starving behind one heavy widget, any widget exceeding the frame budget advances its `served` turn proportionally (capped at 4 frames), and any widget deferred 3 times is promoted ahead of normal priority so oversized motion widgets cannot starve behind continually changing cheap widgets.

Live migration of an already-mounted widget between lanes is intentionally not performed. Three pieces of state are bound to the widget's lane for the lifetime of a mount: (1) subscription ids encode their lane (`id = n × lanes + lane`), (2) native allocates a panel's live cell-group subscriptions in its owner's lane class (`cells_id(lane, lanes)`), and (3) worker-local `widget.state` owns `OffscreenCanvas` handles, decoded `ImageBitmap` icon caches, and table/race layout state that cannot be moved atomically across workers without tearing down and re-registering the widget. Cost-based placement at mount, re-placement on remount, and non-starving budget packing achieve lane balance without breaking whole-picture continuity.

## Playback-aware idle renderer preloading

Idle renderer prefetching ([ADR 1203](1203-widget-async-paint.md)) pauses whenever any playback clock is playing and resumes when playback pauses, while on-demand `loadWidgetRenderer(kind)` always loads immediately. It uses `requestIdleCallback` without a forced timeout, requires `!deadline.didTimeout && deadline.timeRemaining() >= 6` before starting a chunk, and serializes `<link rel="modulepreload">` tags from the Vite-emitted `meta[name="bp-widget-chunks"]` manifest by awaiting each link's `load`/`error` event before scheduling the next chunk or resolving `whenPreloadComplete()`.
