# 1397. Follow pipeline isolation, cost balance, and work proportional to change

The follow pipeline keeps the playback clock, the time gate, the follow stream, and whole-picture installs ([ADR 0505](0505-frame-and-drawing.md)). To support 30 visible widgets at 60 fps and 1,440 game hours per second on four slowed cores, four invariants govern correctness and cost across native, the pull worker, the drawing lanes, and the page.

## Cell-group ownership across seeks and per-subscription fault isolation

A dated panel that names live cell groups owns those cell-group subscriptions across seeks. `Facts` tracks the latest native panel's cell groups (`nativeCells`) separately from `carried` facts, updating them only when a new panel replaces them or the subscription unfollows. `Facts.seek` drops old facts without releasing `nativeCells` (and never carries a failed picture), and `Facts.prune` retains `nativeCells` even when rewinding into an earlier carried panel; on seek or route bind, `Lane` replays queued future-seek panels before follows and drains held cell answers afterward so early cell answers never wait for an unrelated packet. Native's follow stream can therefore send changed rows for a reused cell group after a seek without encountering a deleted base.

A decode, merge, malformed live-section, or missing-base fault on one subscription fails only the affected subscription, never the whole drawing lane, and is modeled as **sticky subscription status** rather than a lane picture. When the lane cannot decode or merge any record belonging to a subscription (followed table, panel, panel's live section, or a cell group owned by that subscription), `Lane.failSub` drops that subscription's facts, seeds, carried pictures, and held records, adds the subscription id (never a cell-group id) to `failedSubs` so every later record for it is discarded on arrival with credit returned across seeks, and posts one `{ type: 'sub-failed', sub, reason }` message to the main thread. For a cell-group fault, `Lane.failCell` fails every subscription currently owning that cell and releases the cell group so a reused cell id never inherits an earlier subscription's fault. On the main thread, `Pipeline` handles `sub-failed` through its existing subscription-refusal path (marking the subscription refused, unfollowing natively, and calling `Page.name(widget, 'failed', reason)` on every widget using it), keeping the named failure sticky across seeks and surface remounts until the subscription is released or the pipeline is restarted (a user-facing Retry control lands with PR #282). Drawing or `crop()` faults for one widget follow the same status path via `{ type: 'widget-failed', widget, reason }`, halting only that widget on the lane without emitting failure pictures or affecting sibling widgets, and clearing on surface remount. Future-seek packets keep only the newest future seek the lane has heard of per native route (older future seeks are superseded and dropped silently with credit returned), bounded by `earlyBuffer` (with one ordered `earlyPanels` queue that coalesces only adjacent completion-only packets of the same `(native, seek)`); if the newest future seek overflows, entering that seek fails its subscriptions through the same `failSub` path. Sibling widgets on the same lane keep serving normally; only an unrecoverable lane-level or transport-level error fails the lane.

## Panel hand-off cadence, paused-idle waits, and bounded speculation

Non-exact panel rows fed through `Apart` (`crates/viewer-layer-core/src/playhead_host/views/apart.rs`) pace from `playhead.frame_period()` clamped to `[4 ms, 50 ms]` with a lead of `3 × period` clamped to `[32 ms, 64 ms]`, run at normal thread priority, and key redundant-post suppression on `(applied, epoch, demand, mirror_gen)` so a join supplying missing tables at the same `(hour, epoch)` always re-posts. `Playhead` splits control notifications (`notify_control`, bumping `control_seq` and `activity` and waking both `watch` and `watch_control`) from publication notifications (`notify_published`, bumping only `activity` and waking only `watch`), firing only after all playhead locks (including `clock`) are released—including in `Consumer::take_from` when `clock.shown` clears a playing seek's hold. While playing, `Apart` checks its display deadline first and steps at most once per period unless `control_seq` or reader `demand` changed (ignoring unrelated publications for other readers). `follow panels` (`crates/dashboard-follow/src/host.rs`) subscribes via `watch_control` plus `PanelRows::on_publish` and host mutations, gating `evaluate()` on relevant work or its display poll deadline; `follow driver` keeps `push.head` advancing while playing for non-exact `shown` panels as well as products and follows; and `Rows::Listed` log reads use `LogReads::rows_notifying` to invoke `rehooker` upon completion so paused final-hour subscriptions run `Engine::fill` without a 60 Hz poll. `Apart`, `follow driver`, and `follow panels` block indefinitely on condvars when paused or idle.

On the page, speculative targeting clamps the playing frame step to at most `1.5×` the expected hours per display frame so a single main-thread stall cannot throw the lane's target hundreds of hours ahead and latch the monotonic target high. The lane reply-delay EWMA is bounded to `[1, 4]` frames, and early entries wait in a bounded per-widget sorted queue (up to 4 entries) where a newer entry only displaces an older entry whose start it covers (`newer.lo <= older.lo && newer.hi >= older.lo`) so open-ended windows like `[120, ∞]` and `[144, ∞]` coexist until their hours arrive. Unclosed early `open: 'followed'` and `open: 'panel'` entries keep tracking shared confirmations on install, while entries closed while parked freeze at the close-time confirmation.

## Work proportional to change

Every stage of the pipeline scales with what changed rather than with total mounted widgets or total table rows:

- **Changed rows (`ui/src/dashboard/follow/tables.ts`, `fields.ts`)**: `patchAnswer` merges sorted changed rows by galloping/binary key lookup onto an 8-way persistent radix tree of 8-row chunks (`RowPatches`), copying at most `8 × columns` cells and `O(log_8 N)` index slots per changed row without mutating the base or retaining stale texts from overwritten rows. Worker-drawn consumers read directly from the chunked view; when a main-thread `answer:` field materializes a flat wire `Answer` via `serializeAnswer`, its bulk tail-copy fast path (when no row reasons changed) guarantees semantic equality of decoded answers rather than byte equality (it may retain unreferenced base text entries until reasons change and compact), and `fieldsOf` collapses `head.value.answer` onto that compacted buffer so the worker immediately drops the older base and patch tree.
- **Lane split (`ui/src/dashboard/follow/split.ts`)**: Records directed to a single lane index directly into that lane's buffer in O(1) without scanning other lanes or allocating empty buffers for idle lanes.
- **Page & Pipeline (`page.ts`, `pipeline.ts`)**: Quiet clock ticks update shared route confirmation boxes for open followed and open stepped-panel widgets without scanning them, carrying `(route, channel, hour, seek, reset)` snapshotted before `crop()` so stale-generation or mid-crop confirmations never leak across resets or changed records. Bounded widgets track exact exit and re-entry boundaries around `T` (including outside widgets) so quiet ticks visit zero widgets, only widgets with queued early entries are touched, and `flush()` notifies only subscribed `dateWatchers`.
- **Drawing lane (`lane.ts`, `facts.ts`)**: `Facts.panels` tracks monotonic route completions in O(1) via route-level `(completedPanel, completedSeq)`, an `openStepped` index, and `pendingConfirm` (confirming stepped panels opened or replaced since the preceding completion on an equal `COMPLETED` while doing zero visits when none opened), lazily materializing `last.c` on read (`completionVisits == 0` on monotonic `COMPLETED`). `Lane.serve()` indexes widgets by subscription, route, dirty state, active motion, in-flight status, inspection, non-open followed and open-stepped panel sets (never dirtying non-stepped panels on `COMPLETED`), and exact exit/re-entry safe windows `[windowSafeLo, windowSafeHi]` over unpinned finite-window widgets (invalidating safe bounds only when a member is actually removed from `windowedWidgets`, with `Facts.nextStart` including future cell-group starts), visiting zero widgets on quiet frames and completion-only panel steps, and redating confirmation-only advances (`redateEntry`) without repainting. `prune()` schedules future prunes in `prunableAt` from the earliest next-prunable hour returned by `Facts.prune()` so subscriptions holding a fallback fact perform zero work on quiet frames.

## Cost-based lane placement, non-starving budget packing, and no live migration

Each lane reports measured smoothed paint costs back to the pipeline on `served` messages for painted widgets. `DashboardGrid` supplies `FollowedWidgetKind` (and `headProduct` passes `kind` explicitly) so `Pipeline` tracks per-lane cost load and per-kind fallback cost by real widget kind rather than instance ID, placing new and remounted widgets on the lowest-cost lane (breaking ties by widget count and keeping copies of one kind together only while within cost share). Inside `Lane.serve()`, if the next due widget's estimated cost exceeds the remaining frame budget while budget remains, the lane continues past it so cheaper due widgets pack into the remaining budget instead of starving behind one heavy widget, any widget exceeding the frame budget advances its `served` turn proportionally (capped at 4 frames), and any widget deferred 3 times is promoted ahead of normal priority and ordered by deferral age and oldest service before motion priority so oversized motion widgets cannot starve behind continually changing data widgets.

Live migration of an already-mounted widget between lanes is intentionally not performed. Three pieces of state are bound to the widget's lane for the lifetime of a mount: (1) subscription ids encode their lane (`id = n × lanes + lane`), (2) native allocates a panel's live cell-group subscriptions in its owner's lane class (`cells_id(lane, lanes)`), and (3) worker-local `widget.state` owns `OffscreenCanvas` handles, decoded `ImageBitmap` icon caches, and table/race layout state that cannot be moved atomically across workers without tearing down and re-registering the widget. Cost-based placement at mount, re-placement on remount, and non-starving budget packing achieve lane balance without breaking whole-picture continuity.

## Playback-aware idle renderer preloading

Idle renderer prefetching ([ADR 1203](1203-widget-async-paint.md)) pauses whenever any playback clock is playing and resumes when playback pauses, while on-demand `loadWidgetRenderer(kind)` always loads immediately. It uses `requestIdleCallback` without a forced timeout, requires `!deadline.didTimeout && deadline.timeRemaining() >= 6` before starting a chunk, and serializes `<link rel="modulepreload">` tags from the Vite-emitted `meta[name="bp-widget-chunks"]` manifest by awaiting each link's `load`/`error` event before scheduling the next chunk or resolving `whenPreloadComplete()`.
