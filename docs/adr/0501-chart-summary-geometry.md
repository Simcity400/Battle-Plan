# Use bounded chart summaries for geometry and exact reads for amounts

The wire summary is decoded once per reply identity. Geometry retains the supplied hours and values, including each unbroken line's bounds and step boundaries. A retained reply can draw in a later frame while its requested width and hours remain compatible. A painter places it at the recorded hours and never stretches it or draws past the playhead. Preserve original run boundaries when every bin is one hour. Wider tiles include hours beyond the current head, so draw completed bins and clip the head bin to its own value at the current hour. An approximate extreme is dated to its bin, and dates shown for several bins span their known time bounds. [Summary decoding and fitting](../../ui/src/charts/chartSummary.ts)

Build dense shapes once per summary and step kind. Join only continuous columns, omit redundant vertices on a level run, and keep unknown or unplaceable columns as extrema dots. The shape's extrema are enough to determine placement under a monotonic axis. Locate just the visible or hit-tested vertex window, with boundary vertices to preserve its joins. Clip a small number of passes rather than repainting for every unknown span. Dense band shading uses display envelopes and exact lines use curve fill. A hit can share painted geometry but must obtain its balance from a separately fetched known amount. [Summary geometry](../../ui/src/charts/chartSummary.ts)

Join dated aliases without spanning unknown intervals. Compact stacked summaries share a bounded screen grid sampled from chronological extrema. Produce a signed stack for selected series and Other using only hours covered by all required parts. Precompute each bucket's samples and allocate band edges only for emitted screen points. Exact tooltip values come from hour queries independently of those approximations. [Compact stacks](../../ui/src/charts/chartSummary.ts)

Keep stable ordered event ordinals and exact decimal counts for dense marks. An actor identity is separate from its ordinal. A single event can keep its authored glyph, whereas a group shows its count and date range. Hit testing of a cluster requests its exact members as pages. A tiled cluster contributes only bins whose last hour the head has passed, retaining its drawn identity until that set changes. [Event mark summaries](../../ui/src/charts/markSummary.ts)

Keep display refresh bounds separate from the exact supplying interval, so a date-label refresh cannot change where the amount was read. Map recording-inclusive prepared spans to half-open drawing ranges and clip them after the head's hour. Approximate geometry keeps the same plotted values with a fade and dashed edge. A pending band marks hours that have no prepared geometry yet, with a distinct legend entry from approximate or loading data. [Playback intervals](../../ui/src/charts/playbackIntervals.ts)
