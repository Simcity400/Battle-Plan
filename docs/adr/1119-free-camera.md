# 1119. Start free camera modes on the visible ground

Choosing Walk or Fly from the map keeps the map camera and its interface until a ground click or the controller's South button over the middle of the view chooses a start. Escape cancels. Choosing one free mode while the other runs immediately takes over the ground it stands on. Leaving a free mode returns the previous map camera and cursor. The press that chose the start cannot be the first press of a double tap in the new mode. A free-mode click captures the pointer only off the presented icons and window controls. When the interface is hidden it keeps the layout from the map camera it left without drawing it. Fly still shows the country names and fades them by eye height.

To find the start, intersect the ray with the first drawn terrain triangle or water plane inside the map's row bounds and far depth, including wrapped horizontal copies. A point above the horizon or a ray entering the map from under the terrain or water chooses nothing. Within a piece of the heightfield between cell and diagonal crossings, the terrain height along the ray is linear. Solve each piece in nearest order, with bisection across a piece whose visible surface changes between ground and water. Rebase the calculation at entry into the terrain band to avoid losing height precision for a high eye. The start's horizontal coordinate is then wrapped to the first map copy. A middle-button pan moves both the shown camera and its target, keeping the pointed ground in place when orbiting. The view and target wrap together when crossing a seam.

For flight, the host chooses a content entity and the loader bakes it off the frame for the map being drawn. A library slot is valid only for the assets which produced it. After a reload the choice is baked again and its changed status is reported to the host. Without an available choice Fly moves a free eye without drawing an aircraft. The frame holds the document apart while drawing, so pass its assets to the flight path instead of reading the viewer's document slot. Flight uses the model radius, explode state and content ground definitions. An unreadable definition is named and the flight uses the drawn surface without a delayed crash animation. A window dump can pin aircraft pose and held controller-style inputs and step them by the capture clock. The host's controller route can start and fly the mode even without system window focus.
