# Keep branch pages shared and picture publication independent

Map and follow consumers share a page read by recording root digest and length. A per-revision gate coalesces concurrent loads, and weak ownership lets the read go once no consumer needs it. Each branch cuts its own timeline from the recording’s selected pieces and matching readable runs. A load or fork can overlap its parent’s hours. A branch takes only that parent’s prefix, then its own continuation. Run boundaries do not invent a contiguous interval where neither piece recorded an hour. An absent branch or one without readable pieces is refused by name.

A playhead clock runs from the first consumer to take the seek reset. Slow composition, a window with no GPU, a hidden map, and a map whose layers all refuse cannot hold other consumers. Independent layers can compose in parallel and each dependent waits for its upstream. A newer seek discards a superseded reset before it is displayed. A composing map retains its previous picture while it queues taken steps, so it cannot bottleneck a sibling map through the shared ring. A follow host also has a private read worker when no map side drives its views.

A playing frame takes the latest eligible products without blocking for every pending maker. A layer drawing an older product contributes its actual hour to the oldest-hour diagnostic. Fed layers can prepare ahead, but a publication is visible only at its hour and seek. Dependents settle from an upstream’s published version and receive accumulated changed IDs once the upstream can be read. A held or saved frame joins the required products at the exact requested hour. Makers affected by the same step are asked ahead of settling any one of them, since the requests may need to progress together. A step touching none of a maker’s declared columns does not ask it to rebuild.

The window tries to take the side lock without waiting. Capturing a stepped picture ties the answer to its request and view, including another view of the same hour. A stop or timeout refuses the waiting capture by name and does not answer a later request with the same refusal. A seek that lands during catch-up drives row demand for the replacement epoch. Camera-only movement can update placement without rebuilding declarations except when it crosses a declaration boundary or a layer explicitly reads camera inputs.
