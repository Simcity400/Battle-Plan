# Preserve production credit and settled fuel observations

Production classifies applied credit by its actual recipient and keeps conversion distinct from new build and refit. An unknown purpose does not count as new production. Rocket totals retain signed fixed5 spending and whole deliveries independently of ready inventory and site removal. A variant-zero row is a scope witness, not a design to sum. World scope establishes owner coverage and local scope also covers unclassified designs. A design under country zero has an unknown producer. Preserve known subtotals separately from exactness; loss or overflow cannot restore exactness.

Construction records daily advances as branch totals. Construction and repair exclude banked inputs and ordinary railway carry. Instant progress has no factory attribution. Count observed spend advances even where the advance or natural repair used no industry. Linked primary output includes its partner without an attributable split and the partner's own row is inexact. Carry the final totals until a written frame sees the ended line. An interval lost during observation withholds the table through the branch. Completion is a separate witness acquired before line or linked-partner removal. A railway cancellation is removal rather than completion. Signed counts cleared on completion remain distinct from levels completed, and old recordings retain their readable prefix.

Resolve equipment imagery from the variant's identity and the content stack. A kind-1 `"."` path marker queries the creator's pool with ruling ideology and graphical culture regardless of the stored active byte; an empty pool lookup produces an inactive null entry and falls through to the equipment fallback. Any other stored entry is validated and tried only when active; inactive entries of any other kind fall through to fallback. Otherwise choose the positive origin, or for a creator with no positive origin keep the incoming handle when the creator's canonical slot is static (below `ReservedCountryNaming.dynamic_tag_boundary`) and for a dynamic slot substitute `original_tag_reference` when positive or else the country object's own handle (resolving when both branches agree if `dynamic_tag_boundary` is unavailable), using the raw handle's witnessed tag spelling for tagged lookups and withholding any lookup whose handle spelling is unwitnessed. For the type and then its archetype, search tagged and general sprite names, prioritizing a capital suffix for a capital design. With exactly one enabling technology, try its documented names with the technology tag (own witnessed tag for static slots, original tag for dynamic slots, or the agreed tag when `dynamic_tag_boundary` is unavailable), culture and general roots. A different equipment family repeats the same fallback and the final pool search can omit ideology and culture. The country-specific pool search tries the raw handle's witnessed tag string, ordered faction members, the capital state's continent (`original_capital` if nonzero else `capital`) and default. Capital state continent reads the recorded `States.continent` lexical token (`0` is recorded null, failed capture is withheld), falling back on older recordings without `States.continent` to the state-template scan taking the first member province with a valid continent before stopping at the first land or lake coastal province. Within each root it tries type then archetype and most recent declarations first, filtered by the reached limit and compatible filters. Stable descending icon weight and reverse icon order determine the first existing result. A resolved absent image is distinct from an unproved lookup.

Design lineage, attribution, roles and bonuses are independent of the saved explicit model and raw version byte. A recorded version zero is valid. Preserve definition snapshots at mutation boundaries to survive repeated edits within an hour and deletion. Ordered mutations reserve sequence zero for named observation failure. Removal records a tombstone without losing preceding readable state. A value key is accepted only when any existing payload with that key is wholly equal. An author liveness report is independently current. Country inputs retain the raw creator reference even when multiple scopes resolve to one notification owner. Resolve modifier keys against recorded definitions and treat a proved absent accumulator key as known zero. Retain failed owner or modifier coverage explicitly. Separate roster, factor and consumption columns so changing one use does not rewrite the other operands. Unit bonuses preserve exact subunit versus category targets, signed width, presence and native stat and grant order, including repeated selectors and zero grant amounts.

Fuel status includes raw quantities and native ring sums, which are independent of the observed awards. A prefilled ring or loaded countdown alone cannot prove a day of fuel production. Capture sums 24 consecutive native updates ending at the supplied read. Keep the source categories and receipt ordering, convert each update's own gain before summing quantities, and keep native fixed5 or Q15 as required by the lane. A consumer's receipt identity includes the native reference, owner, host and observed name or refusal. A country/base award does not identify a wing name. Each update belongs to the interval ending at its settled sample. The fresh native source witness maps to the ring slot when the countdown was one. The current day's sum omits that oracle-only witness and individual update details; older formats retain their update-by-update window. After a gap or reset establish the first authenticated update independently of country identity. A short interval or overflow leaves the day unavailable with its reason and keeps the status prefix, current demand and other countries usable.
