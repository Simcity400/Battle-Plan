# 1306. Reconstruct weather particle history on the presentation clock

Weather is global recorded simulation state. The first nonzero weather flag in the game's priority order selects a region's wanted kind. Each anchor's pair uses its authored member descriptors, with the small bank preferred only when present. The content stack resolves authored effect objects, named blocks and filenames. Weather selects the last matching particle block and named entity objects the first. Refused pairs keep their place in the bank without drawing, and refused objects have a named diagnostic. Only systems that load count toward shared storage. Group named objects by particle file, parse each file once, and merge decoded texture pages in deterministic first-seen order.

Each region compares wanted and held kinds only on frames whose province update list includes the region. A weather switch-off is a wanted kind of none at that producer boundary. An unlisted region retains its pairs even if wanted weather changes or disappears and returns. A newly created pair starts at full alpha. Each producer refreshes the fading member's alpha and active bit, and refreshes the primary member only in the far band. Deactivation retains its alpha. Content decides the distance gates, fade and playback rates. A producer timestamps its new pair on that frame's presentation clock even if it is offscreen. Sound events and delayed systems use that timestamp. Replacing a pair discards its timestamps and moving the clock back restamps them.

Pair selection draws once for each admitted anchor in recorded traversal order before camera culling, including off-camera anchors, because each region's anchors advance a shared counter-hash stream. Cache the resulting choices for the field, presentation and change count. Sound and particle passes share those choices. An anchor uses the map coordinates and the camera supplies wrap placement. Within a frame, acceptance by the first camera envelope that admits the parent remains stamped for each later wrap. Earlier wraps cannot draw it, and an anchor absent from all views allocates nothing. Camera views are unbounded by a fixed-width bitmask. Prepare replacement runtimes off the frame path, preserve matching regions and their missing emitters, and hand off retired storage. Contiguous playback retains populations. Seeks, branch and country changes, gaps, and step backs reset them. Starts are viewer-owned because the recording has no historical particle buffers, random draws, sub-frame deltas or enqueue order.

Particle parameters retain the last number on each of the base and spread tracks and choose the first recognized animation on the base track. Spawn-time curves transform the base at subsystem age before drawing once around it. No spread means no draw. Curves bake 64 Catmull-Rom samples with extrapolated endpoints, then read a clamped age scaled by 64, even though baking uses steps of 1/63. A subsystem applies its force names in order, including duplicates, ignores unknown names and numbers, and withholds a supported subsystem whose named force has an unsupported type or whose nested structure cannot be replayed. Skip hidden subsystems with their children. Force type matching folds case and authored yaw is in degrees. A zero yaw keeps the native pair of zeros. Turbulence uses normalized interpolated gradient vectors, retaining the native contiguous permutation and gradient read behavior beyond the permutation table.

The leading subsystem owns its system's turbulence fields. All subsystems draw their creation values from the leader's stream before the fields are seeded. A populated subsystem advances each shared field once. Applying a force uses its position and amount for that tick, with a local force's center following the transform. A zero amount consumes no field. Field lookup follows turbulence application order. A subsystem advanced alone has no sibling's fields. Keep the large fields heap-owned so moving runtime records between hours transfers their ownership without copying them. Integrate force as time divided by mass, then apply the separately scaled displacement. Use the authored local transform for local births and interpolate the source translation for moving world-space births. Preserve the comparison simulator's own evaluation order.

Once created, a system advances siblings on one clock. A delayed event at the exact current timestamp has not fired. If the clock crosses its start before an update, the new system gets that frame's full update. A culled weather runtime freezes its population and rebases its clock, so reentry preserves only existing particles. An activation starts empty. Long suspensions do not invent unseen frame subdivisions or a steady-state cloud. Subdivide ordinary unscaled deltas in double precision and apply authored playback to each float step. Culled enabled runtimes still perform the incremental sort. Deactivated runtimes skip it.

Accumulate fractional emission debt and keep debt blocked by capacity. A ranged emission rate draws anew each emitting update. Births in a tick share one emitter orientation and draw and retain their individual atlas tiles. A particle expires strictly after lifetime, with ordinary storage swapping in the final particle and trails preserving order. Process a replacement before advancing the index. New births move immediately without aging during their birth tick. Finish emission while live particles remain. Separately, hidden model events still advance their private populations and seeds, withholding only submission. All emitter runtimes for an event retire together after the last living sibling. A duplicate observation refuses without retiring entries after the incomplete list.

The main camera supplies one sort key per runtime, even for wrapped views. A new runtime starts at the second gap. Each frame runs two staggered compare-exchange half passes at that gap, leaving the final record alone and keeping ties in place. Preserve the partially ordered storage across frames. Weather ticks once on its worker after producer and sound work. Rendering and export share the resulting immutable packets. The frame takes the worker state back even when drawing is refused.
