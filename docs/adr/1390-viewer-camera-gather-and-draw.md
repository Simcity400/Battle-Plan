# 1390. Gather model candidates with a held camera and draw with the current camera

A retained model list can survive camera motion inside conservative gathering slack. Use a padded held camera for candidate groups and include wrap-copy identities. A pan or bounded turn can keep those candidates. The current camera's own ground and per-figure cull decide which figures reach preparation, its regular and alternate lit queues, and its shadow casters. A padded candidate outside the current ground contributes no draws. Pose work uses current ground on each wrap and follows the same layer switches as the draw. The native map-frame actors use no ground rectangle, as their draw does.

Record the exact current camera cull and ground bits used to choose the list's figures. If either changes, or a group's bounds move, examine the route figures in the previously gathered groups, using their stored view and group indices. If only instances move and the rule stays the same, examine the changed instances in those groups. A crossing splices the figure in or out. Geometry outside the gathered list does not enter this path. A full gather remains available when its retained inputs change. A figure with no draw units still has a route position. This matters when a caster walk reaches a figure whose descriptors all belong to the alternate queue.

The optional parity check tests the current camera and the source routes independently of the list's stored candidates. It identifies a drawn figure outside the exact camera and an admitted nonhidden figure missing from the appropriate queue, including figures a gathering missed entirely. Nearby motion still uses the current eye's detail choice. Building history likewise limits the candidates by the held camera and derives per-frame admission from the current one. The shifted-eye regression keeps a wanted figure's membership equal to a complete frame walk even when the padded held camera would still admit it after the figure crosses the true mesh distance.
