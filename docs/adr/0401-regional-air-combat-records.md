# 0401. Preserve ordered regional combat records and independent failures

The retained regional air-combat holder has two vectors. The owner's wings occupy the first and foreign wings the second. A holder's raw reported owner may differ from its census container. Census position identifies the country and region. Native lookup coordinates match wing, classification and a date since the current hour within the appropriate vector. Selection is a value. The loader may append duplicate coordinates and a zero wing id is a valid saved value. The readers retain both vectors' physical order and original dates. A process address is a transient read context, never a recorded identity.

The original updater finds or appends the record, adds mission bits, raises the signed plane high-water mark with attribution and equipment, and updates a damage entry. The default attributed country is zero before a positive high-water mark. The independent daily pass prunes records older than its clock-relative timeout. Playback derives expiry. Between full reads the reader may take the dated suffix that the reverse lookup can reach. The suffix and full-read results carry the vectors' native counts. The collector observes the complete authenticated holder census and the updater, with load rebuilding the state. An unavailable or truncated census is withheld rather than presented as a complete shorter result.

Damage preserves its first or second vector, full cause and signed fixed-point amount. The initial vector and second vector have distinct cause sets. Equipment preserves native variant identity and signed quantities. Each capture can authenticate an equipment variant once per address while the game is parked, and the next capture discards that address witness. Damage and equipment failures leave the independent record rows available. Unreadable record roots keep their position and named reason. Checked growth preserves exact omitted-row counts from unaffected headers.

The aircraft map's participant vectors are separate input from the combat records. The reader keeps reciprocal donor, wartime ally and enemy vectors in their native order. Each row carries the owning canonical slot, ordinal and other raw link, including repeats. The viewer authenticates links through the recording. The capture authenticates diplomacy headers and checks a vector's count against the live country-link count before staging disjoint destinations for its entries. A donor display may append an absent donor and optionally its own donors.
