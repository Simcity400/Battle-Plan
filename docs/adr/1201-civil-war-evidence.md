# Civil war lanes reveal recorded evidence at its supplying read

The timeline looks up a single branch reply. Each war has a collapsible heading, expanded initially, with each breakaway below its parent. Siblings follow their recorded order. Validate that every listed side reaches a root when following the parent relation. The heading prefers a country that existed before the outbreak and uses its name at the outbreak. Active sides use the shown hour and former sides use their last listed hour. A side's hover reads its tag within its listed span. The accessible description names all lanes while flags follow the viewport. Tooltip-only relationships and outcomes can read their dated countries while open.

The recorded civil-war predicate determines whether the participants were fighting. An ongoing war has no proven final outcome. A listing for which the predicate never held has no ending to name. If a recording ends during a fight or skips hours after its last observed state, the absence of a read cannot establish an ending. Where the next observation bounds an unseen change, the date is a bound. A winner requires evidence that the other sides were annexed by a side, and exit or outcome evidence becomes visible at its supplying read while displaying its own date. Seeking back to before that read removes the outcome and its presentation. An outsider's annexation alone does not prove a winner.

Selections match native holder identities, so aliases can match the selected country and a later tag reuse cannot stand in for it. A range retains wars that overlap it. Prepare the lane structure at meaningful transitions, including changes in fighting or listed tags and the date or supplying read of an exit or outcome. Between those transitions the shown lanes can stay unchanged. Unknown names and colours remain explicit.
