# Reuse naval operands within one acquisition

Transport capture follows the original unit's transfer selector and retains its identity on refusal. The signed combat-reference count determines whether transfer is prevented. A selected transport speed records the operands before final wrapping arithmetic and the caller's multiplier. The collector can carry those owned operands without rerunning conditional acquisition. It honors the native order of the DLC, definition, country, navy, weather and intel checks. Missing intel lanes and out-of-range weather contribute zero where the native calculation does so. Faulting positive country references still refuse. A region-specific failure is independent of other regions in the same capture.

One acquisition can reuse an ordered region slice, identities, weather, modifier metadata and navy operand arrays. Shared inputs are read when their first consumer reaches them. A failed whole-array read can fall back to the individually guarded operand read used by the single-region path. Failed memo lookups are not recorded as success. This is how batched calls preserve each region's value or first refusal. The cache expires after one acquisition, and the consumer releases owned array copies before starting another.

Strategic navy acquisition is by authenticated physical manager slot. Linked-country aliases do not choose the slot, and slot zero remains null in the manager. The mission readers share the physical navy observation and consume its already acquired header. The reserved country can use the independently selected launch provider. A failed movement header does not invalidate an independent launch path. The caller supplies payload buffers. Refused vectors retain demand without publishing a prefix. Private manager controls and pointers end with the frozen pass.

Naval combat transfer lists preserve raw source order, including repetitions and stale handles. A resolving transfer has one units row even when two combats name it. The row retains every resolving nonzero unit occurrence in its stored order. The combat can extend onto a sea province through a live transferred unit at that province. Carrier-air counting walks the dense container list and its wing pools without deduplicating their occurrences. Save loading can repeat a container. The acquisition deduplicates only the set of requested bases, retaining first-reference order. Group, plane and wing demand is derived from the original header extents and a torn demand hint is dropped.

Naval hover capture walks the ordered source occurrences the game visits. Side hovers take country, region weather when present, terrain and commander terms in that order. Empty sides take none. Escaping ships and carriers with linked air groups take each ordered source for that member. The tree visit records nonzero leaf display bytes and original child order. Repeated siblings remain occurrences. Ancestor cycles or null children refuse. A gated zero root needs neither its descendant reads nor a label. The collector and replay share the owning combat, side, member slot and source ordinal join without retaining a process address.
