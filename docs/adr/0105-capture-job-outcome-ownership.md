# 0105. Keep each worker's storage, outcome, and coverage with its job

A capture worker may keep frame-owned sources leased until its final borrowers finish. Empty reusable boxes do not own a second set of parked row, arena, hash, or source allocations. Reset retires undelivered reports and releases their leases. An assembly consumes a completion marker at most once. An absent, failed, or refused worker cannot fall back to an allocating read on the callback thread. The same rule applies on a forced hour. A new frame reset allows a fresh observation. Test probes constrain the specific handoff or direct walk because unrelated readers may legitimately access the same native root.

Job ownership determines failure containment and read accounting. The template tables belong to Countries and resistance to States. The ordinary naval counter censuses survive only the successful job and its one assembly. A failed part, refused admission, or missing marker discards the censuses. Ship model work lends part zero from the family's own capture and stitches part costs into the same verdict as its unsplit walk. A failed root census lends no model part. Province order jobs return their census leases but forget a held walk if the job consumed its marks and failed. A worker that returns an earlier original refusal preserves its named verdict. Independent hourly design changes still drain when their registry census is off slot.

Research work returns its own storage, outcomes, and costs even if Governance fails. Stability and war overview likewise keep failure independent of Slow. A failed sliced job forgets its potentially partial store, while a completed job that did not publish a slice invalidates the slice so it is read again on the next hour outside its normal slot. Without a retained store the ordinary stability path reads just the current slice. Score and rail observers and army icons keep their own first-stage verdict through a Late failure. Army icons read only once the shared division sources have joined. The spool can lend only its explicitly movable packs and the corresponding caches to conversion helpers.

The daily production jobs retain Slow's admission and completion decision as a common prerequisite. In their slot they also need their own completed readings. A missing or failed prerequisite retains the original named refusal, even after reassembly. Off slot they skip without a read or an allocation. Split modifier assembly keeps the same row results as one whole walk, selects the first failure in range order, and takes shared definitions from the first nonempty range. The daily job returns loaned storage even when range zero was absent. A refusal can clear a prepared split before the next frame reset, and a reset always discards an old split. Changing the number or boundaries of ranges on the next hour never reuses the previous split's selection.

Capture mode gates jobs and sub-walks that supply no requested table, including the masked portions of mixed packs. Map marks excluded families as unavailable without walking them and leaves its included tables alone. Full runs every gated walk. Variant lanes are claimed only after the primary Variant job and all jobs in the DivisionStatus chain. This avoids a burst of claimable variant ranges occupying all workers while the division ranges are still waiting. It does not change what the Variant job reads or publishes.
