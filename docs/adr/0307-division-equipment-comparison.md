# Compare division equipment at the division level

The overview reads recorder-derived rows for the requested slot and attribution, with each division's holding already capped at its own need before summing. A drill reads divisions, pools and template needs and repeats the recorder's comparison for the selected division or template. Needed and held archetypes match under ASCII folding. A holding does not imply a specific design requirement. Unclassified holdings stay in a separate bucket. Each type reports need, capped filled amount and its own shortfall. A surplus type can be visible with zero need and shortfall. A negative holding fills none and increases the shortfall. National stock is dated context and never reduces a division's shortfall.

Prefer a division's newly minted equipment rows, then its daily rows while they are within a day of their read. A complete newly minted read can prove an empty pool when neither source has the row. An unread pool shows needs with unknown holdings. A comparison is known only when pool, need and every design's classification are known. A missing country row on a complete overview establishes zero divisions for that attribution. No divisions successfully compared means the shortfall count is unknown. Resolve template needs once per content binding and retain archetype order from the template's first occurrences.

For templates, select every roster-owned entry, including unused templates. Raw owner zero is reserved and does not name a tagged country. Keep authored roster order, with templates missing settings ordered afterward by handle. Counts come from the derived slot, side and template ranges. Base combat width is the recorded sum of sub-unit widths before country and commander modifiers. A failed model read supplies empty grids along with its refusal. A priority outside the designer's content-defined range has no proven name and is refused by value.
