# Keep retained naval results independent of live combat state

Naval results retain their original ship and aircraft snapshots and transferred hit arrays after those live objects disappear. The retained result supplies its own authenticated identity. Ship and convoy handles, country aliases and the widths and signs of numeric operands stay native. Result admission alone does not establish which icon or control the viewer should show. The result roster and the ordered province buckets are separate sources. The global list may swap-remove entries, while the province bucket preserves the order of the map icons. One walk matches each retained row to its own bucket using temporary pointer scratch, checks the complete list, and reads each bucket in chunks. No pointer scratch enters a recording.

Detail capture has its own outcome. A retained HQ commander can be known absent even when its handle was nonzero. Only a resolving commander permits the borrowed HQ range to be visited. Each support item's character reference is authenticated separately. Detail faults withhold details without erasing a retained result. Influence capture keeps the original definition operands and source order. Shared accumulators may reuse the same term span while repeated occurrences remain visible. Hit buffers share the air and naval lanes. One-touch read faults can fall back to individual hit reads so the grouped copy does not refuse more than the individual path. Count-only sizing validates source headers and never supplies recorded values.

Result creation and merge are observed by the collector because the retained result loses its combat source. The original call supplies the complete result and combat objects. Ineligible sides and combats that were already emitted supply no outcome event. Temporary strikes can finish and disappear in one call without ever getting a persistent reference. A live birth uses the recorded game clock. The combat day and duration fields are counters, not absolute birth timestamps. A separate off-thread demand estimate counts all registered wings because mission changes can expose targets that were absent when sizing ran. Wing population alone is no bound on event count.

Country-owned accident reports preserve vector order and the original references after the ship disappears. Damage remains signed fixed-point, and the sunk byte is rendered through its nonzero predicate. Creation, accumulation, regional clearing and expiry are covered by the report certificate. Sunk-convoy buckets retain the first-seen order of month, killer and owner combinations. Raw country references and an unknown killer survive. The aggregates cannot recover individual route or combat attribution. Sunk-ship records are anonymous list occurrences with full names and independently typed class and equipment references. None of those fields identifies a ship. Their retained list can include identical occurrences and can change order through pruning. Combat incarnation, battle slot and hook sequence belong to the event observation rather than the retained list.

The strategic region supplies the completed daily dominance snapshot in its original region order. Each value object must agree with the containing country key and region, and its six signed fixed-point scalars are recorded without recomputing them. Country aliases are retained. The spool validates unique row identities off-thread.
