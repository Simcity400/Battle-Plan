# Keep route admission separate from arrival estimates

Earlier recordings carried separate fresh-route graphs, owner movement packets and nearest-base caches. Current playback estimates arrival from the recorded routes and a division's base speed. Continue recording the native transport speeds, before content scaling, needed for movement. Railway-gun demand covers each owner and strategic region traversed by its recorded ordinary or alternate paths. Division demand covers the source region of each reached naval-transfer or sea leg. A failed query leaves that owner-region pair unknown.

Movement reads a state term for each demanded state, roster owner and logical owner. An expeditionary owner takes priority when positive. The roster owner selects the source modifier block, while the logical owner determines whether its relation to the state controller is friendly, hostile or neither. Record only the relation speed the branch actually uses. The army term always applies. The hostile-destination attack term is independently demanded and has a separate refusal bit; peaceful moves can still use the common terms. Apply recorded modifier definitions on playback.

Only units that can incur the noncore-supply term demand the owner's access witness at the location province. These units are neither exiled nor exempt by their recorded movement flags. The applicable define can depend on the air form. Country speed records keep the raw entries for the movement-priority subunit and category keys used by its templates. River crossing records keep the raw factor of the logical owner's relation to the crossed province's controller. Both tables distinguish an unreadable domain from an absent entry or relation.

Railway departure selection keeps the native signed level entries in definition-neighbor order and observes the selected source building separately from sorted `RailEdges`. The reached damaged branch alone demands partial health and keeps its signed sentinel values. A present packet can report an observed null building; a missing packet may instead have an unreached gate or a failed read.
