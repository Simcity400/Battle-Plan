# 0906. Reconstruct naval mission icons from retained operands

The naval mission icons derive counts and convoy efficiencies at refresh. Hourly mission rows keep strike force, convoy raid, convoy escort and invasion support inputs. Related task force, ship and counter rows supply the reusable owners, location, mission state and ship classes. Local and weather modifiers are read only with a location, and an out-of-array weather region has no applicable entry. The recorded modifier catalogue provides feature and clamp rules. Retain the raw mission owner even when no region row is demanded. The fleet leader, ordered attached headquarters and mission region range are independent. Captured active-raider spotting, target liveness and the retained region can change separately. Keep the operands of the first live trade target, falling back to a live transport target.

The navy's nonempty regional ledger retains registration order and repeats, including registrations outside the mission's accessible list and those animating out. Select pride from the last counted registration bearing it, and count escort efficiency for the fleet's first escort registration. An absent presence ring uses the native sentinel. For a present ring, its first 720 entries supply counts above zero and at least zero. Its physical region slot agrees with the region's identity.

Country inputs are acquired on their daily slices and also promptly for a new owner or changed commander fingerprint. Combined task force modifiers start at the roster owner's accumulator, while escort efficiency uses the mission owner's entry. Region hubs preserve original order. The escort breakdown is demanded only for registered escort mission owners. Its unavailable value and an observed empty breakdown are distinct. Commander contributions come from the fleet leader followed by the four headquarters accumulators. A reached but unresolved or ineligible commander has absent entries, and no row means it was not read. Slice evidence retains the actual sample hour and prevents claiming a fresh modifier computation from a carried read.
