# 1203. Keep political history, observed identity and execution causes separate

Ruling-party popularity uses recorded `party-popularity` parts under the keys that supplied the data. Keep series requests in content order so a ruling-party change only reorders the existing bands and legend. Place the ruling party's band first. Use a content ideology hue toned by the active palette's lightness and chroma, with theme-dependent categorical colors for absent hues. Unknown ideology tokens follow named ones in content order. Exact popularity is pending until read, and unknown when the recording withheld it. Cache joined tiles by identity and repaint when tiles land. The header renders on government, leader, party-name or availability changes, while the live value paints along the clock even when a paused hour receives a new answer. Name a known leader even when popularity is missing. Read popularity to assistive technology when its value is absent from the compact visual header.

Distinguish a recorded government transition from a census difference or an election date. Neither supplies an execution cause. An exact transition can replace a census marker at the corresponding endpoint only after its supplying read is reached. Grouped markers can be explored page by page and the keyboard can move to a change inside the drawn hours. A new country identity or a gap is not a change event. A name or portrait change can update a known leader's presentation without changing the leader's identity. An unidentified leader does not establish a replacement. Terms and leaders without recorded labels explicitly report that uncertainty.

Stability's event markers use only enabled histories and clip to recorded group membership and shown hours. Branch and refusal edges do not prove an event. A war relation start does not distinguish declaration from joining an ally. An enemy's capitulation can use the same hour's active or ending relation, or an immediately preceding end when that known enemy changed its tag. A gap blocks that inference. A faction run spanning a tag change continues the same membership. A sole first member may establish a founder only where the recorded roster supports it. A dissolved faction can still be named from its last recorded roster for the departure. User-provided display names can replace content names.

For law markers, the law is the idea and not the slot it occupied. A law slot can hold a designer. Keep the same law across compatible country identities and the same character across role changes within its seat. A later identification does not turn the previous appointment into a new one or name an unidentified prior holder. An initial idea or a run after a gap is not a witnessed change. Date subsequent changes by the first read that saw them. Focus completions are occurrence-based, including repeated completions first seen in the same read, and use their first observed bounds. Only scoped focus titles require a dated title read.
