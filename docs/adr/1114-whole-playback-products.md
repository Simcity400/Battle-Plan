# Build whole playback products off the frame thread

The battle plan depends on earlier orders' overlap counters and label positions in drawing order. This makes the whole plan the worker's unit of work. Ordinary plans declare the memo tables. Invasion, active order and labelled paradrop triggers can reach additional recorded tables during calculation. Those tables are learned under the recording's executable tables, and a build that needs new declarations waits for their data. A recording that lacks an input supplies a named refusal. The proof compares label positions and keys with upload generations excluded.

Workers read the map side's committed mirrors. They finish an in-flight build and use the newest available rows for the next build. Reusing the same table allocations can advance how far a plan stands without recomputing its geometry. Work done for an earlier compose cannot count toward the new picture. The battle plan may still draw its prior product during a seek until the replacement is ready. A switch to another viewing country withholds that earlier plan immediately. An ordinary frame may keep an older plan or accept newer rows within its settling policy. A driven whole settle requires the requested hour's rows and returns a named refusal when it cannot obtain them.

Whole layer builders compare both the table identities and any other inputs they read, including time for hourly builders. Reusing a product at a new hour requires the builder to confirm equivalence. Clock is declared explicitly when the builder uses it. A discovery pass may supply a missing table temporarily empty to learn additional dependencies through the same pass. Its output is withheld until the real tables arrive. A build panic is a named refusal and the worker can continue. A stopped worker publishes its refusal in place of an old product. Workers also retire superseded products so freeing large plans and frames stays off the frame thread.

Exact layers such as stock-mode fills represent the hour's own picture. Once a first product exists, each publication waits on the side's thread. A product that stays behind reports the hour it represents. After a wait runs out the side can keep receiving products without repeatedly stalling. Other whole layers report lag without holding the window's drawn hour. A seek or compose starts a new generation, even at the same hour on another branch. It owes the scene a reset install. Taking several worker products before the scene receives one must not consume that reset. A closed layer releases the reader and the held product. The fallback full builder can draw until the new view receives its first product where the layer's view policy permits it.
