# Layer publication uses recorded change sets

The map side owns the replay mirrors and computes layer products. Each layer declares its columns and upstream layers for the view. A schema mismatch withholds the affected layer by name. A reset composes from the mirrors on the side's worker. During playback the side marks entities from applied steps and settles them oldest first. Keep row resolution out of the mark phase where the layer implements that split. Staging owns the upload bytes so the frame thread can install the results without repeating the computation.

Camera placement is separable from the table declaration. Use the shown placement and the zoom target where objects should settle during the camera's ease. A view before the first frame has no placement and a zero viewport. Recheck a layer's declaration when its placement key crosses a cutoff or changes reach. A layer without a placement key reruns its own input declaration. A draw cutoff can release the tables until a reset brings them back. Scope measurements use the current admission for visible provinces and the eye's distance for audible entities. An unplaced entity counts as settled and has no in-view contribution. Only the focused map permits sound.

Dependencies settle in registration order. A dependent can trust its upstream's result only when the upstream finished its dirty entities. Accumulate upstream marks until every drawn dependent has settled. Pass a whole-change signal separately from a list of IDs so a consumer cannot silently miss a reset while selecting a category of marks. IDs can repeat. Dirty queues keep the frame of their earliest pending mark, including entities discovered as other marks are resolved.

Playing frames take ready products. The ordinary lag allowance is two publications. A rate limit is expressed in makings per second, and the allowed lag is its interval in publications plus time for the making itself. The host distinguishes a worker's published hour from dirty entities the side still carries. A whole settle waits for the requested hour and reports a missing product by name. A stopped head has no following playback step to absorb unfinished work, so layers that support the still wait use it to catch up. Individual layers can poll in a side-lock window when waiting there would prevent delivery of the frame. A layer whose expensive product follows the head at its own cadence reports that hour without advancing or holding the shared drawn hour on its own.

Some products prepare ahead. Hand up to the last applied step before publishing the due hour to workers, and keep a product from a future hour until that hour is applied. After a pace change drops speculative steps, retain the last shown valid product and hand the replacement steps in order, including any replacement applied by the frame that notices the change. When a layer receives only applied steps, discarding its unsent buffer is enough. The workers can fold multiple queued applied steps and produce the newest result together. Reset generations fence old jobs and products out of the new picture.

Timed layers own hour-stamped command sets. The frame takes the installer once and writes commands as its head reaches their hours even if the side is busy. A whole picture installs the available sets for the requested hour, and a compose invalidates earlier sets. A timed layer has no upstream or dependent layers, since the rest of the side publishes interval products. The publication checks can identify a layer with outstanding commands or excessive lag. A temporary overlay may replace border plans, so an installer can assert its own plans again.

Hourly reads reuse the same change sets and decode to recover the exact hour of a move. Resolve a step's changed province cells against the mirror immediately before that step. Using a later mirror can assign a move to a different unit after compaction or slot reuse. Omit units born or deleted within the step when the before-step identity cannot establish their movement and use their publication placement. Seeks, piece crossings and catch-up steps without hourly reads likewise use the publication placement. Preserve the distinction between a missing column and a recorded empty change.
