# Exercise collector ownership and wake ordering with synthetic inputs

The acquisition fixtures retain their synthetic address space until every accepted worker has drained. They exercise the production submission and readiness paths with no live game input. A stopped pool can only return parked ownership. Each enclosing Batch still drains accepted, cancelled, and unadopted tickets. The presentation guards release held producers on unwinding, with the guard placed after Batch when its lifetime must end first. Test code that retains a prepared job also retains its nested tickets and store loans until both waves drain.

Use held publication gates to distinguish dependency readiness from collection order. The presentation scheduler must run the first setup, preparation ranges and final ranges without the callback collecting the initial ticket. Unrelated work may finish while preparation is held, and the contended presentation continuation must survive even if the callback never collects it. Final-input scenarios cover incomplete raw, stopped preparation, panic, rejection and cancellation. Test both success and stopped/cancelled successors. A failed stitch follows the original Naval and wing settlement, even if another previously queued job can finish in the meantime. Reaching the barrier twice must preserve the earlier admission verdict. An early clean stitch with a refused census publishes that named refusal without a new read. A contained stitch panic must wait to stop the pool until settlement, even on drain during unwinding. Its first existing census reason takes precedence. Use synthetic publication without live readers to test those orderings.

Test sliced-store failure by withholding a converted family’s publication and reading the omitted owner again on its next opportunity. The forget mask is consumed once and other families retain their certificates. Test acquisition cancellation during delayed restore with and without a free frame. It records loss without reclaiming or reusing partially restored data. A later frame does not make an earlier lost event interval complete. A cold attach and recovery preserve explicit table refusal, and the decoded output cannot make an incomplete row look like a recorded value.

Keep the builder, clock and frame-pool tests separate from queue timing metrics. Hold the builder across an hour so the next take wakes it while a spare remains, and test a burst that consumes the spare and builds its own frame without waiting. Stop a parked builder, one building a frame, and one held before its next look. No further build may begin after stopping. Compare allocated bytes by counting actual frames and each shared owner once. A low-stack regression checks that returning and preparing frames does not reserve a whole frame on the stack. The spool wake contract includes an early wake, whose token must still be available at park, and a no-op when no spool thread is registered.

The clock fixtures grow the capture’s clock separately from the free frames’ clocks. A held spool lease must not prevent the next hour from acquiring its exact campaign clock. Failure to allocate frame storage or a bigger clock fails the capture path without aborting and leaves the opportunity for a later capture to grow. Avoid allocations from panic messages inside a deliberate allocation-denial scope. Test identity changes beyond any former text boundary so the full campaign ID is observed. Retired tables keep their cadence descriptor but do not run. Sliced tables read their selected owner slice hourly. A CountryIntel read delayed by a loan reports cost only once it actually runs.

The division fixture compares with the corresponding typed walk using the same starting capacities at empty, short, exact and ample sizes. Its two countries and alias share three divisions, including null links and signed equipment values. Its nonempty delivery is progress-available and has produced rows. It verifies per-table independent output and exact refusal reasons, including faults in a shared census, an optional status read, or an equipment/request entry shared by multiple paths. An empty authenticated pointer census can supply independent consumers while the full division source is unavailable. A successful capacity grant and an overflow-free job do not by themselves prove source acquisition. After a packet moves, its displaced buffer cannot authenticate that census a second time. Decode installs the captured native detail rows while preserving independently acquired presentation and core data. The fixture uses independent counters for the next unit ID and dynamically typed references. A refused daily mark leaves those distinctions unknown. Province and consumption tests use the actual daily equipment window and full refresh’s fuel roster.

The wars fixture mirrors the typed reader’s input shape with three countries, an alias raw target, and two differently sized war rows including signed extremes. A foreign relation is skipped after gathering types, and a retired war can authenticate without publishing rows. The primary walk visits owned war rows once at the first endpoint. Tests compare the named refusal for used but unreadable links while allowing unreadable unrelated slots. An alias target resolves to its canonical slot before comparing ownership vectors.
