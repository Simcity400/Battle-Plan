# Pace map publication and share province work within a frame

The viewer targets thirty simulation publications per second, capped by display refresh. Publishing more often has a cost even when the display cannot show the result. A publication that starts less than one interval late keeps its original schedule. A longer delay starts a new schedule so the simulation never bursts through missed publications. The simulation looks ahead by the last publication's measured time to screen, capped at one interval, plus the interval over which the next publication will be visible. If a display frame falls outside that range, movable objects hold their last published place. The optional publication check reports missing, backward, or excessively late drawing layers by name.

Each view has one province schedule consumed by units and icon owners. Admission uses the camera and the current province boxes, including visible elevated bounds and the side-plane margin. A held candidate set limits the boxes tested during bounded travel and supported free-eye turns. Each frame admits from those candidates using its actual camera. Viewport, box generation and the held camera or slack participate in the retained key. [Held camera admission](1190-held-camera-admission.md) Every admitted province updates and stays dirty. Out of view, its province ID determines one of one hundred update phases. The ordinary off-screen update clears show bits and removes the old presentation. A steady offline frame can clear all dirty off-screen provinces in one pass.

Selection raises the force count to two. Actual updates spend the count and apply the in-view flag, so deselection leaves one forced frame. Selection does not add a clean off-screen province until an ordinary update reaches it. A click after the scene prepared its list can update that list once. A rejected scene cannot spend the count. Discarding pending work at a successful hour or perspective change restores dirtiness and keeps selection, counts, phase, and icon pools. An unsuccessful replay apply leaves the original work pending. Where there is no scene advance, counters advance once per presentation on their own clock.

The free camera tests distance along its forward axis against the far plane, including at screen edges. Ground rays keep their world-wrap copy for camera movement. Province picking maps the hit back to the canonical map copy. A ray above the horizon or beyond the map ends without a ground hit.

Redraw accounting attributes work to the measured frame. Worker builds are excluded because they can complete during an unrelated frame.
