# 0201. Share one authenticated land-combat census without changing read outcomes

The acquisition worker collects the hourly combat-window and marker operands while the simulation thread is parked. One census authenticates the participants used by the commander, detail and unit readers. Each side's commander is selected once for the detail and tactic readers. Two gathers capture the army and stat-block fields used by the four division readers and the modifier member. Readers share per-source results only during that pass. They encounter a shared value or its failure where the unshared reader would have read it. Reference tests disable these shares and compare tables, outcomes, failure order and demand with the complete production pass. Naming storage in the reference walk still places each shared group key in the arena once so its capacity means the same thing as production.

The census sizes the division destination without an extra read. Its largest side bounds the scratch for committed and reserve lists, which are subsets of the full participants. Each combat uses one country reader for owner and exile context. Exile frames apply only to committed divisions. Exile storage follows the original headers and commits atomically with its division. A short name or exile arena omits that division and counts its exact demand. Successfully acquired term prefixes can grow and commit without a second native read. Movement, naming and commander caches authenticate data only within their pass. Reusable worker and spool storage grows from live demand. An optional local failure remains independent, and readers that depend on the failed census report their unavailable source.

The reserve hover uses the speed share from base speed times the movement factor, divided by the live slowest-speed threshold, less one and clamped from zero to two. This is the component the hover consumes. The capture can obtain and retain its operands before owned conversion. A refused share leaves the division available. The share applies only to reserves. A reset must forget unfinished owned capture. A country source uses the positive expeditionary owner when present and the roster owner otherwise. A reserve's contextual leader may select an ordinary unit modifier or a separate-HQ one. Only sources covered by the native writer certificate can carry. Matching the receiver, accumulator and row identity in sorted source order lets one forward cursor consume each previous tree at most once. A failed tree withholds its own row, and a commander with a zero root contributes no hover line.

The spool joins a modifier member to a published division only when its values match exactly. Unjoined members keep their own values, and modifier member counts follow the shared modifier-side read rather than an independent capture. A refused dependent side or unread members use their own outcomes. Border modifier values remain usable after an independent country-set failure. Roster spans preserve native committed and reserve ordering after rebasing into the arena. Copying a prefix never changes the native order of lists displayed directly by the viewer.

The side hover reads the stored anti-air cache and prevented-damage lanes from side operands. Tombstones remain in retired positions. Display conversion truncates prevented damage to the display step while preserving missing sentinels. Leader hover uses the game's fixed-point skill ratio with signed truncation and the native exceptional result for zero requirement or sentinel experience. No selected commander or a known invalid next level yields no progress, while a refused read keeps it unknown. The learning display keeps native order and requires a ratio strictly above the live threshold. A refused threshold makes the subset unknown. Optional commander, level and threshold failures keep the available parts of the row.
