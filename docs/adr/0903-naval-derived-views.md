# 0903. Keep naval metrics faithful to their recorded source and ordering

Naval dominance uses the selected design's recorded definition weight, range, speed, carrier size and heavy attack. The strike contribution counts all retained accessible-list occurrences and applies the defined decay. Building counts wrap before their content coefficients, and air bases, radar and naval bases fold in that order. Enemy strike effects multiply raider power before the final bound. The display branch keeps its own text multiplier ordering. The fixed5 saturation guard applies to signed overflow, with native wrapping for intermediate products.

Regional mine vectors borrow each authenticated owner's sorted range and use the witness and final key to locate it. A missing owner stays unavailable even when the regional table is complete. A known owner can prove its sparse missing regions separately.

Naval mission counters combine the relevant roster owner, task force, location, fleet leader and the greatest nonnegative headquarters contribution. Ship weights and counts wrap before the terrain division. Weather reduction is clamped, and an unusable fuel numerator keeps the full native default. Range keeps the native minimum fraction and the second guarded division. The division guard recognizes the two lowest and the highest signed numerator sentinels. Escort efficiency uses the mission fleet's qualifying escort task forces on its exact group range with the mission owner's modifier. Raider efficiency takes qualifying raiders from its fleet and range and the task force's own and combined raiding factors. A regional escort ledger uses only its first registration for each fleet. An empty escort ledger, no escort entry or an invalid maximum convoy count uses the hidden sentinel. Raiding averages its reached registrations. A navy without a presence ring contributes full presence to display, while the raw absence sentinel stays distinct and does not trigger the alert by itself.

NavalSupremacy cached rows belong to their supplying midnight epoch. Control sums use directed owner status, independently of the viewer's explanatory roster. The explanatory group uses the viewer, its allies and family members as a unique set, then counts enemy and blockade occurrences in roster order and applies the minimum floor. The share divides friendly by the wrapping sum and returns zero on a zero denominator. One world can share its admission checks across viewers. A refused diplomacy input may withhold a populated region, while an empty region needs none of those inputs.

Positive faction membership is authenticated in both directions. Country0 may omit itself from its cached faction roster. Consult the target faction roster before a subject pointer. A null faction admits the singleton without a roster read. The positive subject census excludes Country0, so the exact hourly reverse and forward status packets supply those pointers instead. A positive raw alias must first resolve through the recorded links.

Repair selection uses queue occurrence order and keeps repeated full ship handles. An assigned selection stops demanding entries after the native result is established. Raw equality includes zero. Other aliases need their canonical links. The model's stored maximum HP has the native minimum of 0.001 HP. Critical damage can qualify an otherwise full-health ship and is required only when that entry reaches the check. A frame and a columnar walk share the same selection rule.
