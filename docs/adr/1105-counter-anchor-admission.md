# 1105. Use authored anchors and gate each counter family independently

## Decision

Seed each province's default unit-stack anchors from its centroid and sampled terrain height before applying authored records. Preserve authored slot order and each slot's authored scale. Standing slots follow the unit's class, with unit scale when absent. Use the same resolved anchor source for counters and province labels. The victory-point anchor inherits the defender's slot when the authored record leaves it unset. Seeded anchors are used when neither the slot nor its inheritance is authored. Report an unavailable standstill anchor by province. Combat icons also need the attacker's own slot, so the content holds the complete stack array.

## Consequences

- The building layer and icon loader share the admitted entities and slot rules. A conflict or ambiguous slot supplies no anchor and the building loader names it. Air bases, rocket sites and gun emplacements use the slot's admitted building type. An admitted icon missing a place uses the province's standing slot. A port icon uses the first eligible port-building visual record in province order and falls back to the dock slot. Follow the port template's spawn-point redirects when building eligible buckets. Region membership follows authored province order, dropping duplicates and undefined provinces with a report. Prepare region icon anchors after the pixel centroids and height field are ready. Name regions with no provinces or missing pixel centroids.
- Ordinary cameras use their height for strict family cutoffs and their map mode's independent gates and priorities. Coarse grouping begins strictly above the selected normal or strategic content threshold. A free eye retains the families and lets each icon fade and group according to its own anchor distance. A preparation includes families the target of an easing zoom will reach. Include every family for an unplaced camera. Use authored bounding boxes and the hour's province-to-state assignment for geographical grouping and keep membership separate if its native owner comparison is unknown. Retain the ordered geography memo per content and state projection.
- Hide unit sources in resources or raids mode and during a peace conference that includes the viewing country. A disabled pass clears retained source membership. Bases, combat icons and retained results keep their independent passes. Port items belong to a drawn base, so an unavailable base pass never reads them. A display filter hides quads while preserving layout, hover, retained windows and reporting. Naval and port owner filtering uses canonical slots. Free-eye grouping and camera admission do not change the facts recorded for the hour.
- Movement follows the settled recorded route point on the content movement map. At every hour the publication records a move, sample the recorded province and movement history. A missing movement map stands movers still. Name reached routes that cannot be placed and leave those movers on their province. Only ordinary unit counters use the rounded zoom lift with 3D units enabled. Its root-height offset fades linearly to zero at the 3D unit cutoff and rounds separately from the projected origin. Port children bypass it.
- The local pointer, fleet, task-force and wing selections belong to the viewer. The local unit selection can affect joins within the strict cutoff if it stays within the content's configured limit. Selected wings gate the strategic base's counts. The observer may open a region or select its mission wings. Ground Crews remains a hoverable control whose click cannot submit a game command. Its displayed cost and supply boost use their own content values. Region and counter hovers identify the simulation operand independently of their rendered placement.
