# Refresh relation owners from a bounded writer log with a full-read fallback

Record the relation status and its owning diplomacy when an opinion changes. Relation replacements always mark the owner because they rewrite its serialized vector. The stability family forwards its existing replacement site. A world load or rebuild ends the epoch. A failed site verification or a missing forwarder leaves the observer unavailable, and the relations refresh falls back to a full read of every owner. A running writer, overflow, missing log, short output buffer or changed epoch also prevents reuse of the previous baseline.

Each native callback appends by index to a ring and holds a running guard until its slot is written. A writer that is lapped during an append can overwrite a newer mark, so it revokes the epoch before it leaves. A concurrent drain cannot certify that slot. The reader samples the cursor before checking for writers and rechecks the epoch. It advances its consumed cursor even when refusing the marks. A full read after that cursor sees every earlier completed write, and later writes are considered in the next drain.

The worker sizes larger ring storage from measured pending marks and the current nonzero opinions. The first writer-free drain installs the offer. A writer can briefly hold the old backing storage, so retirement and freeing are separate steps. A later writer-free drain confirms that the old ring can be collected off-thread. A cache-line-aligned cursor separates heavily written fields from memory writers and the capture read concurrently.

Daily in-place recomputation runs on every existing pair, so its recorded work counts only changed opinions. Owner diplomacy is assigned at status construction and read from that live status. Status reset, construction, reading and slot replacement also notify the war family; reset and slot replacement forward country counter changes. A refresh during a whole-domain writer must read all statuses, and the writer ends the unchecked interval before its active guard leaves. Tests that drive the process-global observation share a lock and reset storage before checking ordered handoff.

`free` recovers each allocation from its original box pointer. `installed` borrows the pointer for the writer and the only consumer. A replaced allocation remains until a drain confirms writers have left. `Hooks::drop` uses `release` only after the last refresh and after installed callbacks were restored and drained, or while the callbacks remain unpublished. `recompute_opinion` forwards its original status argument unchanged. The `relation` macro forwards the verified status, relation and slot arguments and marks every replacement call.
