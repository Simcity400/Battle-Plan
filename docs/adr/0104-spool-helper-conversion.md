# Give each spool helper an explicit conversion scope

Native capture and typed row projection finish before spool conversion. The spool can queue individual conversions without moving the whole frame. The normal conversion marks its owned table ticks only. When a helper queue is active, the spool skips that queue's packs and merges their output before applying a whole Ordinary refusal. A marked `Sync` pack can be borrowed through the checked view, and country appearance may also read `Sync` air data. Characters, intel, portraits and division presentation instead have one queue owner. The cached packs borrow only their documented spool state. The exclusive division projection can also read the current hour. The helper queue cannot access other packs' state.

The safety of `HelperView`'s `Send` and `Sync` implementations relies on the scoped `Helpers::join_some` call. Its mutex orders each helper's accesses with spool ownership. Ordinary shared packs and the air pack read by appearance are reached through `sync_pack`, whose bound proves `Sync`. The hour's queue lends each cached or exclusive pack to exactly one thread. The spool skips those packs while the queue runs, and no other pack reads them. Division presentation's exclusive observers finish before the join starts, and outcomes, slice selection, merging and reuse wait for it to end. This gives a queued non-`Sync` pack the exclusive access of a mutable borrow. Its lent caches return after the join, and no other pack is reachable through the view.
