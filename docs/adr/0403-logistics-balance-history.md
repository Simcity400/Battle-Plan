# 0403. Record logistics history separately from the live logistics display

The country's native logistics series hold signed fixed5 values indexed by archetype position in the equipment database. The daily pass advances and clears every initialized series and then fills stock and, for countries outside AI control, balance. Other countries' balance is missing and their stock is already in Stockpiles. Stockpile mutations outside the daily pass can refresh its records in place and apply the same AI-control gate.

The balance subtracts bounded demand and net outgoing pool differences from the available count. Availability uses recorded stock or the live pool cache as appropriate for the category. Truck and train adjustments apply before the result is clamped at zero. Demand includes deployment shortages, reinforcements, garrisons, cached air requirements, operations and logistical vehicles. The demand and pool-difference sums use the game's fixed bound. The native view can refresh with demand and its category summary includes incoming lend-lease, so its display can differ from the stored daily record. The collector records neither the displayed category total nor a guessed balance for AI-controlled countries.

Keep the identity of the exact native balance record in acquisition scratch. The writer observer may supply the availability operand for that record. Daily and refresh calls use different caller stack slots for the adjusted available count and share the record, actual balance and index arguments. Authenticate the record and signed index before accepting a write. Advancing a history zeroes reused records, while clearing zeroes existing buffers. Tracks use their own observer admission because other country history writers reach other series. Size storage from the live country roster and archetype headers before attaching, and grow worker storage from authenticated headers before publishing.

The schema distinguishes stock series 15 and balance series 16. Positive balance means surplus. Each record index has to match the recorded equipment archetype's own index and its position in the equipment database. Validate each daily window's current slot, its record's ownership and the field count before copying values. Gather adjacent headers and read both complete sets of archetype values in guarded batches.

The live country walk keeps each country's own raw tag. The first eligible country names the archetype list and its script tokens from authenticated records. An all-AI census completes empty without consulting the equipment database. The reader follows only the current daily records of the two observed series. Reused control observations preserve the original refusal. A failed gather keeps that failure across subsequent affected slots before any per-slot rereads could manufacture success. Slots beyond the authenticated control counts use the live player decision where applicable. Synthetic scale fixtures share object storage so the test can exercise the same lookup and control paths across many countries.
