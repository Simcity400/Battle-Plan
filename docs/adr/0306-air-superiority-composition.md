# Compose air superiority from the recorded viewpoints

The game's strategic air entries are keyed by the runtime region slot. Friendly scores add friendly roster contributions and subtract enemy suppression, and the enemy score reverses those rosters. Both clamp at zero. A comparison of sides is a comparison of their representative views, including that view's wartime allies and volunteers. A side uses its leader. A leaderless coalition can choose its best member for each displayed region, while an absent viewpoint or unrecorded roster can withhold the result. A complete sparse score table can prove a zero for a witnessed owner with no row in the requested region. An unwitnessed owner has no air object, and a partial table cannot prove an absent pair is zero.

Build side epochs once per open timeline at group, war and tag-holder changes. A side's historical anchor needs the whole timeline's group history and supplies its stable key and colour provenance. Resolve a tag or canonical slot at the displayed hour. When two raw rows name one tag and region, keep the greatest raw owner ID. A negative row invalidates that owner. Scores remain live at an hour while the reply supplies the region structure and side dictionary. A theatre selection uses the chosen theatres' member-wing regions at the same hour. Unknown theatre membership withholds that filtered result.

The daily view composes each cell at its day's last recorded hour. Bins from chart summaries normally supply the values, with a keyed frame read at a stretch endpoint that is inside a summary bucket. Resolve owners, side and theatre membership at the same selected hour. Count only completed days in rankings and count a day once even when a stretch changes inside it. A day cut short by a load counts in the stretch where its last reading belongs. A recorded day that is still in progress can have a cell without contributing to rank.

The ranking uses the sum of composed daily activity, with region as the tie breaker. Derived tables provide exact totals at the edges of pieces with the same viewpoint and theatre membership. Cut at side representative, holder and theatre-region changes. A leaderless coalition whose view cannot be represented by one owner's row, or any incomplete derived input, falls back to actual daily composition. `AirRegionActivity` can bound that work by the raw scores for every owner. Process candidates in descending bound and finish when every later bound is below the exact last selected total. The bounds count friendly scores for the side comparison, and include enemy scores for a viewpoint.
