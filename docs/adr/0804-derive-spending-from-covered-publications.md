# Derive spending from covered publications

The power ledger attributes a payment to the canonical slot of its raw country at the hour the payment was read. An unresolved link keeps the explicit no-holder identity. Items are identified by typed operands, assigned an ordinal at their first payment and disambiguated when their length-delimited hashes collide. A charge distinguishes an unevaluated subtraction from a change that is not a payment. Daily upkeep, costs and deficits are merged by the native day. One-off payments, unknown daily credits, recurring days, daily credits, daily gains, unmatched costs, instant gains and command-power cap losses are emitted to per-hour log tables (`PowerSpendDays`, `PowerSpendPayments`, `PowerSpendGains`); `PowerSpendTotals` is retired at format 155.

The first hour of a run exposes the existing game backlog. Its power changes and cost publications do not count toward spending or holds. Totals and open-day parts reflect the input coverage. If a required input is partial or missing, its named status propagates and the affected totals are withheld. Coverage is taken from the last linked raw country with a capture and is published per currency and hour. A payment requires a witness covering its epoch, event hour and currency at the read hour. A daily cost also needs a covered daily cost stream and a batch that reconciles with the recorded political application. A query uses an open part only when its currency has been covered since the day began.

`CountryPowerDaily` holds the latest daily application, so a replaced or deleted row closes the previous day using its retained cells. Open-day daily costs are tracked per raw country, epoch, day and application, and projected onto `PowerDayParts` by holder and item: when several applications on the same holder pay the same item, their amounts and payment counts sum, coverage holds only while every contributing application is covered and the projected sum stays representable, and any unknown or overflowing contribution makes the projected amount unknown and uncovered. At midnight each open application settles independently into its holder's totals in O(parts) aggregate steps. Daily cost reconciliation mismatches are tracked per raw country; a holder reports a daily mismatch while any country linked to it or holding an open batch under it is mismatched, each application's part coverage uses its own raw country's reconciliation, and a daily row missing political power (such as a command-power-only update) does not clear an unresolved political mismatch while an open matched political batch remains for today. A complete republication of today's batch replaces any earlier open batch of that raw country for today, including one previously held under another holder or the no-holder identity, while parts from power changes stay; opening a real batch for today clears any earlier same-day empty confirmation until that day closes or is confirmed empty again. A batch that only lost rows acts on that raw country's own open batch, grouped as it was published: a part its remaining rows still pay exactly is kept (refreshing its first source ordinal if reordered), one they pay differently is rebuilt from them, and one they no longer pay is withdrawn, including on shared holders and the no-holder identity; when a country's link has already moved away from the batch's holder (including after link loss), surviving rows move to the country's current holder while dropped parts are withdrawn from the previous holder. A country with no positive cost rows withdraws every open batch of today for that raw country when a fresh daily application read that hour records zero cost with a current-hour daily-cost stream witness (regardless of whether its application sequence advanced or spending was covered since midnight) and marks that day confirmed empty — whether the daily row publishes zero cost with an empty cost table from the outset, an ordinary revision replaces positive rows with explicit zero rows, a staggered update revises the matching cost rows to zero on an earlier hour and delivers the matching fresh daily zero later (clearing any steady mismatch from the earlier hour), a fresh zero daily row is re-read unchanged once its witness or recovery arrives (including across resume), or a deletion leaves no positive rows; otherwise the missing rows (or offsetting nonzero cost rows) withdraw and confirm nothing. A country whose link changes mid-day (including from the no-holder identity to a real holder, or relinking back to the holder still keeping its unknown batch) takes its open batch with it. When the cost rows are read that hour, the country links to a real holder, and its current cost rows still list that batch for today, a batch on another holder is withdrawn and read again under the new holder, and an unknown or uncovered batch still on the same holder is read again in place while preserving its coverage break. Otherwise — when the cost rows are not read, the batch has no rows left, or the country loses its link — the batch stays on its previous holder with an unknown amount and no coverage, and nothing is opened under the no-holder identity by the link loss alone. A publication made while the country is unlinked moves its open batch to the no-holder identity (withdrawing any earlier open copy) and is followed when the country links again. After hours the ledger did not run, the first hour with complete cost rows reconciles every held open batch (including ones held under the no-holder identity) directly against its raw country's current rows before any day settles. Until then a day that closes settles its cost parts as unknown. When recovery lands on or after midnight for a closing held day that has not already settled, and the recovery hour itself writes revised cost rows (or a confirmed zero-cost replacement) for that raw country and day after a link move or sequence replacement, the stale held batch is withdrawn before rolling so the recovery hour's publication applies under the country's current holder rather than settling unknown on the previous holder; similarly, when the recovery hour itself issues a deletion-only revision for a moved/unlinked country's closing held batch and the batch changed, surviving rows are rebuilt onto the country's current holder (withdrawing dropped parts from the old holder) before rolling while an unchanged batch or one with no recovery-hour publication stays on its prior holder. An unchanged batch is left as it was proven (refreshing any reordered part's first source ordinal in place without altering its amount, read hour or coverage), and its holder is checked against the daily total like a moved daily row. Otherwise its parts are reconciled like a deletion against the daily application read that hour, which reconciles nothing when the daily rows were not read: exact parts are kept (with refreshed first source ordinals), a changed part is rebuilt, a dropped part is withdrawn, and a new row opens a part in that application's batch. A rebuilt or new part is covered only when that application reconciles the batch, its sum stays representable, and the capture covers its day. When recovery revises every remaining positive row of a batch to zero and a fresh daily application with a current-hour daily-cost witness confirms zero cost, that day is marked confirmed empty before discarding the empty batch. A changed batch of a country whose link changed leaves the batch unknown and uncovered unless a recovery-hour deletion-only revision for a closing day moves its surviving rows as above. A batch whose rows are gone is withdrawn and its day marked confirmed empty when a fresh daily application of no cost with a current-hour daily-cost witness read that hour confirms it, and is kept for today when that application adds up to its parts. Otherwise it is unknown and uncovered, and a contradicting application names its mismatch. Only an application reconciled in place at its current holder is suppressed from publishing again that hour, so an actual publication written after a link loss on the recovery hour still applies under the no-holder identity. Batches the ledger never held, including those under the no-holder identity, are not read. An earlier day that has not yet settled for its raw country is settled at once. Once a country's day has settled — at midnight, after being withheld as unknown while waiting for cost rows, on a late first publication, or on a covered zero-cost confirmation with no open batch left — the ledger records that exact closed day per raw country as merged contiguous day runs and ignores any later publication for that country on an already-closed day while still accepting unsettled earlier days. Recurring totals track distinct counted and uncovered native days per holder, currency and item as merged day runs (so out-of-order closed days count each distinct day and each uncovered day exactly once). Every settled daily cost part classifies as recurring by its source kind regardless of its cause (including diplomacy relations and blockades), and every read hour that settles recurring spending or daily credits also appends per-`(holder, currency, item, native_day)` entries to the derived `PowerSpendDays` log table (introduced at format 143, each row living its read hour), preserving exact native midnights when multiple native days settle in the same read hour and carrying daily credits' native dates separately from debits without altering the 32-column `PowerSpendTotals` layout. Positive daily costs count as payments, negative ones as credits, and costs without a matching application on the gains row as unmatched. A batch at or before the run's first hour is omitted. When a value would exceed its stored integer range, it remains unknown and uncovered until the observation is rebuilt.

Covered command power allocations add a hold. Releases and refunds remove it, and an unattributable refund invalidates all of the holder's holds. Holds are carried while their capture stream remains continuous and reconciled against its allocated total. The recorder rebuilds the ledger indexes from their own tables on resume. The resume blob (writer version 5) keeps the last hour, the run's first hour, whether a reconciliation is still due, each country's link at the last hour, each mismatched raw country, each country's merged settled day runs, each recurring item's merged counted and uncovered native day runs, and the open-day daily cost batches, validating payment counts, suffix source-ordinal bounds, distinct first cost ordinals, coverage/unknown consistency, run ordering, uncovered-subset invariants, and unmatched item ordinals on save and load.

Table layout is handled in one input module. Power changes come from rows written in the current hour, including available rows from a truncated read. A daily cost publication starts from a country with a cost row written or deleted in that hour, or one that moved holder, and reads its batch in ordinal order, starting from zero.
