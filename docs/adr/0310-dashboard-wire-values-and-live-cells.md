# 0310. Keep exact dashboard values separate from structural replies

The dashboard can keep a structural answer while playback changes its numeric cells. Publish a new structural reply when its rows, identities or order change. Paint the quantities for the reply's current hour and keep an absent live-cell value distinct from a known zero. The panel push identifies the hour outside the structural game-score payload, so an unchanged panel need not be sent again.

Raw fixed-point values cross the wire as strings. Log cells use decimal strings for numbers, base64 for list bytes, and separate `missing` and `notRecorded` cases for an absent optional value and unobserved data.

| Surface | Cell coordinates and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Air missions and statistics | Region rows use `wings`, `m<bit>`, `ours` and `enemy`. Wing rows use `planes`. A mission column carries the game's bit, label and strip frame. |
| Air and naval comparisons | Air side values use `friendly` and `enemy`. Side colors are dated country looks and faction names resolve through the groups read. Missing assignments carry a reason and known zero is distinct. Daily naval control uses 1 for friendly, −1 for enemy and 0 for contested. Observed operands are signed fixed5. |
| Land battle and army loss | The final loss flush is explicitly terminal. A design amount is distinct by handle even with a repeated name. After the first read a side can send changed positions until design membership changes. Retained monthly manpower uses `ground`, `air` and `navy`. Ledger designs use `amount`. The game month is twelve times the year plus its zero-based month. |
| Intelligence networks and mines | An intelligence network row is keyed by directed owner and target. A mine amount is keyed by region and owner tag. Unknown mine owners and unknown amounts remain countable evidence even in a region with no known mines. |
| Navy and occupation | Ships use their full handles and a `hull` cell with the recorded maximum. Occupied state resistance and compliance use fixed5 percentages, with daily speeds. Required garrison template amount is distinct from manpower assigned and from strength, which is a fixed5 fraction. Exiled governments use the raw country ID for live values. |
| Balance, power and reserve | Balance values span −100000 to 100000 in fixed5 and preserve the first record listing the country. Record contributions and static-modifier drift retain effective daily and weekly increments. The ledger dates recurring entries by the end of their day and names a purchase at its first payment hour. Daily ledger items use `spent`, allocation entries use `amount`, and reserve rows use holder and ordinal with signed amounts. |
| Production | A line's handle identifies active and requested factories, Q15 efficiency, fixed5 speed and cost, and output since the reported hour. Weekly units truncate `speed × 700000 ÷ cost`. A licence's party uses its recorded canonical slot and tag when available. Organisation rows use daily funds and points. Dockyard repair amounts integrate the recorded rates over hours with one denominator so all design parts add to the total. |
| Score and territory | Score components are addressed by tag and part, and recorded score multipliers are fixed5. Territory amounts use slot and `value`. World totals and unnamed portions are separate cells. Holder-by-region values keep an exact denominator for whole-state shares. A missing region or holder key leaves the ownership or region unresolved. |
