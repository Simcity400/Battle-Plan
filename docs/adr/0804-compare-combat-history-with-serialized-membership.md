# Compare combat history with independently serialized membership

Combat log comparisons require all saved months on a full read and compare only countries witnessed by `SliceReads` on a sliced hour. The tail of each array is always expected. Recorded older entries are also compared, because a country can receive a full refresh when its prior read is too old. A read with no rows still establishes coverage. A missing saved group confirms deletion only when every saved theatre was readable and no theatre has the global reference. A transfer to another country does not delete the group. The prune detour's final rows are reported separately. [Combat log comparison](../../crates/recorder-oracle-combat-log/src/combat_log.rs)

Serializer membership is independent of payloads. Retained group logs can be empty, and a zero amount omitted by a pool still has to belong to the saved row and receiver. Fixed5 amounts compare exactly despite alternate decimal spellings. Field presence and order still compare, and the shared save parser keeps its spelling-sensitive equality. For lend-lease, compare the giver's saved pools and receiver even when one pool omits zero. [Observer comparisons](../../crates/recorder-oracle-combat-log/src/observers.rs) · [Retained history](../../crates/recorder-oracle-combat-log/src/retained.rs)

The army serializer saves division totals while formation memberships come from the theatre's regular groups. Army-group membership must not double-count a child army. A proved unsaved slot-zero regular group can qualify only its reciprocally observed members for the inferred split. Every other grouped and ungrouped member remains comparable, as do the totals. The independent text-save census test reads the save supplied by `BATTLEPLAN_CENSUS_SAVE`.
