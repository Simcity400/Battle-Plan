# Reproduce ribbon and railway gun presentation from recorded inputs

Battle-plan geometry keeps fixed5 rounding, wrapping, and operation order because changing when each product is truncated changes ties and signs near zero. The quarter-turn approximation is intentionally imperfect. Centroid ribbons always subdivide between smoothing passes, whereas border chains use their content-defined subdivision flag. The floating-point path widens sharp corners away from the turn and offsets repeated visits so ribbon legs remain distinct.

Raid and battle-plan ribbons use the same ordered manager and definition buckets. For a fixed-control ribbon, emit one pair per smoothed control through the head cut, then the head. The head is determined by authored head length independently of technique. A moved pair at the cut keeps the control's original distance for width and texture position. A nonzero texture-loop length replaces total length for that position. The head starts from the body's cross-slope, and the positive perpendicular edge carries the first texture V coordinate.

Admission considers a single horizontal seam copy, preferring the negative-X case, and counts contact with the side planes as visible. Combined views preserve which arrows a prior view already submitted, and labels are collected only in the inner-mask pass. Even an empty mask bucket can need a stencil clear. Native allocation advances to the next 512-byte boundary and fills slack with the last vertex. A single strip uses the padded buffer length and split strips use the actual arrow length.

The scene runs the ordinary ribbon pass before entities and the late passes after. The fixed vertex stride is 32 bytes. The gap after XYZ supplies no fourth position component. The four interpolants give distance along the ribbon, cross-strip position, a head marker, and a texture variant. Definition buckets follow file order with the widest arrows first within a bucket, and the first definition of a name wins. Unreadable boolean overrides keep the prior value. The label font and technique come from the last applicable text declaration. Later graphics passes can disable lighting factors without changing geometry.

The recorded content supplies execution defines and the native constructor defaults. Follow the reached branch before asking for its inputs, so a missing input cannot affect an inactive branch. Army-group children share the parent callback, and generic raid arrows retain their constructor state. Unmet prerequisites can stop movement while the inner execution effect stays active. Geometry reads distinct pruning limits for offset-bound orders and unbound fronts. Paradrop lift depends on path length and the authored ceiling. Draw label offsets along the curve and keep its textual technique separate from the label's country name. Flip the texture V coordinate when the stored teeth side differs from the authored culling direction. The graphics layer may use replacement art where available and falls back to the recorded layers when a replacement fails. Assigned and unassigned masks remain separate resources.

Plans, air overlays, raids, and trade routes share arrow definition buckets while retaining their own admission and visibility decisions. Garrison areas, invasions without routes, incomplete paradrops, and fronts without usable pairs draw no ribbon. Required execution facts that are unknown withhold the order and its children, with a reason. Curve-closeness measurements refer only to unbound centroid paths and are reported in thousandths of a world unit. Trade-route controls retain world position and picking radius. Test each stored point against the normalized pointer ray at its eye-distance, including radius equality, and yield every matching line once in draw order. A continuous segment between two missed points does not establish a hit.

The recording contains railway-gun and rail operands. The viewer computes the class-13 camera, fog, route, join, and counter rules from those operands. For the action, use the ordinary route even when an alternate animation path exists. Combat or a route wins over reference-entry state. A gun in combat attacks when its owner is an attacking participant and otherwise holds. War at the destination is tested against the controller of the last ordinary-route province. The stack compares all gun members and takes the visible-member mean for the status bar. Native nonpositive maxima count as full and fill arithmetic wraps before division.

The stationary land warning requires raw rail-neighbour count, cooldown, and building evidence for an untransferred, unexiled gun. Sorted rail edges cannot certify the signed pair count. A null rail or building or a nonpositive building level establishes the warning. Missing pair-count or cooldown evidence leaves it undecided.
