# 1398. Standardise the widget frame, header, body states and settings

Every dashboard widget shares one frame (`DashboardGrid.tsx`) and one body-state component (`WidgetState.tsx`). Keeping chrome, states and controls in the frame keeps all widgets consistent and lets widget bodies stay focused on their own data.

**Header.** One 38 px line in fixed order on every widget: icon, title, optional summary pill, then on the right: subject, view switch, time, settings. Nothing else enters the header; chart zoom reset renders in the chart body, and optional body captions (`WidgetCaption`) render in a dedicated slot above the widget content area so bodies lay out in the reduced height without overflowing. A clipped title shows its full text on hover and keyboard focus. Summary pills stay short (at most 24 characters), never carry controls, dates or measure captions, and hide whenever the body shows a state. Widgets without a subject show no subject chip. One frame-owned view switch per widget renders as a segmented control for two or three choices and as a menu for four or more, writing through the same saved-option path as settings. One time chip replaces the Live toggle and shows `Live`, `Pinned · {date}`, `Whole campaign`, or a range; clicking it opens the same time editor as settings, while follow-only widgets show `Live` as plain text. The settings gear is always last on every widget, including Campaign map, whose map controls live in a `Map` section inside the standard settings panel. On narrow headers, the frame measures available width against the actual rendered control widths on resize and header content changes (never per frame) and drops stages only when the current stage overflows, in fixed order: summary pill hides, view switch becomes a menu, subject becomes flag-only, title truncates, and time shrinks to a dot; time and settings never drop.

**Body states and failure classification.** `WidgetState` draws every non-picture state with fixed precedence: outside the recording, not in this recording, nothing chosen, no members, not recorded, couldn't load, loading, nothing happened. Only retryable `Couldn't load` uses error styling and `role="alert"`. Failure classification is strictly type-based (`classifyFailure` in `queryError.ts`), never matching error message text: `RefusedError` renders as neutral `Not in this recording` with no `Retry`; `ShownError` renders `Couldn't load` with its plain reason, `Retry` and `Details`; unclassified `Error` renders `Couldn't load` with `Retry` and keeps raw technical text strictly inside the `Details` popover (with `Copy`) and `data-detail`. When a refusal or failure carries only untyped backend text, the second line reads `BattlePlan couldn’t tell why. Details has the technical reason.` rather than leaving a bare headline. `follow/pipeline.ts`, `follow/page.ts` and `follow/panel.ts` preserve subscription and panel refusals as `refused` / `RefusedError` rather than downgrading them to failures. Retrying any followed stream or registration failure restarts the whole `Pipeline` through `restartPipeline` (disposing the old reader worker, lane workers, ports and page state and bumping a pipeline generation key on `<Body key={pipelineGen}>` in `DashboardGrid.tsx` so the frame, header and grid stay mounted while widget bodies remount into their clean loading state and re-subscribe on the new pipeline; body-local UI state such as scroll position, expanded rows and open details windows is intentionally discarded on Retry); in-place recovery inside `pipeline.ts` and `page.ts` was rejected because re-creating channels and rehydrating routes/mounts/bindings in place left stale reset counters, leaked reader workers, un-replayed pinned mounts, cross-session races and per-route holes. Loading fills the body with a skeleton rather than a spinner, reveals `Loading {subject}…` after 2 s, and accepts a family skeleton hook (`useWidgetSkeleton`). While paused on a held picture, a corner `Reading {date · hour}…` badge appears after 400 ms without replacing the held picture; while playing behind, the badge uses an undated `Catching up…` label (placed in the header for maps so the native surface cannot cover it). `scale="section"` renders the same states compactly inside drills and held failure notices.

**Settings and value states.** Settings sections follow one order (`Title and icon`, `Campaign`, `Countries`, `Time`, `Size`, `Options`, `Map`, `Colours`, `Axes`, `Interaction`) and apply immediately. `Reset` appears with its label when settings differ from defaults and offers a 6-second `Settings reset · Undo` notice. Unavailable choices remain visible and focusable (`aria-disabled`) with a reason hint. `ValueState.tsx` provides the single DOM rendering for the five value states (`ValueState`, `MissingPicture`, `MissingName`, `valueStateText`) with flat plain props (`kind`, `text`, `unit`, `reason`, `narrow`, `atLeast`, `className`) and no dependency on chart families.
