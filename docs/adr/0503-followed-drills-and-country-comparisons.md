# Follow the observed hour without rebuilding country panels

Air and bombing drills keep their mounted structure while playback advances. The followed painter reads parts and totals at the clock's current hour, updates the history head, and leaves hours outside the recording empty. A pinned breakdown identifies the country named and flagged by its body at the displayed hour. Other widget headers use the widget's country selection. Headers stop following while their widgets are out of view. Faction headings reread the historical name and render only a change.

Comparison panels keep independent country values and share settings. Group comparisons retain the branch-wide union of members and clip each country's values to its recorded membership. A balance with a country-specific denominator does not offer an aggregate. Diagnostic notes are keyed by slot and named at its displayed hour. An equal note list preserves the previous state, and memoized panel specs keep per-panel dependencies stable across note updates. Selecting a widget campaign uses shared campaign state, which opens each recording once and serves it to every widget and map.

Military air comparisons use the native holder as the series identity. Races and bars can answer from windows carrying earlier totals, while breakdowns, histories and lines read their full span. Followed rows retain a holder that leaves the ranks so it can return without new artwork reads or a render. A top ranking has only nonzero holders. A followed drill changes structure when its holder or ledger completeness changes. Market tables and drills preserve the product's offer, contract and agreement keys and their recorded terms. The occurrence list filters either party, shows newest first and reveals rows at the head as playback reaches them.

Logs and spending panels render a half-open block of hours and reveal rows already mounted ahead. An unrelated render within that block keeps its end. Spending starts a new block before a previously shown row can change, such as on a coverage change or the first day of a month. It preserves unchanged row objects, uses prefix sums for running totals, and requests dates for the mounted and newly drawn rows together so row-measurement reports normally add no request or render. The country column alone depends on arriving names, and each row uses its own recorded date. Held failures remain outside the widget body, so a drill or an early state cannot conceal them.
