# 0505. Keep dashboard reads aligned to playback

Country looks belong to the hour named in the query. A tooltip asks for its visible countries only and combines each exact name and flag with a continuity-checked sample while that part is pending. A completed read with an empty or failed answer stops showing the sample. Idea tooltips read the targets named by their current reply on demand and retain those answers until the reply leaves, with failed reads retried while shown. Date-scoped power ledgers ask for each dated alias in aligned chunks and cut the held answers at the displayed hour.

Log counts are grouped from six-hour buckets to bound the size of a reply. Fixed ordinal pages count from the oldest row, so page keys survive playhead moves. The playing view lays out blocks ahead and reveals their rows from the frame clock. Prefetch runs before the next block is needed. A page beyond the rendered block can land without causing a render, and an older block's list demand cannot cancel a page the clock is still approaching. Failed cut pages and unrecorded hours keep their missing state and reasons. Cursors and prepared replies can be reused after remounts and setting changes.

A moving chart view keeps one read per lane until it has something to show. The latest view takes over after that answer lands. This avoids continuously cancelling reads on slow connections. A settled answer can cover later views when its own change hours allow it. Streamed partial answers cannot provide that guarantee. The client, source and selection are part of the lane identity. Leaving a lane cancels its work and a failure belongs to the requested view. The air ledger follows by holding its current and next windows, with cumulative replies joined from the span's start for the completed view.

The wire format keeps native 64-bit IDs, lengths, and signed values in decimal strings. Country tags stay opaque because they come from the recording's mod stack. Spot artwork is loaded separately from the facts it decorates. Existing sprite frames are reused under the recorded source and content revision, with text displayed while artwork loads or when its request is refused.

During following playback, country labels read from the last identity change at or before the head. Looking up labels at every moving hour would request and render facts that did not change. Hourly facts that do need a fresh value use a trailing update and immediately show the first value after a quiet period. A settled reply held on the client also shows on the lane's first render without a loading transition.

Widget catalogue placements compare options after applying field defaults, because a value equal to its default is omitted from storage. Content presets take the place of a generic default card when they exist. An unavailable capture mode stays distinguishable even for older product shapes that carry availability without a reason.
