# 0403. Retain Combat Log state from authenticated acquisition and writer events

Combat Log acquisition follows the game's manager and orders-group lifetimes. Slot zero is a null sentinel. A qualifying writer uses the positive expeditionary owner or the roster owner and requires a live orders group. Land equipment loss also requires the feature gate and either a country gate below one or a live-player roster match through canonical country links. The collector reads held logs for all countries. Current player status, AI control and viewer perspective do not establish the coverage of retained data. Air and naval equipment losses do not use these arrays.

Own loss arrays are keyed by exact variant, orders group, month and reason. Combat, exile, paradrop and overrun use reason zero. Attrition uses reason two in combat, three during exercises and one otherwise. Volunteer transfer also uses one. Recoveries use their own array, grouped by month with reason zero. The loader discards unsupported own-loss reasons. The retained-history reader takes own losses and recoveries from `CombatLogEquipment`, and carries enemy amounts separately.

Only the latest entry can change. When a writer appends a different calendar month, the old entry becomes final, and each entry keeps the date of its first write. Each selected country gets a whole read at branch start, on refresh or recovery, and on its first selection in the second calendar month after its last whole read. Between whole reads, authenticate every month header and read the two latest pools in each array. An older pool can reuse rows read in full after it left that window if its country, group, kind, month, exact date, reason, entry identity, pool storage and count still match. Absent entries leave the table and empty pools contribute no rows. A whole read records fresh values and reports a changed sealed month in the collector diagnostics. The game uses a fixed 365-day calendar and fixed month lengths. Month buckets combine zero-based month with elapsed years. Text-save hours start at one and native hours at zero. The daily manager prune follows the hour-23 sample and applies the loaded maximum age before removing empty or unresolved groups. A retired group can no longer receive writes, so its existing values remain final until the prune removes it. The separate combat history prunes records outside its inclusive hourly retention interval, including future entries, after the date advances and before processing rounds.

Read extra history only for a reached group, using its authenticated header. The owning worker can grow storage, but a partial failure withholds the result. Settled battle-pool indices can be recycled and cannot identify a live battle. The collector asks only for indices reached by startup history or a reference append or release. When a mutation touches the last month, capture only that settled entry. A reference event establishes the slot's lifetime, so the quiet boundary needs only the reached slot's current occupant, without visiting unrelated slots or payloads. Capture own amounts from their existing replay source and avoid rescanning the previously authenticated side loss pool. Sizing away from the game thread includes training and attrition variants before a group exists.

The private mutation stream is allocation-free. Machine addresses serve as transient lifetime keys and the reducer translates them to replay keys. A failure invalidates the whole transaction, including facts already staged. The same applies to supplemental observer operands. Relevant game state can change without changing a pool's size or total amount, so those aggregates cannot authenticate its individual amounts. Finalization drains pending losses before a side is deleted. Terminal acquisition uses the finalized values and streams exact entries without allocating scratch. Only positive manpower and equipment admitted by its archetype allocation are serialized. Zero entries are retained only when the relevant pool allows them.

The shared combat registry counts complete land and naval object pointers. The land reader takes its combat body from each complete object's secondary base. In a retained template event, the native win count has settled at the observed total-counter instruction. Compute its upcoming wrapping increment from the reached template without rereading the owning group. Supplemental pool admission uses the same allocation-quantity gate as side pools. A combatant's manpower observer counts removed and remaining participants, and stores category and aggregate equipment as signed fixed5 amounts. Its raw equipment entry consists entirely of integer words with every bit pattern valid.

## Country selection and capacity

Walk managers in slot order and group logs, month entries and variants in their stored order. A full equipment walk reads own loss and recovery arrays. Additional retained groups supply enemy losses, manpower, templates and battle references independently. Authenticate each manager header even when selection omits its group data. Prepare selection from the live manager census and stop on a refusal. Ask selection once for each manager with a group log. Its returned window applies to all of that country's ordered arrays. Initial-history consumers can share the whole authenticated group copy, with their own demand gate.

Validate each identity, including references, and refuse a reason mismatch for the four own-loss arrays. The recovered array's stored reason is zero and its array supplies its distinct replay kind. Month buckets must fit the row key and be unique within each array. Pool addition merges equal variants, so a repeated variant is invalid. Sort identities to detect duplicates within bounded callback storage. Copy whole native headers during guarded acquisition and reserve using exact live pool counts. If a pool cannot be stored, withdraw that country's rows and retain the complete prefix and demand. Later pools add their full native demand without reading amounts. The recorder only receives a complete country or its refusal or truncation.

The worker can hold authenticated variants by pointer for one parked walk. Clear the pointer memo before the next walk, since identities can change across hours. Sealed equipment rows retain the variant IDs recorded when the pool was read. It grows with actual pool headers, starting without allocated storage. The table retains an empty slot for bounded probing. A callback without a memo authenticates every variant individually. The census keeps manager country tags as raw references, and group logs retain their complete orders-group references until serialization and key translation.

The prune boundary first reads the final retired-group rows from the manager. Resolve each group with the normal registry. An unresolved group belongs to the prune set, and invalid or unreadable registry data refuses the scan. The collector follows only affected countries during the hourly scan. The prune capture may report exact truncation without treating omitted rows as a malformed read. Test coverage exercises guarded read order, unselected group contents, whole-country capacity and memo reuse across different fixtures. The scale benchmark times full reads, hourly slices, window reads and the deleted-group scan for interactive and broad-coverage workloads in synthetic memory.
