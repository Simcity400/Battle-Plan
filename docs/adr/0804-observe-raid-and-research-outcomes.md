# Derive raid and research outcomes only when observation proves them

## Raid ends

`RaidEnds` publishes one row per finished raid so readers do not need to scan its history. The first complete read establishes the baseline and omits already-ended raids. An end row lives at the first read of an ended raid or the first read that proves a previously live raid disappeared. A deletion across an incomplete read leaves its last cells unavailable. A same-hour deletion can use the recorder's retained cells. The victim resolves from the game's target-country rule at the end observation and is frozen there. The launch witness is a phase of flight or a finite nonzero distance. The three fixed64 sentinel values cannot establish distance. The first observed hour is exact only when the preceding hour was read without the raid. The resume keeps only the last read hour and the first-read and launch witnesses for live raids. ## Research assignments and coverage

Country slices have independent read hours. A holder has an observed read when an owner is read and all its owners with input rows are read at that hour. Valid slot observations preserve each row's recorded position and validate any technology occurrence and token. Coverage can reach one read period less one from the observed hour, and only while table completeness, ownership and owner read hours still establish it. A break or unobservable hour ends the cover. A holder whose tag changes closes its assignments even if the same canonical key is used immediately by the next holder.

Between continuous reads the writer distinguishes completion, a switch to another known technology, and an unknown end. Completion evidence includes an increased technology level, a newly researched inventory entry, and completion without a move to another slot. If a departing slot leaves completion unresolved, the assignment can wait for evidence for one read period in a separate lane while the successor is active. Expiry, reassigning the same technology at its prior level, or conclusive evidence ends the wait. A noncontinuous observation ends active assignments unknown and stops the waits. Completed entries are emitted once per hour. Temporary lanes prevent a new assignment from overwriting the prior assignment's outcome, and ended waits in those lanes clear the following hour. The resume keeps read and coverage boundaries in its blob and restores open assignments, slot availability and indexes from the tables.
