# 0101. Sample uncertified collector inputs and keep experiments opt-in

The collector carries a complete reading only while it can prove that the writers relevant to that reading have remained quiet. Capture flags enable the narrower read and select its required observer families. Without a certificate the collector follows the ordinary read for that family. Diagnostics and synthetic fault injection remain opt-in so an instrumented run does not establish production timing or behavior.

## Certification boundaries

| Feature | Reuse and fresh reads |
| --- | --- |
| `relation_changes`, `history_changes`, `opinion_changes` | Refresh the air relations vectors, air history holders, and opinion statuses that their respective native writers touched. |
| `character_changes`, `party_leader_changes` | Character writes invalidate country-leader and advisor role traits, advisor modifier caches and tooltip keys, and affected party leader rows. Party writes also invalidate their party leader rows. Role sites share the advisor family's hooks. |
| `advisor_pool_changes`, `appointment_changes`, `character_record_changes` | Pool and listed character writes invalidate available advisors. Pool token writes invalidate advisor tokens. Hired advisors and country active and retired character lists require a complete walk and a quiet hour across their pool, appointment, character and roster writers. |
| `roster_changes`, `census_changes` | Roster stability carries the character and ScriptFlags scope walks and the role census. Scientist roles have their own certificate. Census writers forwarded by the roster and advisor families also invalidate the role census. |
| `flag_changes`, `variable_changes`, `party_changes` | A marked ScriptFlags manager invalidates its scope. ScriptVariables always rereads the store seed. Political party popularity and the ruling flag are fresh each hour. Party and idea list changes invalidate their respective carried rows. |
| `political_status_changes` | Capitals, elections, parties, ideas and popularity writers cover the combined politics walk. The politics capture family forwards its relevant writer. |
| `building_changes`, `territory_changes`, `province_changes` | Building changes invalidate their building status. Territory and forwarded building writers invalidate state or province rows. Manpower, StateFlags and BorderConflicts remain hourly, as do border-conflict provinces and the fort operand of fortified provinces. |
| `wing_changes`, `mission_changes`, `air_record_changes` | A wing equipment-pool writer invalidates its equipment. The registration and unregister operations invalidate the regional mission registrations, with mission headers and the wing census read fresh. Regional combat rereads a holder changed by the air-combat updater and uses its records since the preceding capture. |
| `rail_changes` | A write at a province invalidates its batch of 64 rail slots, including cooldown, building and selection operands, indexed levels and edges. The collector reads the cooldown list every hour and marks all its provinces on the daily countdown. |
| `navy_ledger_changes` | Every ledger writer also writes its registered-mission set. The collector always reads the set and can skip a navy's region ledger headers when the set is empty. |
| `air_base_changes`, `air_base_carry_changes` | For an air base being read, the dense list names the country-container vector slots to read. The base's pools, wings, fuel and capacity are read with it. A state air base can carry its registration subtree while container, pool and wing writers are quiet. Country links still resolve hourly. Capacity refresh, creation and destruction invalidate the base and manager slots. The raid, ship model and building families supply the relevant notifications. |
| `army_stat_changes`, `ship_model_changes` | The active-stat writer invalidates the division's attack operands. Army and stat blocks are still read each hour. Ship, medal-store and carrier-base writers invalidate held ShipModels inputs. The fleet context, position, countries, weather and variants stay fresh. |
| `country_input_changes`, `technology_changes` | Country stability inputs and detail lines, and research technology rows and definitions, reread marked countries alongside their daily slice. Captures account for the native writers of status, membership, definition eligibility and bonuses. |
| `focus_changes`, `projects_changes` | After the daily tick, read every state-owning country. Between ticks, read countries marked by a national-focus or special-project writer. Builds without the corresponding feature read every country every hour. |
| `weather_changes` | Read the native update window at the cursor. Reseeding, reset, console commands, loading, cursor jumps and gaps force a full read. Builds without this observer read every row every hour. |
| `appearance_changes`, `counter_changes`, `visibility_changes` | Reread marked owners alongside the daily slice. Appearance observes changes to identity, color, graphical culture, names, subjects and forwarded political and character changes. Counters observe modifier, roster, military, territorial and relationship changes. Visibility can carry the raw accumulator after a quiet modifier rebuild and reset witness, with definition and eligibility checks kept fresh every hour. |
| `raid_changes`, `raid_intel_changes`, `naval_tree_changes` | Raid state reads a marked owner within the hour, alongside its daily slice. An unobserved hour reads every owner. Raid intel can reuse raw strength and defense only with the same accumulator header and country tree certificate. Country and commander naval hover trees carry until a rebuild. Other reached trees read every hour a live combat reaches them. |
| `world_input_changes`, `province_unit_changes`, `retreat_ban_changes` | Province name, naval access, tension and modifier source checks run hourly and may use the last certified reading. Modifier carry is per country. Province order lists invalidate on append or erase. Commander retreat bans invalidate when the forwarded naval tree writers reach their modifiers. |
| `area_changes`, `local_subunit_changes`, `template_changes`, `faction_changes` | Area writes reread affected provinces' current and former areas and neighbouring areas. Local sub-unit and requirement writers invalidate the owning country. Division templates can carry their fallback name key independently of the hourly icon walk. Faction goal and companion state depend on their writer certificates. |
| `war_changes`, `unit_leader_changes`, `unit_leader_trait_changes` | Read each war's casualty counters afresh even when the shared war set and fixed fields are certified. A quiet roster hour can retain unmarked leader rows. Lifetime and modifier writes forwarded by the naval tree family invalidate leader and trait rows. |

## Diagnostics and fault tests

`advisor_profile` and `callback_profile` bound capture timing samples. `phase_cpu` measures cycle costs by spool, helper and sizing step, while `worker_sampler` samples the in-process workers. `executor_join_help` gates a comparison configuration that tries up to four times to claim eligible work within half the initial expected remainder. It predicts the cost of cumulative attempts with a decaying maximum, and uses a mean for awaited work.

The breakdown, attack and naval-tree witnesses record native operands separately for `recorder derived` to check. `conversion_check` compares both ScriptVariables representations in each hour and reports differences in `status.json`. Live smoke can opt into a one-shot journal append failure through a `columnar.fail` marker. Allocation fault tests refuse selected checked allocations. The foreign-unwind oracle runs in a separate parent-controlled process with a separately built native test DLL. Collector spool tests use the recorder's replay path to save their spools.
