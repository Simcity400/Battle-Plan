# Vision and weather retain only results of the applied frame

The vision worker copies the mirror tables on compose and updates its private copy from each step. Its products carry the step's applied hour and epoch along with changed province bytes, canonical controllers, unit sightings, the limitation notes and any changed global parts. The frame may lend future steps, but cannot publish a result from before a reset or for an hour it has yet to apply. Steps already applied can coalesce to the last product. The frame waits for all required work only for a whole settle. A gap publishes no new visibility until the first recorded step after it triggers a whole refold. Missing content and unnamed tags are named limitations rather than zero visibility or an empty country name.

On a perspective change, the worker restarts from the mirrors at the applied hour with the new choice. It replays steps lent ahead, and the displayed hour remains the previous result until the new product arrives. A pace change invalidates future steps and similarly restarts from the mirrors. A layer receives future steps only in its applied epoch and stops before a step that resets or packs a read table or carries no hours. Those steps arrive at application so the worker does not fold from a stale baseline. A rebuild still names the provinces whose values changed, allowing readers of province bytes or controllers to invalidate selectively. Global inputs rebuild by links, countries, viewing country and admitted slots. Country-link resolution preserves an absent controller as missing.

Weather is derived from recorded flags in priority order, with no region kind in a gap or from a table that supplied no usable row. It stages its region field off the frame thread and installs only after a kind change. The particle pass retains particles belonging to regions whose kind remained the same. The branch and hour supply continuity and seeds, so rerendering a frame or applying an hour with unchanged weather does not restart the field.
