# Translate anchored overlays between placements and keep atlas coordinates stable

Lay out and paint overlay geometry relative to its anchor. While the camera only pans at the same height, pose and lens, shift only affected windows and hit shapes in whole interface pixels. Free-eye views, an unprojectable anchor or a move beyond the padded placement require a new layout. Padding gives offscreen windows room to enter before a new layout is necessary. Compute movement from the placement camera and compare it with the shift already written, so rounded deltas do not accumulate. This keeps the placement error within one pixel per axis until a new layout replaces it.

Track shifts by painting generation. A new painting starts with its own quads, and a painting of a reused layout still starts from that layout's camera and playback hour. Place a publication's movers at the drawing frame's actual head, even when the publication was prepared for a later hour. Preserve fractional playback time as an integer hour plus a fraction. A single-precision value at an absolute replay hour would snap to four-hour steps. Track endpoints hold position outside the published interval.

An upload key and stamp also have to identify the position of a retained window. When moving reused geometry, add the new step to the offset the composition already wrote. Apply the same displacement to its rectangle, exact corners, clipping, hit shapes and world transform, accounting for the transform's inverted Y direction. These are the changes a fresh painting at another whole-pixel origin would produce. The anchor judge checks every quad against its own expected shift and reports travel, track coverage and moves that round to the previous pixel so a viewer can distinguish a playback stall from rounding.

Keep fixed-size texture pages and allocate new pages as cells fill. The page size respects the adapter limit, with a supported default before the device reports one. A cell larger than a page is refused, and no country-count limit is needed. Stable page dimensions keep normalized coordinates stable. Cells have no gutters. Whole-pixel 1:1 and minified sampling stays inside the cell, and scaled variants clamp their taps to its texel centres and average the minified footprint.
