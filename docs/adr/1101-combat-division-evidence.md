# 1101. Preserve combat-division hover order and field-level refusals

Committed division rows route designated stat children to their own fixed-point formulas and every other visible child to the summary. Reserve rows ignore which child was hovered and request reinforcement. The builder clears its old fields if the entry can no longer be built. Stat hovers retain native sign and overflow, including the defender organisation line's attacker bonus formatting quirk. Fixed-point division scales with wrapping, represents a zero denominator as the callsite's sentinel, and withholds the exceptional signed quotient that would trap. Fixed-point display truncates or rounds only where the native callsite does. A committed division's icon sequence follows recorded modifiers, opposing committed armour and piercing, contextual leader, fuel, anti-air and exile. Every required unknown operand stops the icon list at that step and identifies the omitted suffix. No later frame may move into the space of an undecidable one. Only above-threshold anti-air reads the side flag. Exile operands can settle independently.

A movement invasion hover requires the amphibious route's next edge and a live directed war against the landing controller. Share canonical-slot joins across all divisions in the bind. A missing link in a live war prevents proving peace, while known war can still be reported. An incomplete input table is a named refusal. Invasion progress uses the recorded route and its first-edge operands. When an explicit source is absent, the division commander's handle, skill and traits are read independently through that hour's recorded tables. A missing commander operand does not turn an entire division opaque.

Reserve reinforcement follows native rejection order. A division that fails advancement or exceeds combat width takes a known rejection without speed-share operands. An admitted reserve computes base reinforcement, country modifiers, contextual command, initiative and the live captured speed share. Use the positive expeditionary owner when selected. A missing or refused share leaves the chance withheld rather than assuming zero. Named modifier fragments use the recorded trees and language, and only a nonzero contextual leader trait contributes its line. An unavailable fragment need not hide the known chance or the other fragment. Unsupported modifier callbacks are a named refusal. Career history is shown only for the viewing country's own divisions, and a recorded null medal is known absence. The deployed panel uses the active cache and the authenticated tactic context, and does not expose unrelated domains or a designer-only suppression witness. Its moving state and weather attrition branch applies only to a logical owner at war.
