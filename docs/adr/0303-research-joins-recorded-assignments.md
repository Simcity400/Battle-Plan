# 0303. Attribute research only within a recorded assignment

Research history uses the timeline's holder and coverage spans and the last recorded cells of each assignment. A technology is identified by its occurrence in the recorded database as well as its token. Reused tokens can have their own text and folders. A switched-out assignment retains its last state on a separate lane while completion evidence is pending. A gap or a different run or holder breaks attribution. If a new stretch follows, the last active assignment ends unknown. Presence in a new stretch cannot prove when the old assignment left its slot.

The slot panel reads the holder's most recent recorded research slice. Every slot must be in order, and a researching slot needs both the technology occurrence and token. The panel publishes that read alone so following the head sends a structural update when its attribution changes. Its live progress cell uses recorded progress divided by the same base cost as the state. The breakdown reads the requested technology only over its assignment and matches the slot, technology and completed level at each read. It holds states to the next read and merges consecutive states when only their days-left countdown changes.

Modifiers and bonuses belong to the matching owner, slot, technology and read hour. Bonuses require a complete, ordered set. The days-left estimate uses a recorded modifier breakdown or progress over a day with unchanged applied days. A witnessed assignment start can use its slot's speed. A first read without a witnessed start or a usable rate leaves the estimate missing. The breakdown uses the game's fixed-point speed arithmetic, with half-away rounding of reduced years, a denominator penalty and a minimum clamp. Sharing clamps each group before clamping the sum. A mod can define more groups, and each group's members and filters come from recorded rows. Category identity needs matching bytes as well as the recorded hash.

Grant inventory is bound to its own read. It uses its reserved scope and the slot breakdown reads the slot and shared country scopes separately. A missing recorded restriction token refuses the inventory with its name. A matching token or a declared definition cannot supply missing rows.
