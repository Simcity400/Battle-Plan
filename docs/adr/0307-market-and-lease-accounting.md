# Keep market progress and lend-lease coverage separate

An hour's market reply uses recorded offers, requests, contracts and agreements for the chosen parties. A missing section carries a named refusal. Keep the listing and agreement structure in the reply and move purchase progress and fuel movement through live cells. Equipment and civilian industry amounts use fixed5, fuel quantities use a denominator of 32768, and shares use thousandths of a percent. Resolve parties by the raw references, canonical slots and tags at the relevant hours. Preserve unattributed rows instead of treating an unresolved party as a different country. Read the naming tables and requested sections first, then the referenced variants, purchase contracts, tokens and states.

The ledger pages the declared `market-events` log. The kind facet selects the relevant request, contract, payment, purchase, delivery or other recorded occurrence. Match a country's seller and receiver facets so buying and selling both count. Preserve a delivery whose amount is unavailable as a refusal. Use the run's newest event on the page to name designs, falling back to the oldest event when necessary. Both parties' names come from the event's own hour.

Lend-lease balance aggregates cumulative equipment and fuel history across raw ids by canonical country or partner slot. A country on the war rosters with no history row has recorded zero amounts. A country absent from the rosters has no proved history. Partner histories use only spans where the tag has a holder and the table is available. Within those spans, zero contributes where a previously known partner's row is gone. Sum intersecting history pieces with a sweep over their boundaries. Unknown coverage still leaves a gap and a partner starts at its first recorded row.

For ended-agreement details, read the agreement's terms and fuel totals at its last held read. Cache the ended rows once per run. A temporary unread interval followed by the same row does not terminate the agreement. Report incomplete schedule or delivery pools by name.
