# 0904. Preserve resource timing and the route identities the map needs

Resource amounts are calculated on demand from recorded state, controller and building operands with the recorded content. Hourly slices include the daily portion, new owners, missed publications and owners marked by observed writers. Uncertified marks trigger a complete read. `StateResourceInputs` provides a row for each read state and carries the owner's read hour in `SliceReads`. The collector rereads this row hourly for states with a resource origin. An amount that depends on its supply-route flag requires the state's read hour to match the requested hour. A missing or older witness withholds the amount with a reason. A controlled resource-free state needs neither origin nor infrastructure and supply factors, because the resource product is zero. An uncontrolled state has no origin. State gates control whether additive keys apply. Infrastructure and supply factors apply only for the applicable origin and building or route witnesses. Building grants and the state cost follow the native scaled accumulation.

Daily shared-slot and energy reads carry both owner and controller so a transfer never borrows the old country's operands. Keep signed fixed5 values and distinguish known zero modifiers from refused reads. Slot availability uses local and global base and additive values with the positive-base factor and loaded cap. Factory energy uses the healthy infrastructure level and active buildings' production flags, with local and country modifiers and the stored energy use per factory level. Shared counts truncate as each game division does. Energy display rounds half away from zero. Building activity follows the recorded active-vector membership.

State resource rows are sparse over all-zero fixed5 pool and modifier values. Temporary resources are recorded when the native list or flag enables them. Controller building overrides are refreshed for the daily slice, held-back owners, changed countries and controllers of states selected for resource and modifier rows. Preserve present but empty override entries and grant only positive sums from the first matching element. Record the country's oil modifier independently. Fuel consumer entries retain the status array order and fixed5 amounts from the same read as `CountryFuel`.

Resource origins distinguish the controller's own entries from entries held by a rights holder. Record their positions, stored efficiency, route factor fixed at construction or load, effective local factor and convoy counts. Keep route bad-color evidence from the native efficiency product. Resource rights use one-based database positions and stored list order. Capture origin and rights rows hourly because the trade task refreshes them.

Convoy clients retain separate owner and counterparty order and the two delivery efficiency factors. Imports and exports use existing exchange ordinals, and other layers refuse an unknown directional position. Their routes are keyed by route class and endpoints or client, according to origin, delivery or naval supply. Naval paths connect port provinces through sea regions. Land paths connect states. A route the map does not draw has empty endpoints and path. Market grouping also keeps the native source and destination states where present. Read daily slices, new owners, route-set changes and writer marks. Fall back to every owner when those marks fail to certify coverage. Preserve the pre-dominance convoy requirement and the region weight loaded from provinces. A country on a bad naval route also needs that route's per-region efficiency for hotspot coloring. A region outside its native efficiency array has full efficiency.

Stockpile quantities for essential raid archetypes are capped at the largest recorded requirement for each archetype. The capped reading preserves the verdict and displayed shortfall while avoiding changes above the requirement. A missing essential archetype is observed no stock in a complete table. Keep licence holder and giver distinct from the variant's recorded origin links, and keep the ordered variants one licence covers. The giver's reverse list is outside the saved contract.
