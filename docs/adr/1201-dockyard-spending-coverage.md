# Dockyard spending preserves totals when attribution is incomplete

The dockyard and military-factory widgets share the production-spending renderer. Each purpose retains its authored band order and category colour across the ring and months. A current month paints through the head's hour. The view switch uses the unclipped header actions when present. Repair queues retain the game's port, priority and ship order, with hull numbers painting from their reported read hour.

Dockyard repair rates are fixed-five per game day. Integrate each run over inclusive hours and divide by the recorded day denominator. Index run ends and unknown coverage so repeated range queries avoid scanning old runs. A whole-campaign repair total starts at the first witnessed output. A missing rate contaminates its range, while known repairs can still appear without production reads. If the total is known and the design split is refused, keep each witnessed part and assign the remaining exact amount to the unattributed portion. A withdrawn design share stays unknown and marks the affected equipment subtype incomplete.

New ships, refits and repairs have separate purposes. Spending with an unrecorded purpose stays in its own part and leaves the attribution of affected chart categories incomplete, even if the unexplained signed inputs cancel. Other known purposes get their own remainder. A leaf with unresolved type content could belong to the displayed domain, so keep the missing share visible without treating rocket production as dockyard output. Known totals remain partial where only attribution was refused. A month has no known total if it lacks part coverage or could omit unresolved designs. A branch's initial month starts at the branch's first read. Preserve exact rational amounts until drawing or formatting, and round straight from the rational once, half away from zero.
