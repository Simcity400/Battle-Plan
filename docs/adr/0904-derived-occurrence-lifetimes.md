# 0904. Keep observed events distinct from changes inferred across reads

## Combat and losses

A land battle is one continuously read handle, province and nondecreasing duration counter. Use a zero duration base only when the previous hour proved it had not started. Otherwise the first read is an upper bound and supplies the base. Peaks use distinct units from complete side rosters, keeping the earliest hour at the peak. A battle ends on removal, handle reuse, finalization, or loss of complete input. A separate result-only finalizer can outlive a never-observed battle. Preserve the final observation and any separate native result with its own side resolution, losses, winner bits and equipment pools. Complete event drainage witnesses the absence of a finalizer, while missing drainage leaves finalization unknown.

For a live naval battle, keep peaks and sunk floors from complete participant reads. Units can detach, so counts are maxima across reads. A retained result can link to the battle named by its persistent native source at or before that source's hour. Refuse duplicate or out-of-order sources, incompatible construction or start, an unresolved persistent merge, or a combat unread across the admissible hours. Unknown side counts stay distinct. Leader battles separately record first participation and later results. Winner evidence requires a consistent one-side result at or after the leader's participation and a single commanded side. The aggregate reflects only read outcomes, and leader removal ends it. Trait gains distinguish an initial roster, a new observed trait and a first observation after a gap. Preserve the token spelling when read, and retain first and latest gain dates until removal. Keep formation command spans with the first observed kind and name. A new leader, lost formation or broken observation ends the span with its last observed hour.

For casualties, use a lifetime keyed by two ordered holder identities and the war relation's start hour. It keeps both sides' last observed counters. A lifetime that disappears across a gap can hide tail losses, so the cumulative row becomes partial. An unknown-attacker count requires initial zero counters and subsequent agreement on the increment across relations. A gap, mismatch or result outside the signed total's range breaks that part for the rest of the run. A division sighting after the baseline is a creation observation, while a recorded deletion witness determines loss or transfer. A division merely absent from a complete census is a disappearance. A unique covered death can supply its date, location and killer. Otherwise keep the read hour as an upper bound. Preserve the supplying hours of names, icon operands and positions and the last complete census as a lower bound for a sighting.

Air losses and aircraft bombing accumulate by resolved holder identities at the supplying read, preserving native cause or caller and the maximum lag from occurrence to read. Count only when both event and country-link inputs are available. Their unresolved side and checked-total overflow stay explicit.

## Event feed, raids and peace

The event feed retains the collector's generation and sequence for occurrences in their read hour. A preexisting backlog is dated by its original hour. Named derived kinds cover an observed country ceasing to own states, a first uncancelled guarantee and a partial or refused feed read. The incomplete kind carries coverage, pending count, script-family refusals and failure text. Keep observed country references and their witnessed identities, optional cause, token and scope evidence, and proved realized political or command power charges. A later event card links to its opener's sequence and read hour. Keep a dispatch open until a final choice or a generation change and let an immediate-effects choice leave it open. A decision opener also expires on cancellation, timeout, completion, removal or broken continuity. Keep a guarantee keyed by the participating raw references and start while the relation is present, including cancelled rows, and remember the first read that wrote it to the feed.

The raids log writes the first observed ending or qualified disappearance once. An already ended raid at baseline is an existing row. Keep the prior complete read, first exact observation where known, launch evidence and last observed position and target. A disappearance across an incomplete interval can withhold the raid operands. Freeze the target state and country at the ending read using the recorded target selection rule. Preserve the reason an unknown victim or slot cannot be resolved.

Peace outcomes are differences of complete before and after reads. Prefer a witnessed conference and distinguish an uncovered transfer from an interval whose conference presence is unknown. Preserve connected groups of changes in the same hour. Transfers identify holders at the before and after boundaries. Subject, government and released-country rows use the appropriate subject, overlord or former-state owner. The outcome may lack a province victory-point witness. A conference span retains the first and last complete roster observations of its turn and finalized flag, and the first finalization and disappearance. Keep whether the outcome is still waiting for an after read, was compared, lacks a before read or overlaps another conference.
