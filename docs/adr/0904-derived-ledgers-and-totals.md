# 0904. Separate observed quantities, partial coverage and presentation totals

## Equipment and production

Equipment loss totals identify the country slot and raw holder, the source and variant. Drains, convoys and lend-lease accumulate from their first accepted observations. The Combat Log also retains expired rows under their original keys until a return or expiry of the log's retention window. Freed lines can preserve their known amounts in the aggregate. Unknown freed amounts, overflow or a withheld drain affect coverage. A lend-lease exchange ending after its last read leaves a lower bound from its last observed interval. A country coverage row keeps land admission, units outside orders groups, resolved ownership, restarted convoy counters and ended exchanges. The run row keeps refusal and coverage independent by source, including the first and withheld drain hours and unresolved countries.

Sunk ship multiplicity is an observed count, not a sum of periodic snapshots. Use a checked signature of the recorded entry and retain the maximum witnessed multiplicity at its sinking hour. A new complete input after a gap or holder change observes the currently listed entries again. The naval loss projection separately accumulates increases between consecutive complete lists and signed convoy bucket changes. Attacker attribution needs a direct sinking witness. Tag resolution and unknown entries are evaluated at the supplying read. For each input kind preserve the complete baseline, current completeness and the first lost interval caused by retention or missing retention metadata.

Production totals keep the latest contributed output for each producer, design and purpose after its line leaves the census. Select the lowest raw country id resolving to its own slot. Unit counts stay a lower bound while a row is inexact or its last count went unseen. Applied factory IC and its unknown-purpose portion have independent coverage and reason. Keep the design and archetype spellings after the definition disappears. Quantities can be grouped by producer and type with or without refits, with a world row for totals and a distinct archetype for unclassified designs. Preserve the first missing hour and reason for units and factory IC by input source. Rocket observation also tracks its producer and resets coverage on a restart. When joining line and rocket totals use the later start and latest production read, retained by a shared clock row. Unknown type still prevents proof of an individual archetype total, and unrepresented archetypes use a distinct remainder row.

Dockyard repair rate is the in-use count times one dockyard's output. Keep total first and allocate by current selected designs only when the naval bases' counts reconcile. Otherwise the design rows are absent and the unattributed rate is unknown. Trade-flow sums pair seller and buyer holder identities with resource identity. Keep an explicit unresolved side and refuse totals outside the representable signed range.

## Fuel and construction

Build a fuel day from applied updates when their award day is known. Keep fixed5 gain and consumer receipts and Q15 transfer and burn values at their source precision. The native rolling-window totals can substitute only across the complete governing interval of the same active land-owning country. Preserve the reason applied data was unavailable and reject overlap with an already kept day. The displayed terms include applied gain sources, unidentified ring gain, the signed transfer classes and conversion and tank clamps. Demand totals preserve the native wrapping daily projection as well as widened sums of part projections, with and without clamping. A failed pack propagates its reason independently of the output's other measures.

The construction project view begins with its first queue read or a separate output witness and ends once the final state is settled. Keep the initial owner, target, cost and ordinal, current assignment and remaining work, and witnessed construction or repair increments with their dates. Distinguish observed work, work added to a queue and possible work no read accounted for. An output-only line has no queue witness. A dedicated one-hour end row retains the life summary so indexing all ends does not replay the project's entire history. Its evidence separately names whether new work or repair was seen and whether output since creation was observed. Apply counts to the holder identity, work kind and building type and state or railway endpoints. Persistent spending rows can remain lower bounds because some lines ended without exact output. Keep the explicit unknown state or type and refuse overflow.
