# 0308. Treat sparse naval rows as zero only after a complete observation

The naval loss series counts ships and convoys from the recorder's tallies by kind and attribution. Each kind has its own observed baseline. A complete read establishes zero for a holder with no row. A monthly total subtracts the count before the month, or starts at the later baseline. Missing reads and uncounted sinkings are disclosed as partial evidence. Sinking rows are dated by the first list read at or after the sinking, including entries pruned during a window. The earlier read distinguishes previously listed rows and duplicate occurrences. Names and cause text without keys stay in the recorded language. Countries and places are resolved with their dated evidence.

Naval control projects the game's recorded rule operands with the recorded content thresholds and ratios. Each day uses its last recorded hour. Within a recorded summary the viewer's row precedes any every-viewer row, and absence of both gives a known zero. A missing day or an unavailable summary produces no verdict. Consecutive days share one history read while the run and viewer remain the same. Mine totals use each owner's sparse witness length. A missing amount inside an observed vector is zero. Missing vectors, unknown owners and unread rows leave uncertainty per region. The displayed mine amounts and refit progress are live cells so the structural panels do not need a reply for hourly changes.

The yard and naval orders panels stage reads from canonical owners through fleets, queues, ships, models, designs and tokens. A repair queue preserves duplicate entries and the separate count requires a complete census. A line's hourly progress decides whether it is active. Daily line metadata can describe limits and targets when present. The panel uses the recorded ship name first. If that is unavailable, it can reconstruct the name from the design's group and ordinal or its recorded override. The design or class can stand in for an unavailable name with the reason. Figures derived from unavailable or sentinel values stay unavailable.
