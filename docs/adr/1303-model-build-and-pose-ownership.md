# 1303. Prepare model resources off the display thread

An interactive frame queues a missing part and continues without its figure while the build runs. A later frame installs the successful part or reports a named refusal. A settled capture builds a needed part in place to expose the exact scene. A worker failure is named and stays refused so a render does not wait on work that will never finish. The loader can also warm the lit, forced and caster variants together with their geometry. A mesh's queue admission becomes immutable once all its nodes are known. Its empty lit queue can then be skipped because the other lit call sees the same scene and makes the same pose and refusal requests.

A descriptor publishes its successful binding program and optional reflected lookup recipe together after all parts succeed. Source order, offsets and dynamic bindings come from the effective program that actually bound. Missing inputs retain ordered diagnostics. A failed reservation for a recipe or installed route lookup uses the normal direct lookup and demand path. Reused poses during one route walk are owned and built for the exact rig the route reaches. A refused later part leaves prior candidates cold and does not commit a new material snapshot.

A visual mod entity keeps a separately baked counterpart from the recording. Animation and mesh replacements that refuse can fall back individually to the recorded content, named. A later draw refusal switches the entity to its recorded counterpart when one exists. Placement, hidden state and animation follow the original slot, while reach, material technique and shader bindings come from the counterpart that actually draws. A refusal with no usable counterpart remains visible by name. The same content stack can append rigs without replacing live histories. Merging a new shard rebases its rig and the rigid and skinned references into the common palette.

A persistent worker receives one pose batch at a time using recycled sample storage. Limbs depend on the rig and the player's sampled state; repeated placements reuse the same pose. The display never waits for a batch. It uses the newest compatible completed set and poses a missing actor where it is read. The history generation excludes old batches after a seek or branch change. A stopped exact picture poses from its own samples and makes no worker request. The diagnostic measures pose age and names the pass without failing the frame. A refused worker leaves the exact local pose path available.

The independent fixtures compare these paths without relying on GPU allocation addresses or approximate float equality. Resource, retained state and sharing comparisons use a stable bijection across frames. Signed zero, NaN payloads, equal poses backed by different retained clips, and visually ordered alpha draws expose weaker comparisons.
