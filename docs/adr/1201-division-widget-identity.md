# Division widgets preserve native identity and observation time

Division equipment is a recorded whole-panel read, with a separate drill panel that can answer a different hour from the overview. A drill holds the native template or division handle under the recording revision and exits when its scope changes. A refusal in the parent stays visible with the drill open. Use the authored archetype as a tagged key so a real type cannot collide with the unclassified bucket. A type's fill sums each division's holding capped at its own need and divides by summed need. Surplus in one division cannot make up for another's missing equipment. Unknown or absent needs stay distinct from a known fill.

The division log draws blocks of dated rows and paints counts from bins and held rows as the clock reveals them. A row is positioned at its supplying read. Its event date is exact only for a dated death and otherwise bounded by the read. The current hour's partially drawn bin uses loaded rows; pending pages cannot supply a total. An unavailable saved template filters out all templates rather than selecting every type. The country selection includes the tags that holders used at their rows' hours. Prefetch names for the newest held block and nearby rows, ask only visible rows outside it, lead with the visible order, and deduplicate place plus read hour. Keep in-flight keyed reads across page and block changes. Distinguish a missing province, a pending read, a failed read and an answer that omitted the place.

The service-record body is shared between the standalone widget and the Most decorated divisions drill. The automatic subject is the highest-ranked eligible division at the ranking's default sort, when it has a medal. Check that the sort accessor still matches that default at compile time. Preserve repeated record entries and their order. A held record tells consumers its own read hour during replacement. Settings fetch in-service divisions at one hour when opened rather than refreshing every clock frame.

A template roster follows the displayed native holder. Draw its current templates in roster order and collect obsolete ones in a collapsed group. Battalion count excludes support companies, and the priority label comes from the recorded content stack. A drawer request captures the read hour when the row or tooltip opens and names the full native handle. It uses the renderer's loading, refusal and diagnostics states so its failure remains distinct from the roster's.
