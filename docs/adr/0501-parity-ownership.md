The parity adapter owns a Windows Job whose handle closes when the supervisor dies. Descendants inherit Job membership even when a browser or encoder detaches. The retained process and Job handles are the shutdown authority. Require the Job before launching Edge, remove Playwright's own exit listener, and guard child-process APIs against PID-tree termination. A Playwright shutdown request goes through the Job stop signal. Publish capture results before reaping the Job, then dispose of the private profile.
