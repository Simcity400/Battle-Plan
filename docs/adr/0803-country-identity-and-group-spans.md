# Encode country identity and presentation changes as spans

A country identity is its canonical slot and raw country ID, witnessed by a Countries row and a link resolving to its own slot. A raw alias names the unique identity at the slot it resolves to. Null or unlinked references and slots with multiple resolving holders have no unique identity. Tag holders choose the first resolving country in raw ID order and preserve an unresolved holder when the tag exists without a resolving country. Incomplete country or link tables close the live tags and open a span naming the missing evidence.

Tag holder, appearance and group rows represent intervals. Their keys distinguish an epoch, so removing and later restoring the same fact creates a new span. Number tag epochs in tag order so insertion order and resumes cannot change persisted row keys. Each appearance span refers to a stable encoded fact for the holder's map colour, name operands, reachable overlord operands and flag inputs. Playback localizes names with recorded content and uses the flag operands to choose artwork. Required appearance inputs can withhold the whole history, with an unknown span standing in until they return.

Group spans hold human players, factions and wars. Faction names carry the literal and token operands and the recorded spelling when present. Order zero identifies the faction leader. A missing roster or unresolved country leaves the relevant history unobserved, while members positively identified can retain their spans. A war continues until its witnessed end falls due, even when no relevant row is rewritten in that hour. War starts and capitulations use their recorded event dates and enter the log only once. Group resumption reconstructs watched inputs from the held tables before updating the spans.
