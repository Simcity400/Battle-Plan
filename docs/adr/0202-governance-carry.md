# Refresh governance tables from authenticated owner changes

## Cadence and assembly

Governance prepares each family's capacity from its own live headers. A sibling hint failure does not block that family, and capacity hints certify no data. Native readers belong to the owning acquisition thread. Research inputs, applied bonuses and modifiers run as a separate hourly job over an allocation-free loan, restored before the frame can be read or published. The host can delegate Focus for the same callback. Cases where the mode omits tables skip their native walks. Politics keeps the ActiveIdeas and PartyLeaders dependencies that certify its election cache. Accepted job failures withdraw partial rows, keep genuine cadence skips and preserve a separately delegated Focus payload.

An hourly research slice includes new or unpublished owners and writer-marked countries. It reads every country on a full or uncertified hour. Technology selection uses the same rule and publishes definitions for the templates its rosters reach. Focus reads every country after the daily tick and on full hours, and otherwise takes new, unpublished and marked owners. Owning a state determines whether an unmarked owner's daily progress can drift. Capture checks that at the slice or boundary and shares the result between readers. A carry reads only drift terms and reports the same failure a full walk would publish. It keeps a technology roster only with the corresponding complete research slot rows. Cost and modifier defines stay common to all research rows in a read, including their refusal outcomes.

The frozen serial drift oracle comes from `eafc953b4f`. It preserves full-walk ordering for status, roster headers, banked days and assigned technology progress. It deduplicates a technology assigned to multiple slots and associates each slot ordinal with its indexed output row. An incomplete or otherwise unholdable read drops its certificate. With relaxed read ordering, a refused advisory gather can fall back to a bounded serial read. A persistent fault still takes the first reason the serial walk would report. Tests compare transient and persistent outcomes, complete replacements and partial failures against the serial reader.

The acquisition thread owns definition selection and certificate checks. FIFO decode assembles already copied technology values while the frame retains their text. The spool thread owns portable conversion and variable-length copies. Shared-walk measurements are charged to the leading table once. Worker-grown storage remains demand for recycled frames, independently of hook sizing. Native power-balance modifier sources are recorded for replay derivation. An optional diagnostic samples native description trees for a differential check.

## Politics and dynamic modifiers

Politics takes writer marks before registering or reading any owner in the walk. A certified quiet hour can publish the preceding complete four-table walk without opening a new one. In other hours it authenticates the country and status and checks array and pointer identities. Per-country hold matches the status and array headers and the certificates of the listed parties and leader characters. Registration precedes reading each party or character, so replacing one at the same address invalidates its hold. Per-country party carry takes popularity and the ruling flag afresh. ActiveIdeas relies on the current epoch and unchanged array entries, whose database tokens are fixed for the session. Duplicate status entries and unavailable advisory storage cannot authorize carry. A complete table can retain its rows even when the sibling table's walk is incomplete. A failed job forgets the hold and the next walk reads afresh.

Dynamic modifiers join the container sequence to the country recalculation and appearance-reset observations. They refresh daily and when a writer marks an owner. A read can publish current data without entering the held store when a writer overlapped it. Each complete census drops departed countries, and a poisoned store discards the interrupted hold. Relative token and value offsets retain the source row spans when appended into the frame's arenas.
