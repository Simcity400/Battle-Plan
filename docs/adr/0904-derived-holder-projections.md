# 0904. Project derived counts through the recorded holder identity

Compile the derived table schemas apart from recorded tables. The recorder's output uses the current country links to attribute a fact to a canonical slot and preserves the raw id when it needs to distinguish holders of a reused slot. A tag's holder is the first country by raw id resolving to its canonical slot. Its key includes the fact and a run-wide epoch, so a row lasts one stretch of holder identity. Incomplete country or link input leaves a named unknown stretch. Tag names and distinct rendered look facts get append-only dictionaries, and look spans carry one contiguous interpretation of holder, colour, naming and flags.

Group history uses the same span model. Group zero is the human roster, and faction identities and names get append-only numbers. Keep membership and roster order, leader, name and presence as separate facts. A war pair is ordered by canonical slot. Unresolved roster entries and unobserved human, faction and war intervals stay explicit. Engine-dated war starts and capitulations live in the recorder's read hour. Retain the instigating and other parties for a war and the capitulated country for surrender. A faction account retains the last explicit faction for the same holder within continuous hours. An ambiguous faction or changed holder ends the account projection.

Army totals and battalions separately attribute a division to its expeditionary or roster owner and to its host. Air totals keep roster and effective owners using a positive country override when present. Negative whole-plane operands withhold the associated count. Split aircraft counts only when a wing's equipment establishes the split exactly. A wing composition is an ordered encoding of variants with positive quantities. Its code is assigned once and lives while any wing has that composition. Ships and their hull values keep roster and logical owner and may also aggregate by class. An unresolved owner, missing task force, negative hull value or missing model counter withholds the affected measure. Deployed manpower uses native wrapping sums and accounts for contribution, fleet crew, ordinary and exile wings, volunteers and expeditionaries with their own roster rules.

Military aid keeps channels and counts live divisions hosted by the recipient. Its separate native tally uses the first listed count per volunteer host. Incomplete channels and unresolved senders or hosts carry explicit unknowns. Supply health follows the recorded native icon classification for each owner. The three counts are unavailable together where ownership or an input to a division classification is unknown. Stockpile rows preserve a zero held at a list position and sum full variant handles across raw holders. A missing link can invalidate the projection and an overflowing total stays unknown.

Division equipment compares each read pool with its own template and tracks unknown comparisons and unresolved owners. Keep the daily pool and stockpile witness hours independently. Type-level `filled` caps each division's amount at that division's requirement. Count known shortages separately from absent operands and preserve unknown designs. Template totals retain separate counters for unavailable manpower and hit points and give a reason when the template's need is unknown.

Territory credits ownership, control and occupation separately. Add a world row with the appropriate counting rule, and keep a recorded zero for live countries without territory. Territory within a strategic region counts each province and victory point once and apportions states by their exact province fraction. Unknown holder, overlapping or absent region, missing map area and unrepresentable fractions preserve their own gaps. Faction totals use the observed members' own territory and factory measures and withhold an affected measure if a member or the roster is unknown.

Occupation counts active occupied states along with garrisoned states. Complete held-state coverage needs a complete garrison read, resolved controllers and a garrison for each occupied state. Unknown active state control withholds the aggregate. Garrison strength uses the recorded equipment, manpower and content powers with a named refusal for a missing input. Nuclear totals keep a row for each live country, use the native whole-bomb and ready-queue values, and wrap the whole-bomb sum as the game does.
