# 1203. Reconstruct research journeys within their observation bounds

Read history once per recording, branch and country and share it between research widgets. Clip outcomes, assignments and slot openings to the displayed hour. A missing current sample leaves the older history usable. A slot that opens during the recording starts where the other lanes had reached, while a slot first observed after a gap may have an unknown start. Keep each gap once in the chronological list. Index the immutable history so an advancing clock can reveal a row without creating new rows or keyboard identities for unchanged lanes. Reuse technology art as rows come into view. Keep folder colors and order from the whole history, and key images and lookups by the recorded template index as well as token. Quoted tokens need lossless keys and two definitions with the same token can be different technologies. Refuse by name when the missing index prevents disambiguation. Accept any number of slots or spans within the reply budget.

The head follows pushed slot structure and paints progress, days left and saved days from live values. A pinned or hovered hour reads that hour, and doctrines fetch when playback stops or steps. The days-left estimate counts down from its recording sample. Unknown live progress can fall back to that slot's latest recorded sample, with its own hour. A slot can name a technology with no corresponding assignment. Its progress and completion stay unknown. Never interpolate an assignment's progress across gaps or mix two assignments. Hover and keyboard focus use the same tooltip, whose date and start can be upper bounds when a start is not proven.

When an explanation opens, read every assignment used through its observed end, or through the selected hour for active research. An explanation stays scoped to that opening hour. A completed journey is keyed by immutable source history and its observation bound. Later assignments, new discoveries and unrelated history updates do not enter its calculation. The earliest joined assignment is the navigation target. Follow assignments through slots only within one continuous country and recorded technology identity and unambiguous observation. Refuse joins across gaps, repeats and overlaps. A slot being assigned is insufficient evidence of a start. It must follow an adjacent unassigned sample and progress accounted for by witnessed credits and at most one daily update. Otherwise a prior assignment may account for earlier work.

Apply or refund saved days at the assignment's stored rate. At a shared boundary process refunds before credits. Initial progress already contains credits on its sample hour. Include only subsequent changes in the reconciliation. Integrate recorded rates in fixed5 day-hours, reevaluating the ahead-of-time penalty each game day. This estimates continuous work and does not identify the phase of the discrete native daily update. At 1×, earned work plus time added by the ahead penalty minus modifier savings equals elapsed hours. Additive modifier sources retain their own contribution. Keep scientist efficiency together because its sources are not additive. A gap, progress reset, missing modifiers, unexplained surplus or changed base cost prevents a complete split.

The explanation compares expected time to time used on one axis. Use `base + penalty - bonus - credits - earlier - remaining = taken`, and show time off-slot separately. Earlier progress is a cause only where the start was not witnessed. A residual under one fixed5 day can be accounted for by rounding and the daily update phase. Keep clamped time distinct and retain full modifier segments even if opposing modifiers overlap. Perform arithmetic before presentation rounding. Reuse the hover segment key only while the segment survives a new view.

The artwork-hook tests replace React dispatch only and cover the actual dated stores, request queue, envelope and reply validation together.
