# Air and research widgets keep history, measurements, and absence separate

Aces and agency comparison follow event-driven panels. Their whole replies determine render identity because their counts change with events. Pinned and hovered views read the requested hour. The ace roster includes an unidentified country only in the all-country view. Its status sort uses the first observed ace date, with unrecorded dates last. A known-empty group short-circuits before reading a historical log.

In the agency table, a name sort requires every chosen country's name and the visible-row flag reads can stay bounded. A head push with identical names and order can reuse those reads. Raw intel is opt-in. While playback runs, the detail keeps its last read hour, which avoids requesting a new detail every hour. A paused detail requests the shown hour and holds the last reply until it arrives. A country is selectable there even when it has no agency.

Active research reads one structure per set of open slots and paints progress, estimates and saved days from live cells. The history's technology tokens are requested together so starting another known technology during playback needs no catalogue read. An opened drill identifies the assignment by its first observation and scopes it to the recording, branch and country. At a recording gap the last observed open slots can be shown as unavailable. Closed slots stay closed. A positive ahead-of-time penalty refers to the technology's authored year.

The air mission panel renders when its regions, registered missions or theatres change. Counts paint from the reported read hour and a wing is counted once per region. A recorded region with no wing on a mission is known to have none there. The same region can have unknown counts for a registered mission. Region drills read the hour when the user opens them, follow with the parent or stay pinned, and use the recording's mission names and order. A selected choice absent at an hour retains its ID. Air statistics use paired channels from the recording schema, with the home channels preceding their enemy counterparts. Fighter losses are the initial channel. Game labels lose their trailing colon for chart display. A statistic retains its exact recorded word and display scale for labels. Only bar geometry uses a numeric approximation. A selectable region can occur anywhere in the country's timeline even when the current hour has no record for it.

Air superiority sorts looked-up regions independently of their painted live shares. Resizing repaints the labels at their new available width immediately. Its timeline reads historical cells on a fixed 256-day grid so a complete tile has a reusable request key. During playback the end of the view reaches to the end of the current tile, then follows the exact head while paused. Rankings remain visible while a replacement loads. Tiles retain regions already named in the view so reshuffling the ranking does not reread them. The current day reads all selected regions' live scores and paints those shown without a structural render. A missing cell is a gap and a not-yet-read tile is loading. A cell and its day's omissions both contribute to what is missing. Equal runs reuse a cell projection and each tile keeps its own sides.
