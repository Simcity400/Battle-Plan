# Recover event feed owners privately and publish with exact receipts

The event feed copies callback witnesses into an hourly arena tagged by frame and generation. A native return consumes only its frame's witnesses in order. Resetting the arena at a boundary makes a frame from an older generation explicitly refused. A witness still unconsumed after the writer thread reaches the boundary identifies an abandoned native observation and makes the interval unavailable.

An occurrence keeps its scopes even when a referenced country cannot be identified. A name copy crossed by a rebuild refuses names while keeping the captured identities. Decision flag events compare the staged value from before the native store with the committed value, suppressing repeated sets. A real result contributes both the occurrence and, for a certified consistent owner, the retained delta. Political balance changes reuse operands forwarded by their existing writer.

Decision and delayed-event registries use native addresses for acquisition and compact labels in the recording. Labels remain monotonic in a world even when all observations retire. A lost writer can have replaced any pointer, so the worker clears native attribution before retrying a census. Revocation with complete observations retires only certificates, preserving exact attribution. Native resets end the members' lifetime even for an uncertified owner. A refusal applies to its owner and registry. Repeats count without emitting another marker until a completed baseline, a reset or a loss re-arms it. An exact member can still carry an occurrence while its owner waits for a baseline. Only a certified owner's consistent member can contribute a retained delta.

The decision index ties each member to its owner's identity and epoch. A member transfer updates that attribution even while the owner needs another baseline. Retirement removes the old member so address reuse cannot inherit a label. A certified owner missing an exact member indicates an inconsistency, and an event without reliable attribution is an interval refusal. A delayed-event retirement outside its iteration pass provides no owner attribution. A retirement within a pass uses the stable identity and world from that pass. A timer absent from an uncertified owner's index can refuse that owner's queue using its census identity, without reading the queue.

Recovery uses the existing census and prepares one owed owner and registry at a time. A certified decision owner needs at most its scalar political balance, and a certified delayed queue needs no native queue traversal. The balance stays updated through observed revocation. The writer cut covers world and identity acquisition as well as content reads. The cut certifies observation only between writers and is checked without waiting. Crossed samples or failed allocations retry at the next eligible boundary. A stable native failure names the census owner without reading a failed header again. The first registry can publish atomically before the second is prepared. Failure in the latter leaves the earlier certificate intact.

Native reads and allocation occur during private preparation. The measured budget includes unacknowledged entries, prior owners and refusal controls for both registries across the remaining census. The census-wide bound avoids a per-owner reserve ledger. Preflight installs exactly the measured offer. A growth failure keeps the baseline uncommitted and uses an already prepared local refusal control when available. A missing control keeps the refusal pending. Ordinary occurrences and complete baselines cannot spend the refusal reserve. A decision write may attach to an exact member after an observed revocation and refuse only its owner. Prepared pointer indexes keep probe room before admitting a member. The delayed index closes its probe chain at erasure so obsolete timers do not leave tombstones that accumulate through a campaign.

The delayed census keys owners by canonical slot and already supplies the pointer, country ID and vtable. The baseline also reads the surrender target under its cut. A crossed sample can then try to read the target purely to name the refusal. If it remains unreadable, the owner still retries without emitting an unnamed marker. Failed preparation and repeated refusals also keep recovery pending. A native reset gives the census a fresh queue to baseline.

At each boundary the worker first processes exact receipts and establishes the world and registry state, then performs due owner work and seals a pointer-free hourly publication. Allocation of the output and its receipt storage occurs outside the shared lease. A receipt identifies its own seal. Matching acknowledgements retire entries, discarded publications re-enter the unpublished tail, and in-flight entries keep their ownership. Batching ascending receipts takes one entry pass. A publication already in flight cannot acknowledge a newer observation loss. A baseline produced by the publication itself can survive its initial partial interval, while an earlier certificate is withdrawn by a later input or forwarder gap. Bootstrap waits for the loss publication's receipt before starting the census. Compaction can reclaim settled storage during a continuous event stream only when no ticket can refer to a moved entry.

Recovery tests cover offers sized to the measured budget while previous publications remain unacknowledged. A prior larger unmet storage demand remains visible to presizing without refusing a baseline that fits. Failure injection uses separate allocation-denial scopes because the presizing helper has its own guard.
