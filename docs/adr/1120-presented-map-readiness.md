# Report the map that was presented

The host's requested branch, hour, country and mode describe a target. The viewer reports the hour and mode of the accepted picture. A layer can still draw its previous product after a step has been applied. The lag report names the furthest-behind layer at its drawn hour. A picture ahead of the clock is still loading and has no lag to report. A paused or stepped frame is ready when its drawn layers, overlay, counters and pending GPU installs agree at its target. The window that accepts a layer's publication also runs its install so a tight frame budget alone cannot hold its picture back.

A hidden window can run only the logic pass. It releases its map reader so dashboard readers can take a seek while nothing is drawing. A widget can also lose its reader when scrolled out while its playhead survives. A seek without a reader stays owed until a replacement joins. Anchors share the seek counter, including later play, pause, rate and step messages. This allows the replacement to take the seek exactly once and start forward or backward play there.

A branch change or a visual-content rebuild replaces the old map reader. A picture settling a new hour retains its loading resources until the last layer catches up. Forward playback across continuous history keeps day and night and battle audio continuous. A seek, backward step, change of branch or viewing country, or crossing a gap starts them afresh.

The recorded limitations belong to the drawn document and its layers. An unavailable viewing-country vision table withholds the overlay and names its reason even when diagnostics are off. A drawn document with an undeclared distant battle effect silences battle audio and reports the refusal. Readback works from the registered native scene texture. Eframe keeps ownership of managed uploads and frees, and subsequent window paint rewrites its own renderer buffers. Readback preserves fixed opaque colour endpoints through gamma conversion and dithering. Export success requires a completed write, and retired scenes must register the replacement texture even when its generation matches a former target.
