# Keep counter derivation off the display frame

The counter-kind registry has one owner for each kind. Whole-hour layout omits kinds served by the active layers. Publish only products made for the currently displayed vision and suppress all of them while composition or a vision refusal leaves that perspective unresolved. Supply the counter layers with the matching type-icon relations, gun destination hostility and branch's daily air rosters. A newly opened handle to the same branch's roster history does not invalidate the existing answers.

Land sources contain division members in the occupant order and resolve order-group memberships from recorded orders and plans. A partial province-order census establishes no authoritative order. Keep a reverse index from the vision province each division actually read, including when that is the division's prior location. Province and unit marks update just those readers. A change in viewing country, fog, or sight epoch invalidates the whole sight product. A camera admission change activates deferred outside-view division and province marks, keeping member derivation proportional to admitted work. Updates to strength or orders can rebuild only the affected part. The action of a division owned by the viewer uses movement, the destination controller's relation to its logical owner, transfer-combat membership and attacker evidence. Record what each action consulted so unrelated writes cannot force all actions to be recomputed. Resolve strategic-redeployment layouts in the recording's executable-table scope, including post-seek composition on another thread. Fixed-point values stored at a display step are converted back to native units before joining. Route words can borrow an aligned byte cell or use a copy, keeping the unsafe alignment justification beside the operation.

The window's land and base counter workers own copies of their mirror columns, seeded from the compose. Send an ordered frame job when the view, vision, relations or applied rows change. The land worker copies changed vision bytes and shares relation data. Both workers settle the jobs they have received together and stamp the resulting product at the newest applied hour. Generations discard products from earlier composes. Playing frames take what is ready. A whole settle obtains its own hour's result. Worker-owned work contributes its stamped publication hour instead of the side's carried work. Preserve product identity when no visible source changed. When one does change, replace the allocation and copy only its affected shards, because the layout recognizes a new product by allocation identity.

Base counters keep positive standing aircraft totals, registered wings with aircraft, registrations naming absent wing rows, and reverse links from wings to registrations and bases. Radar groups are keyed by both state and raw country, and controller changes affect the indexed host states. These joins let a single changed wing, base, radar group, state or raw-country link revisit the affected sites instead of the full census. Census and viewing-country changes can sweep those sites in bounded units. Update placements before rebuilding the affected sites. A product still missing part of a step stands at the preceding hour. Country-controlled provinces, base boxes and radar evidence use the same builders as the full-hour path. Strategic location icons, air volunteer allowances, naval bases, naval HQ and facility icons are separate from this base product.

Regional air counters are drawn in strategic air mode. Admission uses registered wings in holder order. Read the last 24 hours of combat records, invalidating a region when registrations, its holder, or the oldest record's eligibility changes. Cache own and foreign record vectors and decode them only for a region drawing missions. A failed registration is reported in stable registration order. A worker asks whether the recorded daily pass visited the viewed country and withholds alerts that need the answer until it arrives. The lookup key includes branch, pass hour and country, and the pending region stays associated with it. A missing Ground Crews table can produce a refusal whose wording includes the current hour, so those regions are marked as the head moves through the gap even when the other columns are unchanged. The proof comparison counts Ground Crews separately when the full-hour builder has no localized hover.

Railway gun counters run the recorded class-13 decision over the whole gun census, including off-camera guns, so first-fit membership agrees with the full-hour product. Withhold membership when the census is incomplete. Order comes from a complete province-unit table. A recorded rail operand is current only at the hour of its row sample. Rather than scan rail rewrites every hour, mark guns whose previous result was on rail when the head moves, and mark affected provinces when rail rows appear or disappear. Gun positions remain in the camera-bound layout, and the proof compares the counter decision before that layout. Token names are resolved through the recording's lexical table and invalidated on a change affecting the token.

`provinces` can borrow the byte slice as words because all 32-bit patterns are valid `u32` and `align_to` yields a correctly aligned middle. Borrow that middle only when there is no unaligned prefix or suffix and the target is little-endian. Otherwise decode into the caller's retained buffer.
