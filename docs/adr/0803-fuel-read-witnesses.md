# Publish fuel days and demand from their own sampled evidence

At a stamped `CountryFuel` read, prefer the applied-update sums if they cover the preceding 24 updates for that read and observation began before the first update. Otherwise the fuel ring can provide the day only when every sample from read minus 24 through read minus one confirms the same canonical identity owned a state. The update carrying an hour's stamp uses the preceding sample's state. An observed sample proving that it did not update takes precedence over an unknown sample. Missing samples, incomplete ownership inputs or an unresolved owner that could affect the answer leave ownership unknown. Run starts have missing prior samples.

Where fuel days overlap, prefer the read in the daily slot. An off-slot read can keep its day until a slot read within 23 hours supersedes it; the later row names the overlap. An off-slot read within 23 hours of an already retained day cannot claim it again. No row is written on a fuel table cell update without a current read stamp. Save the bounded ownership changes, continuous-observation start, and retained and pending days, and rebuild current country and state maps from the tables on resume.

Fuel demand groups the fuel consumption pack and its parts in the game's army, air and navy order. Preserve its wrapping native rate sum and project it over a day. Separately sum the known daily part projections in widened arithmetic, both signed and clamped to nonnegative contributions. A family with missing part values has an unavailable native result while independently known families remain available. A stale pack stamp, rejected or missing pack, or parts that cannot reconstruct its lists withholds the pack with its reason. A new table read can invalidate a pack even when its header remained unchanged. Resume rebuilds the part indexes from the held tables.
