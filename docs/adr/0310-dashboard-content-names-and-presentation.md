# 0310. Bind dashboard names and presentation to the observation

Use the recording's content definitions and dated naming operands. A resource declaration keeps its identity by recorded index, even if a mod repeats its token. Keep named parts of an artwork reply when its description or thumbnail is missing. Modifier formats and localisation carry the viewer's language when available. Production names and art come from the variant and organisation at the reply's hour. A shared image can appear once while several variants point to it. Naval yards use the recorded ship name first, then a name rebuilt from recorded operands and content, then its design or class when no name resolves.

Flag sizes select their own authored atlas. The full, medium and small atlases search the corresponding normal, medium and small paths. The shield follows the game's authored top-bar treatment. The panel style keeps the shield's texture while evening the broad edge shading. Aircraft catalogue entries distinguish named aircraft candidates, bare meshes with no attached states or animation, and references whose descriptors were never registered. An unrestricted catalogue includes each of those entries with its provenance. Views that look up recorded art keep distinct designs that happen to share a name.

The map stores scoped overrides and resolves omitted choices through parent settings. Audio retains fractional volume values and only the focused map plays them. Hidden counters remain available to hover and selection. The recording's mod list retains both recorded identity and display name. Auxiliary style runs carry the country's flag and style across observed hours so a replay position can look them up directly.

Leader history follows the recorded holder and dates command, training and the start evidence of each combat. The selector's last reply can carry its last observed name and owner. Dated expenditure and political names use the naming operands at the relevant payment or observation. Law, subject and idea labels resolve through content and keep known identity separate from unknown entitlement or missing metadata.
