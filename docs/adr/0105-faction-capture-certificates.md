# 0105. Select faction capture from independently verified writers

The faction visitor authenticates each roster entry once. Accounts are writer-selected independently of membership and join country data by canonical slot, since the simulation retains distinct aliases for one country. Pending notifications transfer ownership on take. The collector retains the marks and their scalar operands until a quiet read can publish them, and checks the current array geometry and receiver identity so foreign-world or replaced storage cannot authorize a read. A nullable completed goal uses the goal token when available and retains its null sentinel otherwise.

Account callbacks update preallocated scalar lanes without allocating or following pointers. The worker closes admission before swapping owned storage and retains the previous allocation while any admitted flight can still address it. Arithmetic callbacks capture only operands at their witnessed stores. Getter temporaries and foreign receivers cannot authorize a change. The authenticated setter consumes the complete operand set on the same thread. Missing sourcing still retains the scalar store that actually ran. Operand versions and the reached refresh boundary stay together until publication. Passive stores witness the native increment rather than infer it from the balance. A new faction can be ranked before its first pool census, and ranking resolves to the current canonical slot. The two subject-side accumulator carries share the source recalculation/reset revision but use independent demanded point keys, so consuming another writer's dirty stream cannot make them current.

Advisor, goal, and companion stores retain independent owner certificates. A fresh owner read uses its own certificate so an unrelated writer does not shift its identity. When an owner was carried solely under the family-wide token, that token must remain quiet through the end of the entire walk. A change in owner geometry refuses even a forced read that already chose to walk all owners. An unregistered owner authorizes neither a native read nor reuse. Goal progress writers can update owned occurrence values without revisiting definitions, slot geometry, dates, or histories. Structural writers use the owner geometry. Rows and refusals carry under the same structural certificate, and the spool and encoder borrow whole-table buffers which the collector repopulates only after they release them. Sorting by row key keeps publication independent of the existing roster's iteration order.

Ordinary faction pool acquisition uses demand and scalar operands rather than scans of native account and resource arrays. A phase guard spans the original refresh and its parallel children. It reads the demanded owner's count and takes arithmetic results from witnessed operations. Extraction copies final registered amounts once at the caller's completed rebuild. The reserved null-resource slot survives refresh, and foreign helpers and traffic do not mark a registered cell. Scalar overlap can hold operand publication without invalidating authenticated owner lifetimes.

Observer watch tables remain live until their patch owners are restored and admitted flights drain. The goal tests serialize mutations of the process-wide table and restore the original callbacks after a test. Busy publication is retryable when a native call remains in flight, including when it is part of a shared attach round.
