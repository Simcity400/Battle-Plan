# 1204. Use dated war and contribution observations

The Wars timeline and War Contribution widget use recorded observations to resolve names, sides and dates. Within a war, a gap leaves its name and roster unknown. Once the war has ended, its last known label and orientation can name the finished war. A last observation without a witnessed departure or peace supplies a date bound. Same-name wars gain their start dates, and participation is continuous across a native holder's recorded tag changes. War IDs are scoped to the recording, branch and viewpoint, so expansion survives a refetch within that scope only.

War and country views share a prepared membership index and use their own overlap slots. Reserving slots within each connected overlap keeps parallel wars visible without reordering during playback. Building the runs visits observed memberships and uses deterministic sorts. The viewport draws only started runs and asks for artwork and names of visible lanes. Fixed colours need all stable participant identities and a complete colour batch. The branch-clipped holder boundary prevents the colour of a later tag holder from supplying an earlier holder. Faction colours require a strict majority of distinct participants, including factionless allies in the denominator.

While following, the timeline renders at changes in the observed lanes or at a step in the growing axis. The playhead and shown-hour reporting run on the frame clock even when the lanes are empty. Tooltips use that clock for the end of an ongoing run and admit casualties or contributions only for the hovered hour. Missing side orientation retains the native side arrays. Unattributed casualty amounts have a separate row.

War Contribution defaults to the most populated observed war at the chosen hour, preferring active wars, and keeps an explicit selection while it is present. An ended war reads its last observed hour. If several native groups fit one war's sides, the largest fitting group supplies its contributions. Scores stay in signed fixed-point units for exact text. Geometry shows each member's magnitude, outlines negative values and hatches unattributed casualties. Side-share bars fill independently, while total bars compare the two sides in each term because terms have different units. A missing member contributes no recorded amount and the group's reason remains available. A signed share is undefined when the side total is zero. On the head, numbers paint from the pushed reply and the widget renders on structural changes.

The War Goals panel keeps the last accepted snapshot and labels its own hour while a new read loads. Fixed goal terms determine render structure. Justification points and completion dates paint from pushed data, and expiry text follows the playback clock. The progress bar uses a clamped share of cost alongside the exact recorded points.
