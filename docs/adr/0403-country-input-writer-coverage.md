# 0403. Combine per-country writer certificates with periodic reads

Country inputs include stability, research, focus, projects, appearance, counters and visibility. A notification names the country whose retained inputs need a fresh read. A periodic read covers per-country drift when the game changes the same field as part of its normal schedule. Per-read context such as world tension, defines, language and modifier keys is collected independently.

## Stability

Effects and commands, explicit setters and the weekly update write stability and war-support bases. The weekly update also owns propaganda penalties. Appearance reset and load can replace these values. Read ruling-party popularity and coastal protection in the daily slice. The politics family forwards the any-hour popularity writers. Interception bookkeeping adds to its air-statistics column through the adder, while the history family observes the daily worker ring advancement. War flags read owned relation membership, instigator state and exile host. Relation replacement writes membership for both endpoints. Instigator and defender setters can write after linking. Exile assignment and link clearing change the hosting link. Daily return from exile belongs to the daily slice, and the relation family forwards diplomacy reset.

Country modifier recalculation is the runtime accumulator rebuild. Dynamic modifier addition, removal, expiry and value recalculation reach it in the same call. Deferred refresh requests from building changes, research completion and wartime ally rebuilding are observed when the pre-hourly refresh pass applies them. Appearance reset clears the accumulator. Separate notifications cover ruling-party choice, leader vectors and traits, active ideas, advisor modifier rebuilds and appointed-advisor membership. Appointment recalculates modifiers. Unappointment is observed on its own because retirement, capture, deferred work and status changes can remove advisors without recalculation.

## Research

Technology assignment and synchronization own slot membership. Daily synchronization updates the roster if the slot count changes, and banked idle days drift. Research technology fields change through classification and level updates, assignment and completion, reset, limited-bonus claim or release, design and experience boost assignment, load or transfer, database reload and fresh initialization. Daily slot progress is a separate drift input. The progress adder marks direct technology-setting and stolen-bonus effects. Assignment, console and GUI paths also reach their existing observed writers.

Technology rows read classifier, level, ahead reduction, applied bonus ids and researched and available technology lists. Propagation matters when the classifier changes. Claim, release and assignment change ahead reduction. Transfer, reset and level writes affect researched membership. Initialization and rebuild publish the technology roster. Limited-bonus and doctrine-cost entries append with their recorded values and remain fixed until reset or destruction.

Modifier inputs also include category and scripted sources reached by country modifier recalculation, idea additions reached by the forwarded idea setter, production licences and scientist category and role state. Licence registration, extension and removal have distinct ownership paths. Project lifecycle and scientist assignment write the category map. The character roster family forwards scientist role changes.

Sharing reads country and group membership, group bonus, faction sharing base and each sharing country's partner levels and classifiers. Leadership changes reorder faction membership, with the current leader first. Technology writers select only partners whose researched instance or predecessor classifier can change. Assignment checks old and new technologies. Rebuild and fresh initialization invalidate all members of the affected roster and faction.

## Other country inputs

Focus and project progress define their owning country. Appearance observes tag, color, graphics, name and subject rebuilding and receives forwarded country and diplomacy resets, ruling-party, leader and role changes. Counter inputs receive modifier recalculation, war and diplomacy, division and leader rosters, neighbours, industrial policy, state and core changes, and character pool lifetimes. State ownership and control changes name old and new countries. Core changes name the core country. Subject rebuilds can change both the subject and overlord, so read those identities before and after it.

Country visibility retains only presence and the raw combat-intelligence accumulator value. Country modifier recalculation and appearance reset cover its runtime mutations, while load and construction invalidate the epoch. Read definitions and eligibility every hour. The country-input sites execute on the game thread and use the owning publication and relocation checks.

## Resolve the mutation owner before publishing the certificate

`Tables` identifies which family a site can invalidate, including both stability and research, technologies, focus, intel-network, project, appearance, counter, visibility and weather data. `Receiver::own` narrows technology observations to their own country for the technology rows. Research modifiers still depend on the countries that share its levels and classifiers. Modifier recalculation reaches the stability and research families together with counter inputs and visibility. The air statistic writer reaches stability only through the interception column. An unrelated column needs no country read. Weather and the console's intel-network writer use call counts rather than a country's row identity.

`countries` resolves the owner through the layout of the receiver. A country supplies its own id. Manpower supplies its owner tag, and relation status follows diplomacy to its country. A war relation names both raw endpoints. A character pool and political status name their owners, a party names its country tag, and a scientist follows its character. Project status, project, progress and licence receivers name the owning country of their specific table. A research slot and technology instance follow their technology status. An unauthenticated receiver or incomplete required identity leaves the caller to mark every country. A native raw link follows country links to the census slot and uses the object's id, preserving aliasing. A signed gate treats a zero or negative old or new country, controller, subject or core link as absent. A positive link that fails to resolve refuses the narrow witness. A subject rebuild can name a new overlord on return even if it named a country at entry, so both observations are needed.

`sharing` visits the resolved members of every group on the technology status's sharing roster. It also visits the current faction's member array because a faction-mode group on a different member's roster can depend on this country. `sharing_affected` narrows a level or classifier change to partners reading the changed instance and its direct predecessors. Propagation can reach dependents and technology listeners transitively, including listeners for prerequisites or exclusions that are not dependents. Missing fields, an unsupported listener, a pending listener container, allocation failure or a traversal larger than the country roster abandons narrowing and marks every partner. An idle partner slot reads nothing. An unreadable slot does not prove idle. Assignment considers the prior instance and the new one.

`slot_roster` observes a synchronization's pointer, capacity and count. They change when the slot roster is replaced. `unit_leader_counts` compares both native roster counts around removal. The writer only erases in place and decrements a count on success, so an unchanged pair establishes no removal. `faction_sharing_base` compares the raw base and member pointer. Daily base refresh marks members only when that witness changes. The phase ordering ensures it observes any earlier pruning of supportive scientists.

## Match the native schedule of periodic inputs

The focus and special-project daily passes run in the hour entered at 23. The collector takes the corresponding full slice after the daily pass. At other hours it observes explicit reset, selection, copy, finish, reward and modifier updates. The politics family forwards the focus-selection and cancellation hooks it already owns. A new focus tree, a political reset or country reuse can invalidate completed-focus rows as well as active progress. Project progress can change outside the daily pass through effects, raid and capture results, or supportive scientists. Database reload and initializer paths replace the appropriate definition or country roots.

The technology progress adder also runs as daily slot drift. Direct technology-setting and stolen-bonus effects can add progress on their own, so `EFFECT_PROGRESS_SOURCE` marks those calls. Assignment, the GUI and console paths reach other observed writers. The weather reader follows the native hourly window only while the count of other writer calls stays unchanged. The count covers initialization, resets, explicit snow, mud and region changes, load, console steps, and province modifier addition, removal and expiry. The expiry rebuild hook observes the individual call made after removal, while other weather hooks observe their entries. Crew and sent expeditionary writers also reach country counters outside the contiguous counter writer range.
