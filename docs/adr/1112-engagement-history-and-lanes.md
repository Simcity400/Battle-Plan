# Fold engagement at each recorded hour and keep the client's viewpoint

A seek needs historical native refreshes, so each timeline folds engagement and icon frames through its pieces in order. Lanes decode only the participating tables and columns, preserving the original row format of old recordings and defaulting unselected columns before typed decoding. Their block-page ranges can skip hours that cannot alter a selected row or table status. The air roster history uses its own block-local lanes and keeps the samples around the daily tick. Input identity avoids rebuilding unchanged viewer operands.

An index is shared by recording, timeline pieces, branch and viewing country, closing when the last holder goes away. Each physical block is folded once per piece even if several branches reference it. A native load is a load signal at its recorded load hour. A session join, unreadable gap or lost load origin cannot establish a previous side's flag, so coverage remains unknown until a witnessed refresh. Do not equate unreadable hours with an hour that contains no combat.

Naval task-force joins and convoy transfers can refresh a side. From format 146 onward, `CombatEngagementRefreshes` records sparse hourly witnesses for side-level unit additions, unit removals, naval transfer additions (each with its execution viewing country), controller-reassignment targets, and local all-combat refreshes (with each local sweep's execution viewing country) in both full and map capture modes, and participates alongside `CountryCounterInputs` and `ReservedCountryNaming` in the lane viewer invalidation set so cached viewer inputs refresh whenever witness rows or zero-view shared-vision operands change. When witnesses are complete, same-roster re-additions, remove-and-readd bounces, mid-hour convoy transfers, same-player or bounced controller reassignments, and one-sided land round retreats are all observed directly; when witnesses are unavailable or unrecorded (formats before 146), the fold falls back to roster, convoy-slot, and sampled viewpoint-change observations while refusing to claim a stale held flag across a diplomacy change. On complete witnesses, controller targets and side refreshes drive hypothetical perspectives under the chosen perspective viewer, while directly observed local sweeps and side refreshes (evaluated under their recorded execution viewpoints, including directed reserved-country shared vision when the execution viewpoint is zero) drive the recording client track without inferring an all-side sweep from a complete empty interval.

The recording client's viewpoint is resolved from `LocalPlayer` and `CountryLinks`, using the observer reference when the playing reference is absent and preserving recording-track continuity across known unselected-observer hours (`LocalPlayer` with both references zero) without serving a country slot for those hours. It cannot be inferred from multiplayer roster membership. Serve the client's carried flag and icon frame when the viewer selects its current viewpoint. A directly witnessed tag switch refreshes immediately. An observer view change without an immediate controller refresh carries the previous viewpoint's flag for that sample. A guessed controller refresh remains ambiguous.

Recorded naval engagement bytes settle undecided flags for that client only. A definite derivation remains independently comparable to the recorded byte, and the summary counts every mismatch while retaining a bounded prefix of examples. The diagnostic reports missing bytes, incomplete census, unidentified viewpoint and unreadable hours separately. Naval icon admission needs primary countries on both sides. Convoy variants count current forward-group members, withholding records with an invalid side, and the frame advances only while the combat is engaged. Unknown or missing icon inputs do not become a valid frame on behalf of other viewers.
