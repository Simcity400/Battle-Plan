# 0203. Share infrequent intel storage and publish owned acquisitions

`CountryIntel` usually walks once per day. One frame-pool buffer serves those walks. The frame holds its loan through spool conversion and returns it when conversion, capacity growth or disposal finishes. A due walk that finds the loan occupied carries the previous sample and asks for another walk as soon as the buffer returns. This avoids blocking capture and allocating the daily population's dense owner-by-target data in every frame. Scratch capacity bounds each guarded copy, so a longer country vector is copied in successive windows. Optional reserve pressure can guide later growth while leaving a complete read's outcome intact.

The Intel residual job respects its per-table capture selection and maintains separate ownership for a loaned ScriptVariables job and the staged relations and capitulation acquisitions. Before loaning the pack, dispatch takes the relations context, owner selection and fingerprint store from its real buffers. The placeholder intentionally has no allocation or selection to lend. The join returns the selection and fingerprint store before views are published. A failed or truncated relations job leaves its rows unavailable with a named reason and makes its selection eligible for the next hour. A final worker failure preserves any other independently acquired table. An accepted failed acquisition does not retry live memory during handoff.

Relations jobs borrow one shared opinion baseline from dispatch through release on the decode worker. Borrowing uses a try-lock, and an occupied or uncertified baseline falls back to a full typed walk. The opinion-write cursor and epoch ensure a carried status still describes the current world. A load, gap or recovery invalidates the whole held baseline. Each drained mark joins its owner to this hour's scheduled slice. The baseline resolves known diplomacy owners, and a live backlink can resolve a newly seen one. An incomplete drain or unresolved owner makes the next walk cover every owner. An incomplete country census keeps held owners live. Growth follows observed native demand and supports a daily sweep writing every held opinion.

The capitulation payload records just the spans the direct walk needs, in the original census and per-country order. The same typed reader on the decode worker reads those owned copies, including war-goal recipients, so the direct path and replayed acquisition retain the same semantic checks, failure precedence, truncation and row order. The mirror stops at the first abort that the typed reader would reach and refuses staging failures by name. Publications keep the canonical country lookup and refuse unreadable records. Vector ordinals too wide for their recording keys result in a named refusal and avoid colliding with another row.

A network summary worker shares its cache across recycled frames. The daily step changes network values. Off-cadence reset and growth witnesses trigger acquisition when observed, while a lost certificate falls back to the guarded native walk. Reuse of an object's address and pair after a lifetime reset cannot prove its payload current. Epoch invalidation marks the cache without starting work on the game thread. A failed membership cut stays unavailable until its next required census. Resizing and conversion happen off the game thread, and conversion uses owned rows and arenas. Refusal rows and permanently absent native rows carry their own outcomes and create no extra storage demand. A row whose arena span lies outside the successful prefix or has the wrong part kind causes a named refusal.

The variable and mission walks keep their script-name lookup keys across hours. When a key is lent to another walk, the caller can use a fresh one without waiting. Retaining a country's unchanged mission-roster allocation lets conversion compare row identity by pointer. A roster that is no longer published is released.
