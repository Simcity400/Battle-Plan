# Schedule extended jobs around their actual source dependencies

The first burst sends the independent Ordinary parts while Countries owns appearance naming. The separate counter part starts after Countries restores that naming, including its reserved visibility observation. Land and naval counter branches keep their own sources. The counter part comes back before the navy part's reserved launch sources are installed in the restored appearance pack. The physical navy pass authenticates the navies, lends them to its mission ledgers and gets them back in the same Ordinary part. Its sliced mission store never travels to another job. Ordinary keeps only the country half of game score inputs. An Ordinary part failure refuses the whole family as its former single-job failure did. It cannot publish a held counter census or leak a stale scalar handoff. The map's typed air fallback uses the original sources before Ordinary joins.

The template dictionary, stability, war overview, raid state, building resources, trade routes, raid launch and target status jobs run alongside Slow, using the table-specific schedules and owner stores. Research borrows its hourly family before Governance itself leaves, then restores after Governance so independent failures do not erase that pack. Focus delegation is decided before the appropriate worker-only family leaves. Variant acquisition drains design changes before its roster decision. RaidAirScores and rail acquisition need only game state and the hour, so the first burst can run them independently. Install them after the map sample and after AirMissions returns the air pack. A failed job refuses both results, requests every score pair again, and forgets the rail store. AirSupply acquires into its spare without borrowing the AirMissions pack.

The production slot dispatches MIO before every modifier range and restores it into range zero at the late join. Split the modifier work from the last complete slot's starts, add each returned range's cost and derive the next split from the completed result. Off the slot the packs skip without walking or allocating. A missing job or failed Slow retains the established refusal and contributes no cost, with the off-slot result still skipped. Templates, production and state flag reports each contribute elapsed costs once.

Map and division visitation seed the later typed work. ArmyIcons uses installed division sources and restores its icon results before Late. Late's order transport reaches division presentation after both jobs join. Raw-gated work only leaves after all raw verdicts arrive. Projects carries its own owner store, while character inputs borrow the same-hour roster's role census without waiting for raw-copy verdicts. Release that census before returning it to its origin. AirMissions holds the histories and shares an owned census with no Ordinary borrow. A failed request withholds registrations and combats, leaves history regions unavailable, and drops held registrations. Certify history only after every part joins. If the attempt cannot certify the hour, withdraw its certificates before the next boundary.

Supply work loans node-census parts while part zero owns train data, selection and the store. The character roster and other split families similarly collect each part's timing and treat accepted interruption as terminal for the family. Ship models first walk a census. Only a completed census lends detail parts, sized from actual ship count without allocations. Join part zero first and restore detail outcomes in census order. A completed ship-model failure remains authoritative until reset or a new first census starts an epoch. Its scrubbed seeds cannot be read again. FinalInputs borrows the last sources after their owners join and leaves variants independent. ScriptVariables is held separately until the last Intel borrower returns. A failed worker refuses its table by name and forgets its store.
