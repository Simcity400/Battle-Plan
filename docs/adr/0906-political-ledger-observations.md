# 0906. Distinguish political mutations, reconciliation and daily inputs

Use performed mutations as the evidence for spending. Both currencies share the observation sequence. The absent requested amount remains unknown. A clamp gets its own reconciliation entry against the observed balance. Completeness is certified per currency and independently of whether an interval contains events. Retain the observed midnight update with its original epoch and day. Fixed5 quantities are signed throughout. Computing the difference in `i128` preserves the result across both endpoints of the incoming signed `i64` balances.

Income components describe one daily net change and do not add mutation rows. Their ordinals preserve repeated relation and industrial organisation sources. A political transition keeps the old ideology independently of old party membership. Its cause comes from actual execution provenance. Election dates carry no cause. Raw country links, character handles and lexical tokens join their owning recorded tables, without reducing reference widths.

Keep fixed5 and Q15 conversions shared between the playback formula and the save oracle. The native gameplay conversion includes saturating and truncating operations. Save formatting of Q15 uses truncation toward zero, which is a separate conversion.
