# 1202. Date faction membership by recorded identity and coverage

Faction timelines use recorded faction and native country identities. Preserve one lane across changes of a holder's tag and create a separate lane if a different holder takes that tag. Resolve names and flags at the supplying hour. A gap neither ends a membership nor proves the roster empty. The timeline can bridge the lifetime across a gap while leaving the membership counts and names unavailable within it. A dissolved faction keeps the name it ended with, but a gap takes no name from another hour. Exclude a delayed membership from country filtering until its first recorded hour. Structured lane identifiers keep modded names and tags from colliding.

Prepare structure at change hours such as lifecycle boundaries, membership and leader changes, and gaps. The playback cursor can move between these without rendering a new structure. Calculate sort keys once for the lanes being compared. Flags load for mounted lanes, while name sorting may need all compared names. A drill closes when its source, branch or faction changes, and never reuses a reply for an hour after the faction dissolved. Count plots leave gaps in both line and fill. Opening a drill or tooltip keeps its supplying identity and date so playing or tag reuse cannot silently change the subject.

The faction product separates current goals from its undated completion and cancellation lists. Only a held goal can supply a live progress cell. A saturated native cache marks a goal without measurable progress, and missing progress must not paint as a completed share. Rewards read on demand at the shown hour. Translate native text controls into visible labels, and omit empty reward scope headings. In the growth chart, country markers use their first known membership. Upgrade lanes prepare a new structure at each start or after each end. A member lane is named at its final recorded selection.

Only the faction's recorded and offered pools appear. A missing amount is unavailable rather than an empty pool. Keep repeated native manpower contributions as distinct rows rather than merging them by tag. Looking into one of them fixes the member at the clicked hour and follows its proved slot and country identifier through time. Amounts and progress paint from live cells, and resource order updates with those cells without changing React structure.
