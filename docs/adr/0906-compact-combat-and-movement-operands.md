# 0906. Record independent combat and movement operands

Format 102 stops persisting unused previews, oracle-only values and facts other recorded inputs determine exactly. Retired layouts retain old framing and tables are withheld by name when their contracts are retired. Supply operands are owned by `DivisionStatus`. Moving divisions retain their stat block's base speed for arrival estimates. The current camouflage field keeps the selected category, while older layouts resolve it from their recorded list and maximum. Format 102 packs stepped display fields in units of the display step. Storage widths stay intact and earlier recordings still decode exact unscaled data.

CAS and commander-side hover operands occupy a separate field in format 102 so an unrelated side change does not repack them. That field holds the anti-air cache, prevented CAS damage, skill progress, offence, defence, description, timed traits and shown learning traits at the precision displayed. Missing leader and learning data stay unrecorded. An older side row can still supply its two retained CAS operands. Other obsolete hover operands stay retired. The survival marker needs only the sum of 24 organisation-loss samples. The compressed side layout marks that sum with an inverted committed count. The decoder sums historical samples when the count shows the old layout. A reader without the new decoder refuses a marked payload. It does not mistake it for the old samples.

The reinforcement projection retains initiative and records only the reserve's computed speed share. Older recordings without the share leave the reserve chance unknown. Printed country and contextual commander reinforcement trees have their own table. Combat member modifiers use a row per member, so a changed member updates one row. Side-level border values remain readable through the retired member-list framing. Each division's fuel, capacity, stats and scales have independent numeric columns so one changed value can delta independently. Earlier formats still decode those values from their retained packets.

Attack playback derives the list from division stats and acclimatization, the direct commander's operands, country night and bunker modifiers, the fortified province's controller-state bunker factor, and raw regional combat-weather entries. Format 101 still answers with its witnessed value. Speed and attrition use the division stat block, commander, province and region weather, supply and fuel, country modifiers, regional air superiority and defines. Retain the whole province weather accumulator because other consumers also need its keys. Format 104 adds combat movement, noncore air flags and sub-unit speed keys, plus state, country, relation, supply-access and transport terms needed by the path. Missing tables and fields are withheld by name.

The supply footer uses stepped display amounts. For divisions, encode received supply as a wrapping shortfall from asked, and received origin amounts as shortfalls from their requested counterparts. In a supplied division those shortfalls stay still while requested totals drift. The origins tag distinguishes old direct amounts and new shortfalls at the same width. Task force and railway gun footers use the same display steps, so one formatter can serve all units. Division grace keeps the whole percentage the game shows without changing its wire step. `CountryFuel` stores the current day's applied fuel total. Historical per-update windows are discarded and cannot certify the displayed total. Its demand lists split into rows per part so one changed rate updates only one row. The current header's acquisition is authenticated by the row hour. Older layouts retain the complete pack with its separate hour.

Regional air combat retains each touched holder and its damage with their owning records. Playback derives daily pruning. The retired daily history and damage tables lack the parent link and remain withheld. The game-derived route graph records each controller area's representative and ordered contacts on its smallest-member label row, with controller and owner labels on every province. The collector rereads areas a native control change may have touched. Route playback also needs commander retreat bans at the frame's hour. Earlier, the reader could only use a daily ban at its owner's sampled hour. Railway gun sea legs require their owner's regional transport speed. Format 127 records exact base stats, accumulators and individual combat modifier values.
