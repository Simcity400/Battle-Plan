# 1302. Use recorded operands to build each stock map mode

Modes share the two political-fill channels but choose different layer identities. Political and navy use strategic regions below countries. Air uses strategic regions alone. Factions, players and ideology replace the country variant. Resources and raids use countries alone, with their separate overlay passes. States, resistance, compliance and operatives put their own layer above countries. Diplomacy uses its own layer alone. Infrastructure, terrain and manpower put their layer below countries. A refused layer leaves its channel empty and reports its cause. Supply is refused because its per-province sources and hourly node amounts are missing. Regional air-supply summaries also lack exact recording inputs.

Use the viewing country for the selected country. A country variant uses its subject-resolved recorded colour unless its mode supplies another. Factions take faction styles and naming from recorded members and the content's name rules. Player names take the first recorded roster ordinal when several players hold a country, and countries with no player carry no name. The selected country can pulse and widen its fill in the players mode even when its colour is faded. Ideology uses the ruling party's recorded group when present. Names may also span the connected provinces of a state or strategic region according to the mode's far and near naming policies.

Diplomacy tests relations in order. A war goal identifies both its recipient and a subject's overlord. If a missing goal input is reached below the war and faction tests, the country cannot safely take an opinion colour. State categories with an explicitly absent colour use the state fallback. An unreadable category colour withholds that region. Per-mode visibility is separate from fill, and local selection does not change another view's regional styles. The raid creation window's source stripes have no replay counterpart.

Air colours use land and sea regions. Their signed fixed-point ratios keep the inclusive contested band and refuse overflow. Zero weights fall back to active-mission counters once table coverage proves the object's region or absence. A skipped lazy read retains its own earlier cursor. Navy uses sea regions and preserves control and gaining-side effects. Air and navy range, assignment and hold emphasis belong to each view's local selections.

Resistance and compliance match controlled states, including occupied ones, to recorded resistance rows. Missing rows stay unfilled. The fold retains the core-table refusal order of the direct oracle. Colour lists interpolate adjacent rows, including alpha, and take the first value for nearly equal thresholds. Manpower and infrastructure blend their content colours over their recorded ranges, with infrastructure using the same hourly maximum as its tooltip. Operatives use the selected country's network only where its target controls the state. Clamp the fixed-point strength to a share and blend by that share times the larger of itself and one half. Quiet networks use a separate pair. All colour math uses the source precision, and packing truncates each component before keeping its low byte.

Terrain is content-static and maps each province to its own region. Land uses its definition category. Sea and lakes can use their strategic region's naval category or the ocean fallback. A missing or refused prerequisite withholds the affected region by reason. The terrain pass keeps its distinct land and water alpha and channel constants.
