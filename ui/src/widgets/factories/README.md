# Factories

Factory counts per country (spec 4.2 Factories; look: mock-ups style-1 and style-2, style A · Clean). One widget, three views (a setting):

- **Race** (default): horizontal bars ranked by the count at the moment shown, re-ranked every hour. Flag and name left of each bar, the exact count at its tip, hairline gridlines labelled under the bars. Rows glide to their new place; a country entering the top comes in from below and fades in, one leaving slides away and fades out. A bar glides between hours only within one unbroken stretch of its record: across a recording gap, or when another country takes the tag, it jumps. **Split bars by type** (off by default) draws one segment per factory type in the type's colour instead of one bar in the country's map colour, with a legend of the types. Every bar is exactly as long as its count. In **Built**, which counts damaged factories, the damaged part is hatched inside the end of the bar (spec); Free after withheld and Top bar have already taken the damaged out, so their bars have no hatched part and the tooltip names the damaged. A negative count (damaged and given away beyond the total) is drawn left of the baseline and labelled as recorded; with split bars each type is its own signed segment, so military −2 and civilian 10 cover −2 to 10 and are labelled 8, the same total as the unsplit bar. **Date in the corner** (off by default, as for every race) draws the moment shown large and faint behind the bars.
- **Lines over time:** one line per country, with names and counts at the line ends (or a legend when they are off), the usual zoom, playback modes, axis settings and keyboard hover over every change of a shown count. With top N, each country's line is drawn over the hours it was among the top N, as a group counted at each hour draws its members.
- **One country by type:** the country's types stacked over time (a negative count stacks below zero, never drawn as zero), with a legend holding each type's count at the moment shown. The country is the first chosen one, the dashboard's country, or for a group (top N included) the member ranked first at the moment shown, drawn over its member hours only; when no member was recorded then, the widget says so rather than show a country outside the group.

**Countries:** by default the top 10 of all countries, re-ranked every hour; N (up to 30) is set in the Countries section. Chosen countries and the other smart groups work too (a group counted at each hour shows a country only while it is a member). A country not recorded at the hour is left out of the race, never shown as zero. The widget asks only for what it draws: the chosen countries, or the top N, which the product ranks over every country at every hour a value changes and cuts to the hours each was among them.

**Tooltip** (hover a row, a line or the stack, or reach a row with the arrow keys; read out): the game's own breakdown for each type, as its construction tab and top bar list it: Total, Owned, From occupation, From subjects, From other countries, Trade exports, License exports, Damaged, Consumer Goods, Trade imports, National Projects, License imports, Market Contracts, Given to Overlord, Given to other countries, then the count shown. Additions keep their sign; deductions are shown as what they do to the count (a recorded damaged total of −1 reads +1). A window too short for it drops the lines that are zero, then shows the counts alone, and still reads the whole breakdown out (`fitTooltip`).

Countries in the lines' and the mix's tooltips are named and flagged as recorded at the hour hovered (`useCountryLabelsAt`, only the tooltip's countries asked for), so an earlier stretch is never labelled with a later name or another country's; the race reads names, flags and map colours at one grid sample inside each country's current identity. Pending parts keep their previous look within that identity; completed missing parts show the fallback. Lines use the same samples at each line's end label hour, capped at the playhead with Live on, including countries outside the top N then. The lines and the mix are cut to the hours shown (a date range, or the branch), so nothing outside a range is drawn, fitted or hovered, even where the time axis is dragged past it. When the top N asked for does not fit the widget's height, the race shows as many as fit and says how many more there are.

**Keyboard:** with the race focused, the arrow keys, Home and End step through the rows in rank order; Escape leaves; the context-menu key opens Jump to. The time views step through every hour a shown count changes.

**Right-click** a row or a point: Jump to that moment.

**Settings:** view; count; factory types (chips of the campaign's own types; all by default); split bars; date in the corner; names at line ends; for the time views the playback mode, window, Show after the playhead and the value axis.

## Counts

**Product `country-factories`** (`crates/dashboard/src/products/country_factories.rs`) replies recorded `CountryFactories` rows (table 257, read hourly) over the branch, as runs of whole factories, for what the request names: up to 30 chosen countries, or the top N (up to 30) by a count of some types. For top N it ranks every country at every hour a value changes, with the same formulas as the widget (tested in both), and replies each country ever among them with the hours it was (`membership`) and its runs cut to them, plus its unfiltered identity segments from Countries and CountryLinks, so at most N countries' runs cover any hour; a request naming neither replies the types alone, for the settings list, from content without scanning the history. Requests on one recording share one scan of a branch: widgets asking at once wait for the first one's scan rather than each scanning (as the service's other products serialise on their caches). The reply's size is counted from the scan, before any of it is copied, and refused beyond 24 MiB (the transport holds 32 MiB); 30 countries with 2,500 changes each of five-digit values fit in about 20 MB. For each of the game's three factory groups (military, naval, civilian) it carries the construction tab's and top bar's parts: total T, controlled C, owned O and damaged D (the engine's fixed5 totals truncated toward zero, `(int)(x / 100000)`, as every game view shows them), from subjects, given to the overlord, from other countries, given to other countries; and for the civilian group its trade and licence exports and what each consumer took (consumer goods, market contracts, national projects, licence imports, trade imports). The counts are formulas over these (user decision, 29 Sep; `factoriesData.ts`):

| Count | Civilian | Military, naval |
|---|---|---|
| **Free after withheld** (default) | T − D − consumer goods − trade imports − national projects − licence imports − market contracts − given to the overlord − given to other countries | T − D − given to the overlord − given to other countries |
| **Built** | T, the game's `Total` | T |
| **Top bar** | T − D − given to the overlord − given to other countries | the same |

**Evidence** (capture PR #142; the RE notes are in `crates/engine/src/factory_group.rs`, `civ_factory_consumer.rs` and `crates/replay/README.md`): `CCountryConstructionsView::BuildCivilianFactoryTooltip@1416fe6c0` shows Total trunc(total), Owned, From occupation trunc(controlled) − trunc(owned), Damaged and each withheld line; `CTopBar::BuildFactoryStatusTooltip@14187f760` shows per group T − D − to_overlord − to_others; `RecalculateFactoryTotals@140e57f10` adds to_overlord, to_others and, for the civilian group, each consumer's allocation (in the order consumer goods, contracts, projects, licences, trade) to the group's `unavailable`, so free after withheld is T − D − unavailable. The product checks that sum against the recorded `unavailable` and warns (in diagnostics) about any hour where they differ; the counts use the parts, as decided. The save writes none of these totals (a load recalculates them), so the fresh-save oracle proves the building levels behind them, not the totals.

**Territory** is the game's own: buildings count in the states a country controls (occupied land included, its own land held by an enemy left out) and its offsite buildings.

**Types** come from the content: a building counts in a group through its `military_production`, `naval_production` or `general_production` flag (`installed_content::buildings::FactoryFlags`), and each group is named by the game's names of the content buildings carrying its flag ("Civilian Factory", or several joined when a mod adds more). Without the game folders, or when no building carries a flag, the group is named by the flag. A type declared twice with different flags names no group.

**Built from the kit:** [CountryRaceView](../../charts/race/CountryRaceView.tsx) owns the race, country lines, type mix, legends, tooltip fitting and reset, names and colours at the visible line ends, and Jump to. [countryRace.ts](../../charts/race/countryRace.ts) owns content-order colours, signed bar parts and damage hatching. Factories selects shape easing within an unbroken stretch, the existing curves, half-pixel mix thinning, sampled race-tooltip names, its tooltip row retention, line-end room and strong mix-country name through the shared options. It supplies the chosen type labels and current counts; colours still use the complete reply's group order. Only what the factory values mean (the counts, damage, tooltip wording, identity segments and reply check) lives in this folder. The product finds countries with `metadata::tagged_countries` (every tag's `tagged_country` in one pass) and `link_slot`, and carries a read with `slot_read`.

## Mod compatibility

- **Factory types come from the content stack.** Every building type any layer declares with `military_production`, `naval_production` or `general_production` counts in that group and names it, so a mod's own factory types appear on their own and a vanilla type a mod removes vanishes (`describe_groups`, tested on a layered modded fixture: a renamed, an added, an unnamed and a removed type). A type without a name shows its token, a group no building counts in shows its flag, and building files the reader refuses (an unsupported modifier block) are said in a warning and leave the groups named by their flags.
- **The three groups are the engine's**, not content: `CProductionStatus` +0x2b0, +0x310 and +0x370 and their template tokens (reader `1414a9220`), cited beside the code. A mod cannot add a fourth; its types count inside these, which is how the game itself counts them.
- **Colours:** types take the categorical palette in group order, never by a name; countries take their recorded map colours.
- **Tags are opaque:** any bytes the engine accepts (spaces, lower case, dynamic `D01`), passed as strings, keyed as JSON, never split or joined on a delimiter, and ranked in the product's byte order. A tag that is not UTF-8 cannot be sent as JSON; the product leaves it out and counts it in a warning.
- **Not from content:** the tooltip's line labels (Total, Owned, Damaged, Consumer Goods…) are the app's own English text, like every widget's labels. The game's tooltip keys (`PRODUCTION_CIVILIAN_FACTORIES_*`, `TOPBAR_FACTORY_STATUS_*`) embed their numbers in format placeholders whose place varies by language, so reading the labels from them is left for a change of its own.

## Not yet

- **Verified on a real recording:** the widget is built and tested on synthetic replies; the browser pass on a format-83 recording with #142's final layout is still to do.
- **Modded factory types** that carry no engine flag cannot be told apart: the engine counts buildings only in these three groups, so a mod's own factory type shows inside whichever group its flags choose, and its name joins that group's.
- **Rocket sites** (the engine's fourth group) are not recorded.
