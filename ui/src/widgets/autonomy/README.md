# Autonomy

Each subject's autonomy over time (mock-up diplo-1).

- **Lanes:** one per subject, its flag and name (as recorded near the moment shown, at a recorded hour of its own stretch and never across a gap, `useCountryLooks`; each part on its own, so a flag that cannot be read leaves the name) on the left with its current level as a coloured pill underneath. A subject that is not a subject at the moment shown (released, not yet a subject, or not recorded) is faded. When the tables are complete and fully identified, its pill says "Not a subject here" and its accessible description says "Not a subject shown here", as the tooltip does. Missing tables say "Not recorded"; incomplete identities or group membership stay unknown.
- **Level bands:** the lane's background is banded by subject level, each level's colour held for the whole span it applied. Levels are named by the game's localisation of the recorded autonomy state, else by its recorded id; their order comes from the content's autonomy states, and their colours are the categorical palette in that order, never tied to a level's name.
- **Thresholds and progress:** the lane's dashed top edge is the score at which the subject moves up a level and its bottom edge the score below which it moves down; the line is the subject's score between them. A score past either edge (a subject at the top level can pass its upper bound: South Africa at 5,125 of 5,000 in part M) is drawn at that edge; the tooltip gives it exactly.
- **Level changes:** a dot where the level changed from one recorded hour to the next. A change across a recording gap is not marked, as when it happened is unknown.
- **Playhead** through every lane while following playback; lanes stop at it.
- **Hover:** the subject and its overlord named as recorded at the hour hovered (`useHoverLabels`); the level, the overlord, the progress between the thresholds, the autonomy score and both thresholds; "no lower level" or "no higher level" when the subject's state has none (the recorded `previous` or `next` is empty). At an hour with a subject or overlord the recording does not name, a lane without a value says "Not known", and a subject without an overlord says its overlord is not known, never that it had none.
- **Keyboard:** Up and Down choose a subject, Left and Right step through its level spans and every recorded change inside one (score, thresholds or overlord: the span's `stops`); the tooltip shows and is read out. The context-menu key opens Jump to.
- **Right-click** jumps to that moment.
- **Countries:** the countries chosen are **overlords** (default: every subject each of them had in the branch gets a lane) or **subjects** (a setting). Smart groups work: a faction's members as overlords lists all their subjects. With membership counted at each hour, a subject shows only while it (as a chosen subject) or its overlord belongs, and hours whose membership was not recorded are left out as unknown (only then: a group counted at one moment chose its countries at that moment, and its unrecorded hours elsewhere hide none of their history); when that leaves no lane where a subject was recorded in such hours, the widget says the group's membership was not recorded then, never that a country had no subjects. A subject's runs are cut by each overlord's membership in one pass per overlord, so a membership of tens of thousands of spans cuts at once. At most 64 lanes are drawn; the diagnostics count the rest.
- **Gaps:** an hour without the tables is not recorded: the line, bands and thresholds break there. A branch recorded without them at every hour says "Not recorded", never that a country had no subjects; one recorded only in part says a country had none "in the hours this campaign branch recorded".
- **Countries the recording does not name:** an hour whose Autonomy or Subjects rows name a subject or overlord that `CountryLinks` and `Countries` do not is recorded but only partly known (`unidentified`). The diagnostics count those hours, and with no lane the widget says the chosen countries had no subjects *the recording names*, and may have had one, never that they had none.

## Data

**Product `autonomy`** (`crates/dashboard/src/products/autonomy.rs`) supplies each subject's runs of recorded autonomy for a branch, with the levels in level order. Empty overlord and subject selections return the whole branch; the branch is scanned once and cached. The widget joins subjects by `Lineage`, splits runs at subject and overlord tag changes, and compares `worldFilter`'s country identities locally. A rename continues the country's history and a reused tag names its new holder. Hourly groups use `countryMembership` to clip those histories. It also lists the hours recorded with both tables (`recorded`) and, among them, those with a row whose subject or overlord the recording does not name (`unidentified`), with a warning counting them.

- **`Autonomy`** is the subject's own `CCurrentAutonomyStatus`: its score, the scores to move down and up (`lower_score`, `upper_score`), its state and the states below and above it. Scores are fixed5: the fresh-save oracle matches the save's `progress=4312.86618` with a recorded 431,286,618 (`crates/recorder/src/oracle/intel.rs`). In part M the bounds are the level's and the next level's `min_freedom_level` times 5,000 (puppet 2,000–3,000, colony 3,000–4,000, dominion 4,000–5,000).
- **`Subjects`** names the overlord. Its `autonomy_ratio` (fixed5, the save's `value`) is carried in the reply as recorded, but its role in the game is not established (in part M it is 0.2 for Australia while its progress is 0.37), so the widget does not show it.
- **Level order:** `installed_content::autonomy` reads every `common/autonomous_states` file through the recorded content stack and orders the states by `min_freedom_level`, lowest first. A mod's states order the same way; a state the content does not define, or whose freedom level is not a plain number (a scripted value or an `@variable`, refused and named in a warning), follows the others by its lowest recorded move-down score. Without the game folders, every level is ordered that way and named by its id.

**Built from the kit:** the virtualised lane timeline (`charts/timeline/Timeline.tsx`) with its taller rows (`rowHeight`), a painted lane (`paint`, using full-timeline `LaneGeometry` through the translated drawing context), a pill in the left column (`pill`) and span `stops`, and its playhead, zoom, keyboard and Jump to; `clipToSpans` for group membership; `curveSegments` for the progress line, `SECONDARY_DASH` for the thresholds, the categorical palette for the levels, `useCountryLooks` and `useHoverLabels` for names and flags. Only what autonomy means (level spans, progress, changes and the rows) lives in this folder.
