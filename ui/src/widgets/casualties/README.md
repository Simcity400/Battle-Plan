# Casualties

Each country's casualties dealt and taken (mock-up style-6, the Casualties card), or a race of losses taken (mock-up style-1, Style A, Clean).

- **Dealt vs taken:** one butterfly row per country, name and flag in the centre. Taken is on the left, faded; dealt is on the right, full, in the same recorded country colour. Both halves share a scale fitted to the largest value shown. Exact values sit at the bar ends, and dealt / taken appears in the right-hand ratio column; zero or unknown taken has no ratio.
- **Race of losses taken:** the shared `CountryRaceView` draws the same selected countries, ordered by known taken with opaque-tag ties. Names and flags sit left of the bars, exact totals (including “≥” lower bounds) at their tips and hairline grid labels underneath; rows re-sort smoothly. Top-N membership remains dealt + known taken in both views, so changing the view requests no new casualty reply. The unknown-loss option changes only the displayed total, not either ranking. A total that cannot be recovered exactly has no bar and its country is named as unknown beneath the race. Gaps draw no values. Race labels, tooltips and Jump to use each row's read hour.
- **Motion:** rows glide to their new rank and bar widths ease, while numbers stay exact. Missing values draw a dash and no bar. A return after a recording gap starts a new stretch without easing across it. Reduced motion follows the shared chart setting.
- **Countries:** top ten by dealt + known taken by default; any user-selected top N, manual picks and recorded smart groups (human players, factions, at war with a country), with their hour/end/ever membership rules. The definition uses `max: Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` and does not offer a new All choice; previously saved All selections remain compatible. No relations means no row, rather than a zero total. A zero recorded counter remains a real zero.
- **Time:** Live off shows cumulative totals at the campaign end. Live on follows playback, growing from each new daily sample. A pinned moment or date range shows the totals at its last hour; these are cumulative counters, not losses incurred within the selected window.
- **Tooltip:** exact dealt, taken with an attacker, losses with no recorded attacker, whether they are included, total taken and ratio. It says these are the game's war-relation totals **across land, sea and air**, and states the exact sample hour directly from the sample date: “As of …; casualties are sampled once a day.” A country unknown total that cannot be recovered says so, in either setting. A lower-bound total shows “≥” and no ratio, with the explanation “At least: a war ended while the recording was not running, so its last losses are missing.” The shared tooltip fits the window and keeps its full content available to assistive technology.
- **Keyboard:** focus the chart; arrow keys step through the countries, Home/End choose the first/last, Escape clears the hover. The tooltip is read out. The context-menu key or Shift+F10 opens Jump to at that row's read hour.
- **Right-click:** Jump to the sample read shown, through the shared clock.
- **Settings:** Views (Dealt vs taken by default, or Race of losses taken), then include losses with no recorded attacker (off), butterfly values at bar ends (on), butterfly ratio column (on). Unknown losses add only to taken. When they cannot be recovered, enabling them makes taken unknown; known dealt and the separately named known taken remain available in the tooltip.
- **Empty:** one muted line, “No casualties yet.”

## Data

**Product `war-casualties`** ([war_casualties.rs](../../../../crates/dashboard/src/products/war_casualties.rs)) answers chosen tags, all sampled countries, or any user-selected top N by dealt + known taken over every recorded country. Unknown losses are display-only: the setting never changes the ranking or requests another casualty reply. Every country ever among the top N is returned with its membership and samples cut to it, so at most N series cover an hour. The two most recently used complete branch scans share sampled country totals across selections. Each typed columnar series, including identities and campaign labels, is counted before adding it to the bounded reply. A cache of packed signed deltas keeps equal values as one run; ranking windows and their full country order are computed once per branch. Names resolve only for requested tags, from snapshots of changed naming inputs collected during that same scan. Smart groups request every recorded member and apply hour/end/ever membership locally. All requests every sampled country. Complete reply bytes are measured against the shared transport reply budget; exceeding it refuses the request without truncation.

**Looks:** names, flags and colours use the shared `useCountryLooks` at each row's exact read hour, held while the next reply loads within the same identity and readable stretch. An answered missing or failed name, colour or flag stays missing. Colours are keyed by tag over the request's whole country list, so changing rank never changes a colour. Identities join through CountryLinks; tags remain opaque, including mod tags with spaces or delimiters. The product resolves every name shown over the campaign through recorded content. The butterfly kit measures those names once per request, with flag room, keeping one label column throughout playback. The column has a maximum share of the width; longer names use an ellipsis while their tooltip keeps the full name. Few rows keep the same capped spacing as a fuller chart. Bar-end numbers stay exact.

**Resolution:** the scan reads and deduplicates relation counters at every recorded WarRelations read. It emits country totals at day ends, at the first read of each readable recording stretch, at the last readable hour before every gap or unreadable hour (including identity boundaries), and at the branch’s last readable hour. Each point retains its exact read hour and holds forwards until the next point, never across a gap. The final campaign sample includes the last readable partial day. Top membership uses these same samples. The columnar reply stores the first read and subsequent hour deltas, with endpoint exceptions. Hold endpoints are separate from confirming reads; clipping membership never creates a read. Values are decimal-string bases and safe integer deltas; exceptional wide deltas remain decimal strings. The client accumulates with BigInt. Equal consecutive confirmations merge into one run, whose read date is the latest day-end at the displayed hour within that stretch. Spans clip membership locally. Separate histories for successive holders of a tag keep uncertainty and exact unknown totals attached to their country identity. The frontend resolves samples locally. Nothing is interpolated.

**Defaults:** new and reset widgets start with the top ten; saved selections persist. Country selections and top N have no chosen upper limit. A previously saved All selection requests every sampled country in the branch, including later-created countries. The complete serialized reply is refused if it exceeds the shared transport reply budget; no countries or history are truncated.

**Known losses are the victim's losses.** `CWarRelation::AddCasualties@140d04b50` adds to the first or second country's known-loss field according to the victim. Therefore taken is the sum of the country's own counters; dealt is the sum of opponents' counters in those same relations. Callers include land manpower (`140c5b350`), division destruction (`140c58750`, from `140c79e70`), `CTaskForce::SinkShip@140d637c0`, `CAirWing::KillAirplanes@140f4e400` and railway-gun damage. Calling these land-only casualties would be wrong. The authoritative fields and cadence stay in [engine](../../../../crates/engine/src/cwar_relation.rs), [rows](../../../../crates/replay/src/rows.rs) and [cadence](../../../../crates/replay/src/cadence.rs).

**No recorded attacker is not an attrition category.** `AddUnknownCasualties@140d051d0` has one caller, `140c5b350`. When its attacking-country argument is absent, a manpower loss is copied to every war relation of the victim. The manpower chain is `140c7fd50 → 140c5b330 → 140c5b350`; its callers are active damage (`140c7bfc0`) and the disruption helper (`140c7ae10`, from `ApplyParadropDisruption@140c7b090`). Active damage is called by combat settlement (`140c7b370`, which initially sets the attacker to zero and then tries to identify it), immediate damage (`140c80020`) and naval-transfer damage (`140e138f0`). These paths pass an attacking-country argument; whether a particular loss is unknown depends on that argument. Ordinary attrition (`140c5fe80 → 140c5e9c0`) subtracts equipment quantities and never enters this manpower chain. The recording cannot name a more specific cause for an unknown loss.

**The game's war display:** `GetUnknownCasualties@140d0fc40` feeds `141e64090`, called by `14189a620` and the war-view update `141e66d90`: each country's known counters against the opposing side are summed, its maximum unknown counter is added once, and the countries are then summed for the side. The diplomacy tooltip builders `140d19cf0` and `140d1a550` use the same sum-plus-maximum, list opposing countries and put the unknown quantity in a separate `CASUALTIES_UNKNOWN` entry, beside `CASUALTIES_START` and `CASUALTIES_ENTRY`. These are native localisation tokens at those addresses; the tooltip’s unknown-loss row resolves `CASUALTIES_UNKNOWN` with the catalogue’s localiser through the recorded content stack, with the key visibly shown if it is missing or refused. The settings label remains app wording: “Include losses with no recorded attacker”.

**Exact unknown recovery:** never sum or take a maximum of the relation counters as a lifetime total. For the observed history, recovery starts only with an all-zero baseline. At each consecutive read, at least one relation must span both reads; every spanning relation must have the same nonnegative increment, and a newly seen relation must not contain an unexplained larger counter. Add that increment once, regardless of the number of relations. A nonzero initial baseline, a recording gap, inconsistent counters, a reset or no spanning relation when relations reappear leaves the country total unavailable thereafter. Even newly seen all-zero relations cover only their own lifetimes: losses after the old relations disappeared and before the new ones started cannot be ruled out, so without a spanning relation the country total remains unknown. This deliberately refuses histories it cannot prove. Per-pair counters stay inside this scan; future Casualties between countries and Casualties breakdown products will request their own pairs. Losses before the recorded baseline, and losses while no relation exists, cannot be reconstructed.

**Ended wars and gaps:** an ended row is still read while present. A disappeared row keeps its last values inside the scan; neither drops to zero. Successive relations between the same identities have separate start-hour keys. Samples hold only over complete recorded hours. Missing tables, unresolved endpoints and missing hours leave a gap, including when values return unchanged. A changed tag holder never inherits the former country's counters. A relation present before a gap and absent at the next readable hour may have ended with losses nobody recorded: both countries’ dealt and known taken become permanent lower bounds. A relation already absent before the gap is unaffected. Even without a recording gap, the last tick’s casualties are inherently lost if a war ends between two hourly reads, because the collector walks active wars only. Every branch with a gap shows a muted note under the chart and the same tooltip explanation: “Wars fought entirely while the recording was not running are not counted.” All totals are over relations this branch has actually observed; a relation already gone before the recording starts cannot be recovered.

**Built from the kit:** [butterfly](../../charts/butterfly/) and [CountryRaceView](../../charts/race/CountryRaceView.tsx) own layout, Canvas drawing, row motion, bar easing, the shared scheduler, sharp sizing, theme tokens, flags, fitted tooltip and context menu. This folder owns only the payload check, exact integer arithmetic, country selection and casualty wording. The shared interval index finds only current top-N members during playback, regardless of how many countries entered the ranking over the campaign. The product streams requested packed histories and uses the existing CountryLinks and slot-read helpers. Flags use the shared `canvasImage(url, loaded)` cache.

## Not yet

- **Cause breakdown or land-only totals:** war-relation counters do not record a cause or distinguish land, sea and air.
- **Unrecoverable unknown baselines:** the optional total remains unknown rather than treating the game's maximum counter as an exact historical total.
- Real-recording interaction checks and the complete React/Canvas frame budget remain unmeasured. The actual widget was compared side by side with style-6 in headless browsers using synthetic data; that visual check does not establish rendering performance.
