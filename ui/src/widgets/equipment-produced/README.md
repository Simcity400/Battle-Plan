# Equipment produced

How much equipment each country has produced (spec 4.2 Equipment produced; look: mock-ups style-1 and style-2, style A · Clean), built from the race kit exactly as [Factories](../factories/README.md) is. One widget, three views (a setting):

- **Race** (default): horizontal bars ranked by the value at the moment shown, re-ranked every hour. Flag and name left of each bar, the exact number of units at its tip, hairline gridlines under the bars. Rows glide to their new place; a country entering the top comes in from below and fades in, one leaving slides away and fades out. A bar glides only within one unbroken stretch of its record: across a recording gap, or when another country takes the tag, it jumps. **Split bars by group** (off by default) draws one segment per type, variant or arm in its colour, with a legend; the segments always add up to the number at the tip. **Date in the corner** (off by default, as for every race) draws the moment shown large and faint behind the bars.
- **Lines over time:** one line per country, with names and values at the line ends (or a legend when they are off), the usual zoom, playback modes, axis settings and keyboard hover over every change of a shown value. Each line's name and colour use its own last drawn hour in the visible range, capped at the playhead with Live on, including a line ending before the playhead in a gap or outside the top N. With top N, each country's line is drawn over the hours it was among the top N; a group counted at each hour draws its members over their member hours.
- **One country by equipment:** the country's groups stacked over time, with a legend holding each group's value at the moment shown. The country is the first chosen one, the dashboard's country, or for a group (top N included) the member ranked first at the moment shown (the kit's `mixCountry`), drawn over its member hours only.

**Measure (setting):** **running total** (default): every unit delivered since the recording began counting (the tooltip says since when); **per week**: units delivered in the seven days before the moment; **per month**: units delivered since the same day and hour of the month before (the month's last day when it is shorter). A week or month is counted between two reads of the same country with the recording unbroken in between (no gap, no hour the tables could not tell, no restart of the count), so the first week or month of a recording, and the week or month after a gap or a change of the country under a tag, is unknown and not drawn; the race says why it is empty then.

**Grouping (setting):** by equipment type (default: the content's archetypes, so a mod's types appear by themselves), by exact variant (named as each read recorded it, never by a later name), or by land, air and naval. **Included types (setting):** the types counted, chosen from every type produced in the recording (content's names); none counts them all. Ranking, bars, lines and the mix count only the included types. **Count refitted ships (setting, off):** a refit line rebuilds a ship the country already had as another design, so its deliveries count only when asked.

**Colours:** countries draw in their recorded map colours (the race kit's `useCountryColours`, nudged apart in the app's fixed-colour mode). Groups take the categorical palette in the reply's order; with more groups than colours, the largest keep theirs and the rest share "Other" (charts rule: a ninth category folds into Other). By exact variant, where every country makes its own designs, each country folds its own: its bar and its mix keep its own largest variants in colour (in the game's order) and the rest are Other (user decision, 29 Sep); the race then shows a note instead of a legend, and hovering names them. The tooltip always lists every group on its own.

**Countries:** by default the top 10 of all countries, re-ranked every hour; N is set in the Countries section. Chosen countries and the other smart groups work too. A country whose value is unknown at the hour is left out of the race, never shown as zero. The top N show a country only once it has produced something (user decision, 29 Sep): a zero is not ranked. The product also reports the hours at least one considered country has an exact total and no considered country with a piece covering the hour has an unknown total, before ranking (all scanned countries for top N, only the chosen tags otherwise). A country absent at that hour does not block these hours. When no country ranks then, the race says no country produced anything in the preceding week or month; outside those hours, including before a complete window, it says the value is unknown. This also works when nobody ever ranks in the branch. The widget asks only for what it draws: the chosen countries, or the top N, which the product ranks over every country at every hour a value changes (by the included types and the measure shown) and cuts to the hours each was among them.

**Tooltip** (hover a row, a line or the stack, or reach a row with the arrow keys; read out): the country's name as recorded at the hovered hour (line tooltips also show its flag then); each group it made, most first, then the total; a value that is not known says "Not known" and is listed last. The note says since when the running total counts and, between reads, when the value was read. A window too short for every group shows the coloured groups and Other alone (`fitTooltip`), keeping the total and reading the full breakdown out.

**Keyboard:** with the race focused, the arrow keys, Home and End step through the rows in rank order; Escape leaves; the context-menu key opens Jump to. The time views step through every hour a shown value changes.

**Right-click** a row or a point: Jump to that moment.

**Settings:** view; measure; group by; included types (up to 16 chosen; a chip says when the limit is reached); count refitted ships; split bars; date in the corner; names at line ends; for the time views the playback mode, window, Show after the playhead and the value axis.

**States:** loading; "Not in this recording" for a recording without the per-line output (with the collector's output hooks unavailable); per week or per month on a branch shorter than a week or month, a note that no value exists yet (never "no production"); the product's warnings in the diagnostics.

## Data

**Product `equipment-produced`** (`crates/dashboard/src/products/equipment_produced.rs`) totals `ProductionLineOutput`, the collector's exact count of every production tick per line (the game keeps none per line): each row is one (line, producer, variant, organisation) and holds the units delivered since the branch's observation began, with the producer read at the tick. The product keeps every row's last count for the rest of the branch, so a line that was deleted, or made and deleted between two reads, still counts for the country that made it. A count the collector could not make exactly (a tick left out) or that disappeared without its final publication makes that country's total of the variant unknown from then on, never a partial sum. An ended line's final count is published only once, so a frame that could hold a publication but cannot be ingested (an unreadable block or frame, a read without complete countries, links or output, or units of a producer the links cannot tell) makes every total unknown from then on, until the collector's count restarts; totals never silently resume. The output is read once a day with the production lines and carried between reads; values end at a gap, an unreadable table or another country under the tag.

Types come from the recorded content stack through `products/equipment.rs` (shared with Equipment stockpile): a variant's recorded token resolved to its authored type's archetype (`equipment::archetype_of`), in the equipment database's order, its arm by the engine's plane and ship classifiers, its name through the content's localisation. A type content does not resolve is kept apart under its recorded token and counted in a warning, never merged into another. As in Equipment stockpile, such a variant could belong to any type (and, when counted, to any arm), so while it holds a nonzero or unknown value every resolved type's (or arm's) value is unknown (user decision, 29 Sep); its own group stays exact. The country's total does not depend on the split, so it stays exact and the bar keeps its length, the part the known groups do not account for hatched; it is unknown only when chosen included types could hold a left-out unresolved variant. A line's kind (for refits) is its recorded `ProductionLines.kind`; a line no census listed counts as new production (warned).

The reply is compact: each country's samples (the hours each value holds) and, per group, its values coded as differences from the first non-zero one, with the samples where it is unknown as ranges (known values after them survive), and the exact total the same way. The widget keeps them coded and reads a value at a sample from the running sums of its differences, so what it holds is bounded by the reply, never by samples × groups (tested with 30 countries × 3,650 samples × 1,000 variants). Its size is counted before it is built and refused beyond 24 MiB.

**Built from the kit:** [CountryRaceView](../../charts/race/CountryRaceView.tsx) owns the race, country lines, one-country stack, country looks and colours, hover-label requests, fitted tooltips, legends, end labels and Jump to. Its presentation options preserve this widget's amount glide within a stretch, curved lines and bands, half-pixel sample thinning, identities and colours at each visible line end, hovered-hour tooltip names, bold mix name, existing label room and keyboard stops. Entries carry each country's group colours and the unknown-attribution hatch; the mix supplies its own groups and their exact legend values. Custom labels, empty-state wording and the measure/grouping reset key also stay the same. The shared race settings and `mixCountry` choose the view and country. This folder keeps production decoding, ranking inputs, group colours, tooltip rows and the variant-colour note; equipment names still come from `dashboard/equipment.ts`, and `readAt`/`readNote` identify the reads. The product ranks through `crates/dashboard/src/ranking.rs`, shared with Factories.

## Not yet

- **Verified on a real recording** only over a short branch: a historical recording of 24–29 Oct 1943 (5.5 days, fresh-save oracle clean) matches an independent sum of its `ProductionLineOutput` rows for every country shown; per week and per month need a longer recording. The save keeps no per-line count, so the totals cannot be checked against the save itself. Older recordings open under the [per-table compatibility rule](../../../../crates/replay/README.md#compatibility-and-recovery); incompatible production tables become named gaps.
- **Equipment art** is not shown: a type's row in the game is drawn with the country's best variant (not recorded), and a variant's own icon needs the production-lines icon resolver (`equipment_icon`, on the Production lines branch).
