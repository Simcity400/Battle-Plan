# Subjects

An overlord tree over time, on the lane timeline ([charts/timeline](../../charts/timeline/)), the approved style-4 timeline look: each overlord is a heading lane whose band grows thicker with its number of subjects, and each of its subjects has a lane beneath it, indented, with a bar from when it became a subject to when it stopped. The bar is coloured by the subject's level (its autonomy state), changing colour where the level changed, with the level's name inside where it fits. A country that is both a subject and an overlord appears in both places; a subject taken by another overlord appears under each.

Levels, their names and their order come from the campaign's content (`common/autonomous_states`, through the recorded content stack): least free first by each state's `min_freedom_level`, then load order. Colours are the categorical palette in that order, never keyed on a level's name, so a mod's levels get colours of their own; a level past the palette shares the "Other levels" colour, and its name is still shown. A level the content does not define shows by its id, after the others.

With Live on, lanes appear as playback reaches them and bars grow to the playhead; with Live off the whole campaign shows. The left column gives each subject's level and the day it became a subject, as the game dates it (the relation's own start, so a subject held before recording began has its real date), or "Ended", "Ended by" (ended during an unrecorded stretch) or "Last recorded". Unrecorded hours stay gaps in the bars and the overlord's band. One relation (the same start hour) continued across unrecorded hours is not said to have ended; a relation that stopped and started again with another start hour is a new one. A change of tag of a subject or its overlord (`country-groups`' `identityChanges`) keeps one lane and one overlord, named by the tags at the moment shown. A run is split when its tag names another country, including two countries swapping tags. Hovering an unrecorded hour says "Not recorded".

Hovering an overlord's band lists its subjects and their levels at that hour, every country named and flagged as recorded at that hour (`useHoverLabels`); hovering a subject shows its overlord, level, the day it became a subject and when it ended. Right-click jumps there. The keyboard steps through lanes, bars and each level change, and the timeline describes every overlord and subject at the moment shown for screen readers.

**Time:** the whole campaign by default (Live off), as every history widget; Live follows playback.

**Countries:** all countries by default; chosen countries or a group (Countries settings) narrow the widget to overlords that are, or held, one of them. A group counts its members under its membership setting: at the moment shown, at the branch's end, or anyone ever. Members are compared as countries, each resolved at the hour its membership counts, including every holder of a reused tag during its runs.

**Settings:** overlord order (first subject, most subjects at the moment shown, name), subject order (date became subject, name), former subjects shown or hidden, level names in bars, and the level legend.

**Data:** the `subjects` product ([subjects.rs](../../../../crates/dashboard/src/products/subjects.rs)): each overlord–subject relation as hourly runs, with the engine's start hour and the subject's level through each run, the levels named and ordered by content, and the hours not recorded. The level is the subject's current autonomy state (`Autonomy.state`), not `Subjects.state`, which is the relation's initial level and does not follow later changes. Countries are named and flagged as the recording showed them at the moment shown (`useCountriesAt`), names for every lane, since every order compares names (as the order or to break ties), in chunks of at most 64 with at most two reads at once, and flags for the lanes in view and an eight-lane margin.
