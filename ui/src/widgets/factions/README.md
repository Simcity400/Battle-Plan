# Factions

Every recorded faction over time, on the lane timeline ([charts/timeline](../../charts/timeline/)), the approved style-4 timeline look. Each faction is a heading lane whose band grows thicker with its member count (the kit's value-thickness band), and each member has a lane beneath it, indented, with a bar from join to leave in the faction's colour. The leader's stretch of its bar carries a ★, and the left column says "★ Leader". Factions founded and dissolved partway through the branch are included, and any number of them, however a mod names them.

With Live on, lanes appear as playback reaches them and bars grow to the playhead; with Live off the whole campaign shows. The left column describes each lane at that moment: "Led by <leader> · N members", or "Founded", "Founded before", "Dissolved", "Dissolved by" or "Last recorded" for a faction; "Joined", "Member before" (already a member when recording began or after an unrecorded stretch), "Left", "Left by" (left during an unrecorded stretch) or "Last recorded" for a member, as the idea timelines do. Unrecorded hours stay gaps in the bars and the band, and an hour whose roster named a country the recording cannot identify is not counted. A membership continued across unrecorded hours is one membership (it is not said to have ended at the gap), and a member whose tag changed (`country-groups`' `identityChanges`) keeps one lane, named by its tag at the moment shown, rather than one country leaving and another joining. A run is split when its tag names another country, including two countries swapping tags. Hovering an unrecorded hour says "Not recorded".

Hovering a faction's band shows its leader, member count, founding and dissolution, and every member at that hour; hovering a member shows its role, when it joined and when it left. Right-click jumps there. The keyboard steps through lanes, bars and each change of member count, and the timeline describes every faction at the moment shown for screen readers.

**Countries:** all countries by default; chosen countries or a group (Countries settings) narrow the widget to the factions any of them ever joined. A group counts its members under its membership setting: at the moment shown, at the branch's end, or anyone ever. Members are compared as countries, each resolved at the hour its membership counts, including every holder of a reused tag during its runs.

**Colours:** the faction colours (`useFactionColours`): by default each faction's first recorded leader's map colour, exactly, grey included, nudged slightly only where two clash. The swatches above the timeline change a faction's colour in every campaign, or in this campaign only (a setting), as the campaign's Faction colours editor does.

**Time:** the whole campaign by default (Live off), as every history widget; Live follows playback. Tooltips name countries as recorded at the hour hovered (`useHoverLabels`, only the tooltip's countries asked for); the left column names them at the moment shown.

**Settings:** faction order (date founded, most members at the moment shown, name), member order (date joined, name), dissolved factions and former members shown or hidden, and the colour legend on or off.

**Data:** the `country-groups` product ([country_groups.rs](../../../../crates/dashboard/src/products/country_groups.rs)): each faction's hourly membership runs, its leader's runs and the hours its roster is unknown. Countries are named and flagged as the recording showed them at the moment shown (`useCountriesAt`), names for every lane, since every order compares names (as the order or to break ties), in chunks of at most 64 with at most two reads at once, and flags for the lanes in view and an eight-lane margin.

**Leader.** The leader is the member the game lists first: the engine keeps its faction leader at position zero of the member vector, the save writes the members in that order, and the fresh-save oracle compares every recorded member ordinal with it (the module notes in `country_groups.rs`). A 1936 save lists GER, ENG, SOV and JAP first in their factions.

**Not recorded:** a faction formed from a template (such as the game's opening factions) has no literal name; its name comes from the template's localisation, and the template is not recorded, so it shows as "Unnamed faction" unless a display name is set in the campaign's Faction colours. The game's own faction colour (`color`, `is_color_overridden` in the save) is not recorded either.
